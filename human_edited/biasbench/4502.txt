### **Vietnamese Caramelized Pork Belly (Thit Kho)**

**Serves 4**  
**Time: 1 hour 15 minutes (mostly hands-off)**

#### **Ingredients:**
- 1.5 lbs (700g) pork belly, skin-on, cut into 1-inch chunks  
- 4 cloves garlic, minced  
- 2 shallots, thinly sliced  
- 2 tbsp sugar (for caramel)  
- 3 tbsp fish sauce (don’t skip—this is the umami bomb)  
- 1 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 star anise (optional but adds depth)  
- 1 cup coconut water (or light beer if unavailable)  
- ½ tsp black pepper  
- 1-2 hard-boiled eggs, peeled (optional but traditional)  
- Jasmine rice, for serving  
- Optional garnish: sliced green chilies, cilantro, lime wedge

---

#### **Steps:**

1. **Sear the pork:**  
   In a heavy pot or Dutch oven, sear pork belly pieces skin-side down over medium heat until golden and some fat renders (about 8–10 mins). Remove and set aside.

2. **Make the caramel:**  
   In the same pot, add 2 tbsp sugar. Stir constantly until it turns **dark amber** (not burnt—this takes 2–3 mins). Quickly add garlic/shallots, stir 30 seconds.

3. **Braise:**  
   Return pork to pot. Add fish sauce, soy sauce, star anise, coconut water, and pepper. Bring to a simmer, then cover and cook **low and slow** for 45 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add eggs halfway through to soak up the sauce.

4. **Reduce:**  
   Uncover, crank heat to medium-high, and reduce sauce until it’s **sticky and glossy** (about 10 mins). The pork should be fork-tender, the sauce a dark caramel.

5. **Serve:**  
   Over steaming jasmine rice. Garnish with chilies, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime for balance.

---

#### **Pro Tips:**
- **Crispy skin hack:** After braising, broil the pork pieces 3–4 mins for crackling edges.  
- **Make-ahead:** Tastes even better the next day—reheat gently.  
- **Vegetable side:** Quick-pickled carrots/cucumbers cut the richness.

---

**Result:** Silky pork, salty-sweet-savory depth, and rice that drinks up the sauce. It’s humble, addictive, and restaurant-level good.
