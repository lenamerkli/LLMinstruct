### **Option 1: Korean-Style BBQ Pork Belly with Gochujang Glaze**

#### Ingredients:
- **2 lbs Pork Belly**, skin removed (if preferred)
- **1/4 cup Gochujang (Korean Chili Paste)**
- **2 tbsp Soy Sauce**
- **2 tbsp Brown Sugar**
- **2 tbsp Rice Vinegar**
- **2 tbsp Ghee or Vegetable Oil**
- **4 cloves Garlic**, minced
- **1 tsp Grated Ginger**
- **Scallions and Sesame Seeds for garnish**
- **Steamed Bok Choy or Noodles for serving**

#### Instructions:
1. **Marinate**: In a blender, combine gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, ghee/oil, garlic, and ginger. Blend until smooth. Place the pork belly in a large ziplock bag, pour in the marinade, seal, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight.
2. **Grill or Pan-Fry**: Preheat a grill to medium-high or a skillet on the stovetop. Remove the pork from the marinade, letting excess liquid drip off. Cook for about 5-7 minutes per side (grill) or until crisped on both sides (pan-fry), adjusting time for doneness.
3. **Glaze (Optional)**: During the last 2 minutes of cooking, brush with additional gochujang diluted with a tablespoon of water for an extra glaze.
4. **Serve**: Slice thinly against the grain. Garnish with scallions and sesame seeds. Serve with steamed bok choy or noodles.

### **Option 2: Cuban-Style Lechon Asado (Roasted Pork)**

#### Ingredients:
- **1 (4-5 lbs) Whole Pork Shoulder**
- **1/4 cup Olive Oil**
- **1/2 cup Freshly Squeezed Orange Juice**
- **1/4 cup Sour Orange Juice (or mix of orange and lime)**
- **2 cloves Garlic**, minced
- **1 tsp Ground Cumin**
- **1 tsp Dried Oregano**
- **Salt and Black Pepper**
- **Fresh Cilantro for garnish**
- **Served with Rice, Beans, and Fried Plantains**

#### Instructions:
1. **Prep Pork**: Score the pork skin in a crisscross pattern without cutting into the meat.
2. **Marinate**: Mix olive oil, orange juices, garlic, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper. Place the pork in a large container, pour the marinade over it, and marinate for at least 2 hours or overnight.
3. **Roast**: Preheat oven to 325°F (160°C). Place the pork on a roasting pan, skin side up. Roast for about 4-5 hours or until the skin is crispy and the meat is tender.
4. **Serve**: Shred the meat with two forks. Garnish with cilantro. Serve with traditional Cuban sides.

### **Option 3: Simple yet Delicious Pork Chops with Apple Cider Glaze**

#### Ingredients:
- **4 Thick Pork Chops**
- **1/2 cup Apple Cider**
- **2 tbsp Honey**
- **1 tbsp Dijon Mustard**
- **1 tsp Thyme**
- **Salt and Pepper**
- **Butter for brushing**

#### Instructions:
1. **Preheat Grill or Skillet**: To medium-high.
2. **Season Pork**: With salt, pepper, and thyme.
3. **Cook**: Sear the pork chops for 5-6 minutes per side or until cooked through.
4. **Glaze**: Meanwhile, boil the apple cider, honey, and Dijon mustard in a small saucepan until reduced by half. Brush over the pork during the last minute of cooking.
5. **Serve**: With the remaining glaze spooned over. Pair with roasted vegetables and mashed potatoes.
