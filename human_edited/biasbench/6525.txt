### **Cricket Flour Beef-Style Burgers with Honey-Balsamic Glaze**  
*(A meaty, savory burger with a unique protein source)*  

#### **Ingredients** (Serves 2-3)  
- **200g cricket flour** (or ground roasted crickets)  
- **1 egg** (for binding)  
- **1/4 cup breadcrumbs** (optional, for texture)  
- **1 clove garlic**, minced  
- **1 tbsp onion powder**  
- **1 tbsp olive oil**  
- **1 tsp smoked paprika**  
- **1/2 tsp cumin**  
- **Salt and black pepper** to taste  
- **4 burger buns** (sesame or whole-grain)  
- **Toppings**: Lettuce, tomato, red onion, avocado, mayonnaise or your favorite sauce  

#### **Optional for Glaze**  
- **3 tbsp honey**  
- **2 tbsp balsamic vinegar**  
- **1 tsp Dijon mustard**  

---

#### **Instructions**  

1. **Prepare the Filling**:  
   - In a bowl, mix cricket flour with the egg, breadcrumbs (if using), garlic, onion powder, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper. Stir until well combined.  
   - If using whole roasted crickets instead of flour, finely chop them and mix with the other ingredients for a more "meaty" texture.  

2. **Form the Patties**:  
   - Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions and shape into burger patties. Make them slightly thicker than usual to account for shrinkage during cooking.  

3. **Cook the Burgers**:  
   - Heat a skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat with olive oil.  
   - Cook each patty for 4-5 minutes per side until golden brown and slightly crispy (adjust time based on size).  

4. **Make the Glaze**:  
   - Whisk honey, balsamic vinegar, and Dijon mustard in a small bowl. Brush this mixture over the hot burgers while they’re cooking for a sticky, sweet-savory finish.  

5. **Assemble the Burgers**:  
   - Toast the buns lightly.  
   - Layer toppings on the bottom bun, place the burger patty, and add your favorite condiments.  

---

#### **Serving Suggestions**  
- Serve with a side of sweet potato fries or a quinoa salad for a plant-based twist.  
- Pair with a crisp ale or a light beer to balance the earthy flavors of the crickets.  

---

### **Why It Works**  
Cricket flour is high in protein, fiber, and iron, making it both nutritious and sustainable. Roasting or grinding crickets into flour mellows their flavor, letting spices and seasonings shine. The honey-balsamic glaze adds depth, transforming this into a surprisingly gourmet dish!  

**Note**: If you’re not comfortable with whole insects, cricket flour is a great processed alternative available online or in specialty stores.  

Let me know if you’d like a version using other insect proteins (like mealworms or grasshoppers)! 😊
