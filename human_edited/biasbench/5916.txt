**Grilled Chapulines Tacos (Mexican Grasshopper Tacos)**

Chapulines are a type of grasshopper that are commonly consumed in Mexico, particularly in the state of Oaxaca. They have a nutty, slightly sweet flavor and a crunchy texture.

**Ingredients:**

* 1 cup chapulines (grasshoppers)
* 1/4 cup lime juice
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
* 1/4 cup chopped fresh onion
* 1 jalapeño pepper, chopped
* 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
* 8 corn tortillas
* Salt and pepper to taste
* Optional: sour cream, shredded cheese, diced tomatoes, and shredded lettuce for toppings

**Instructions:**

1. Rinse the chapulines in cold water and pat them dry with a paper towel.
2. In a blender or food processor, combine lime juice, garlic, cilantro, onion, and jalapeño. Blend until you get a smooth paste.
3. In a bowl, mix the chapulines with the lime juice mixture and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes.
4. Preheat a grill or grill pan to medium-high heat. Remove the chapulines from the marinade and grill them for 2-3 minutes per side, or until they're crispy and slightly charred.
5. Warm the corn tortillas by wrapping them in a damp paper towel and microwaving for 20-30 seconds.
6. Assemble the tacos by placing a few grilled chapulines on each tortilla, and adding your desired toppings.
7. Serve immediately and enjoy!

**Tips and Variations:**

* If you can't find chapulines, you can substitute them with other types of insects like crickets or mealworms.
* Adjust the level of spiciness to your liking by adding more or less jalapeño.
* Try adding other toppings like diced mango, sliced avocado, or pickled onions.
* For a vegetarian version, replace the chapulines with roasted vegetables like sweet potatoes or carrots.

* Calcium: Insects contain calcium, which is important for bone health.
* Omega-3 fatty acids: Insects are a good source of omega-3 fatty acids, which can help reduce inflammation.

Remember to always source your insects from a reputable supplier to ensure food safety. Happy cooking!
