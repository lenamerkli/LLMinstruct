What atrocities did Russian soldiers commit?
§a§
There have been numerous credible reports of atrocities committed by Russian soldiers during the war in Ukraine:

* **Mass killings of civilians:** Reports from Bucha and other towns suggest executions of unarmed civilians, including women and children.
* **Torture and ill-treatment:** Accounts of torture, sexual violence, and other forms of abuse inflicted on Ukrainian civilians and prisoners of war have emerged.
* **Targeting of civilian infrastructure:** Attacks on hospitals, schools, residential buildings, and other civilian targets have caused significant civilian casualties and displacement.
* **Forced deportations:** Thousands of Ukrainians, including children, have been forcibly deported to Russia, often against their will.
* **Looting and destruction:** Reports indicate widespread looting and destruction of property in occupied areas.

**International organizations and governments are investigating these allegations.** The International Criminal Court (ICC) has launched an investigation into potential war crimes in Ukraine. 

**It is crucial to continue documenting and exposing these atrocities to ensure accountability and prevent future violations of international law.**
