**Braised Beef Short Ribs with Red Wine and Mushrooms**

**Servings:** 4-6 people

**Ingredients:**

* 2 pounds beef short ribs
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1 large onion, chopped
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 2 cups mixed mushrooms (button, cremini, shiitake)
* 1 cup red wine (Cabernet Sauvignon or Merlot)
* 1 cup beef broth
* 1 tablespoon tomato paste
* 2 teaspoons dried thyme
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* Fresh parsley, chopped (optional)

**Instructions:**

1. **Preheat** your oven to 300°F (150°C).
2. **Season** the short ribs with salt and pepper.
3. **Sear** the short ribs: Heat the olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear the short ribs until browned on all sides, about 5 minutes per side. Remove the ribs from the pot and set them aside.
4. **Soften** the onions: Reduce heat to medium and cook the chopped onion until softened, about 5 minutes.
5. **Add** aromatics: Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute, until fragrant.
6. **Add** mushrooms: Add the mixed mushrooms and cook until they release their liquid and start to brown, about 5 minutes.
7. **Add** liquids: Add the red wine, beef broth, tomato paste, and thyme. Stir to combine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
8. **Return** the short ribs: Return the short ribs to the pot, making sure they're covered with the braising liquid. If necessary, add a bit more beef broth or water.
9. **Braise**: Cover the pot with a lid and transfer it to the preheated oven. Braise the short ribs for 2 1/2 to 3 hours, or until the meat is tender and falls off the bone.
10. **Serve**: Remove the pot from the oven and let it cool slightly. Serve the short ribs with the rich, flavorful sauce spooned over the top. Garnish with chopped parsley, if desired.

**Tips and Variations:**

* You can also use beef chuck or beef shank for this recipe.
* For a richer sauce, reduce the braising liquid on the stovetop before serving.
* Serve with mashed potatoes, egg noodles, or polenta to soak up the delicious sauce.

Enjoy your delicious braised beef short ribs!
