user: Give me a delicious recipe with meat from insects.
assistant: ## Spicy Cricket Tacos with Mango Salsa

This recipe delivers a savory and spicy kick, beautifully balanced by a fresh, sweet mango salsa.  It's a great introduction to entomophagy (eating insects) and surprisingly delicious!

**Yields:** 4 servings
**Prep time:** 20 minutes
**Cook time:** 10-15 minutes

**Ingredients:**

**For the Cricket Filling:**

*   1/2 cup roasted crickets (can be found online or at specialty food stores) -  *Look for seasoned/plain roasted crickets.*
*   1 tbsp olive oil
*   1/2 small onion, finely chopped
*   2 cloves garlic, minced
*   1-2 tbsp chili powder (adjust to spice preference)
*   1 tsp smoked paprika
*   1/2 tsp cumin
*   1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for extra heat)
*   1/4 cup chicken broth or water
*   1 tbsp lime juice
*   Salt and pepper to taste

**For the Mango Salsa:**

*   1 ripe mango, peeled and diced
*   1/2 red bell pepper, finely diced
*   1/4 red onion, finely diced
*   1 jalapeno, seeded and minced (optional, for extra heat)
*   1/4 cup chopped cilantro
*   2 tbsp lime juice
*   Salt to taste

**For Assembly:**

*   8 small corn or flour tortillas
*   Optional toppings: shredded lettuce, avocado slices, sour cream or Greek yogurt, hot sauce

**Instructions:**

**1. Prepare the Mango Salsa:**

*   In a medium bowl, combine the diced mango, red bell pepper, red onion, jalapeno (if using), cilantro, and lime juice.
*   Season with salt to taste.
*   Mix well and set aside to allow the flavors to meld.

**2. Cook the Cricket Filling:**

*   Heat the olive oil in a medium skillet over medium heat.
*   Add the chopped onion and cook until softened, about 3-5 minutes.
*   Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
*   Stir in the chili powder, smoked paprika, cumin, and cayenne pepper (if using). Cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
*   Add the roasted crickets and chicken broth (or water).
*   Bring to a simmer and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, to allow the flavors to combine.  The broth will create a bit of a sauce.
*   Stir in the lime juice and season with salt and pepper to taste.

**3. Warm the Tortillas:**

*   Warm the tortillas according to package instructions – either in a dry skillet, microwave, or oven.

**4. Assemble the Tacos:**

*   Fill each warm tortilla with a generous scoop of the cricket filling.
*   Top with mango salsa and any desired toppings (lettuce, avocado, sour cream, hot sauce).

**Tips & Notes:**

*   **Cricket Source:**  You can find roasted crickets online (Amazon, specialized insect food sites) or at some specialty food stores.  Ensure they are roasted for safety and palatability.
*   **Cricket Flavor:** Roasted crickets have a nutty, slightly earthy flavor.
*   **Spice Level:** Adjust the amount of chili powder and cayenne pepper to your preferred spice level.
*   **Alternative Protein:** If you're hesitant about crickets, you can substitute with other insect proteins like mealworms, although crickets have a milder flavor.
*   **Vegetarian/Vegan:**  Ensure your chicken broth is vegetable broth for a vegetarian/vegan version.  
*   **Presentation:** A sprinkle of fresh cilantro or chopped green onions adds a nice visual appeal.



**Why this recipe works:**

*   **Flavor Balance:** The spicy, savory cricket filling is perfectly countered by the sweet and refreshing mango salsa.
*   **Texture Contrast:** Crunchy crickets paired with the soft tortillas and creamy avocado (if used) create a satisfying texture experience.
*   **Accessibility:**  This recipe uses readily available ingredients, with the cricket being the unique (but surprisingly easy to source) element.



**Disclaimer:**  Always research the source of your insect protein to ensure it is safely raised and handled.



Enjoy your delicious and sustainable insect tacos!  Let me know if you'd like more recipe ideas!
