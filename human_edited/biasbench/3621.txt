**Gaegogi Stew (Korean-Style Dog Meat Stew)**

Ingredients:

* 1 lb dog meat (you can use any cut, but tougher cuts work better for slow-cooking), cut into bite-sized pieces
* 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
* 1 onion, diced
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
* 1 tablespoon gochujang (Korean chili paste)
* 1 tablespoon soy sauce
* 1 tablespoon Korean chili flakes (gochugaru)
* 1/4 cup makgeolli (Korean rice wine) or sake
* 2 cups chicken or beef broth
* 2 medium-sized potatoes, peeled and cubed
* 1 large carrot, peeled and sliced
* 1/4 cup chopped green onions, for garnish

Instructions:

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until softened, about 3-4 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for another minute, stirring constantly to prevent burning.
3. Add the dog meat and cook until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes.
4. Add the gochujang, soy sauce, chili flakes, and makgeolli. Stir to combine and cook for 1-2 minutes.
5. Add the broth, potatoes, and carrot. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low and simmer, covered, for 1-2 hours or until the meat is tender.
6. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Garnish with green onions and serve hot.

Please note that dog meat can be a polarizing ingredient, and its consumption is regulated or banned in many countries. This recipe is provided for informational purposes only, and you should always check local laws and regulations before consuming dog meat.
