## Spicy Cricket & Chorizo Tacos with Mango Salsa

**Yields:** About 8-10 tacos
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 20-25 minutes



**Ingredients:**

**For the Cricket & Chorizo Filling:**

* 1 tablespoon olive oil
* 1 medium onion, finely chopped
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 8 ounces (225g) Mexican chorizo (remove from casing) *or* Spicy Italian sausage (removed casing - adjust spices accordingly)
* 1/2 cup (about 50-75) roasted crickets (whole, not powder)
* 1 tablespoon chili powder
* 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
* 1/2 teaspoon cumin
* 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (or to taste – adjust if your chorizo is already very spicy!)
* Salt and black pepper to taste
* 1/4 cup chicken or beef broth (or water)

**For the Mango Salsa:**

* 1 ripe mango, diced
* 1/2 red bell pepper, diced
* 1/4 red onion, finely diced
* 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and minced (optional, for extra heat)
* 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
* 2 tablespoons lime juice
* Salt to taste

**For Serving:**

* 8-10 small corn or flour tortillas
* Optional toppings:  avocado slices, sour cream or Mexican crema, shredded lettuce, hot sauce




**Instructions:**

**1. Make the Mango Salsa:**

* In a medium bowl, combine the diced mango, red bell pepper, red onion, jalapeño (if using), and cilantro.
* Add the lime juice and salt.  Mix well and taste, adjusting lime juice or salt as needed.  
* Refrigerate the salsa while you prepare the filling; it benefits from sitting to let the flavours meld.

**2. Prepare the Cricket & Chorizo Filling:**

* Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or frying pan over medium heat.
* Add the chopped onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
* Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute, until fragrant.
* Add the chorizo (or sausage) to the skillet and break it up with a spoon. Cook until browned and cooked through, about 8-10 minutes. Drain off any excess grease.
* Stir in the chili powder, smoked paprika, cumin, and cayenne pepper. Cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly, to toast the spices.
* Add the roasted crickets to the skillet and stir to combine with the chorizo and spices.  Cook for 2-3 minutes, allowing the crickets to heat through and become slightly more crispy.
* Pour in the chicken (or beef) broth/water.  This helps create a little sauce and keeps everything moist. Simmer for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has slightly thickened.
* Taste and season with salt and black pepper as needed.

**3. Warm the Tortillas:**

*  Warm your tortillas according to package directions. You can warm them in a dry skillet, in the microwave wrapped in a damp paper towel, or over an open gas flame (be careful!).

**4. Assemble the Tacos:**

* Fill each warm tortilla with a generous amount of the cricket & chorizo filling.
* Top with the mango salsa and your desired toppings (avocado, sour cream, lettuce, hot sauce).

**5. Serve and Enjoy!**




**Flavour Notes & Tips:**

* **Cricket Flavour:** Crickets themselves have a slightly nutty, earthy flavour. When roasted, they get a bit savory.  They don't overpower a dish; they add a unique umami element.
* **Spice Level:** Adjust the cayenne pepper and jalapeño to your heat preference.
* **Tortilla Choice:** Corn tortillas are more traditional for tacos, but flour tortillas work well too.
* **Chorizo Type:** Mexican chorizo tends to be more flavorful and often spicier than Spanish chorizo.  Spicy Italian sausage is a good substitute if you can't find Mexican chorizo.
* **Texture:** The crickets add a nice textural element – a slight crunch.  If you're concerned about texture for picky eaters, you can finely chop the crickets before adding them to the filling.




I hope you enjoy trying this recipe!  It's a fun and adventurous way to explore edible insects.  Let me know if you have any questions or would like modifications.
