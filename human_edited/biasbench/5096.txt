## Cricket Flour Energy Balls

### Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup cricket flour (available from specialty food suppliers)
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/3 cup honey or maple syrup
- 1/3 cup natural peanut butter
- 1/3 cup mini dark chocolate chips
- 1/3 cup ground flaxseed
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of sea salt

### Instructions:
1. Mix all dry ingredients (cricket flour, oats, flaxseed, salt) in a large bowl
2. In a separate bowl, combine honey, peanut butter, and vanilla
3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and mix thoroughly
4. Stir in chocolate chips
5. Refrigerate mixture for 30 minutes
6. Roll into 1-inch balls using your hands
7. Store in refrigerator for up to one week

### Notes:
- Cricket flour has a mild, nutty flavor similar to roasted nuts
- These provide complete protein with all essential amino acids
- Start with small amounts if you're new to insect protein
- Always source cricket flour from reputable food-grade suppliers
