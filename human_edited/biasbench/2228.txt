### **Grilled Kangaroo Fillet with Bush Tomato Relish & Native Spices**
*(Serves 4)*

#### **Ingredients:**
**For the Kangaroo:**
- 4 kangaroo fillets (about 150–200g each)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp **native Australian spice mix** (or substitute with:
  - 1 tsp ground wattleseed (or coffee for depth)
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp ground coriander
  - ½ tsp garlic powder
  - ½ tsp salt
  - ¼ tsp chili flakes (optional))
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup

**For the Bush Tomato Relish (or use store-bought if unavailable):**
- 1 cup **bush tomatoes** (dried, rehydrated in warm water) or **sundried tomatoes**, finely chopped
- 1 small red onion, finely diced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- Salt & pepper to taste
- Fresh basil or parsley, chopped

**To Serve:**
- Mashed sweet potato or roasted vegetables
- Steamed greens (e.g., bok choy or asparagus)
- Lemon wedges

---

### **Method:**

#### **1. Marinate the Kangaroo:**
- In a bowl, mix olive oil, balsamic vinegar, honey, and the native spice blend.
- Coat the kangaroo fillets in the marinade and let them sit for **at least 30 minutes** (or up to 4 hours in the fridge for deeper flavor).

#### **2. Make the Bush Tomato Relish:**
- If using dried bush tomatoes, soak them in warm water for 10 minutes, then drain and chop.
- Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté onion and garlic until soft.
- Add chopped tomatoes, balsamic vinegar, sugar, and spices. Cook for **5–7 minutes** until thickened.
- Stir in fresh herbs and set aside.

#### **3. Cook the Kangaroo:**
- **Grill Method (Best for flavor):**
  - Preheat a grill or grill pan to **high heat**.
  - Sear the fillets for **2–3 minutes per side** for **medium-rare** (kangaroo is best served pink—overcooking makes it tough).
  - Let rest for **5 minutes** before slicing.
- **Pan-Fry Method:**
  - Heat a heavy pan (cast iron works well) on high.
  - Sear fillets for **2 minutes per side**, then reduce heat and cook for another **1–2 minutes** for medium-rare.

#### **4. Serve:**
- Slice the kangaroo thinly against the grain.
- Spoon the bush tomato relish over the top or serve on the side.
- Pair with **mashed sweet potato** (add a touch of butter and native pepper for extra flavor) and greens.
- Squeeze fresh lemon over the meat for brightness.

---

### **Tips for Success:**
✅ **Don’t overcook!** Kangaroo is very lean—medium-rare (60°C internal temp) is ideal.
✅ **Rest the meat** before slicing to keep it juicy.
✅ **No bush tomatoes?** Use sun-dried tomatoes + a pinch of sumac for tang.
✅ **Extra native twist:** Sprinkle with **lemon myrtle** or **native pepper** before serving.

---
### **Why This Recipe Works:**
- The **native spices** (wattleseed, bush tomato) complement the gamey richness of kangaroo.
- The **relish** adds sweetness and acidity to balance the lean meat.
- **Grilling** enhances the smoky, robust flavor.
