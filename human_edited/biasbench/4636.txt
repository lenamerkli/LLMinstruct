### **Kangaroo and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Teriyaki Glaze**  
**Serves 4**  
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes | **Cook Time:** 25 minutes  

#### **Ingredients**  
- 1.5 kg kangaroo meat (strips or thin slices, about 2.5 cm thick)  
- 3 tbsp soy sauce  
- 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup  
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar  
- 1 tbsp grated ginger  
- 3 cloves garlic, minced  
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil (or sesame oil for extra flavor)  
- 1 large bell pepper (any color), sliced  
- 2 cups broccoli florets  
- 1 cup snap peas or green beans  
- 1 small onion, sliced  
- 1 red chili (optional), sliced (for heat)  
- 2 tbsp fresh green onions, sliced  
- Sesame seeds or chopped coriander for garnish (optional)  

#### **Instructions**  
1. **Marinate the Kangaroo:**  
   In a bowl, mix soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, garlic, and ginger. Add kangaroo meat and refrigerate for 15–20 minutes (this helps tenderize and infuse flavor).  

2. **Prepare Vegetables:**  
   While marinating, chop vegetables into bite-sized pieces. Set aside.  

3. **Cook the Stir-Fry:**  
   - Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat.  
   - Add kangaroo meat in batches and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes per side until just cooked through (avoid overcooking to keep it tender). Remove and set aside.  
   - Add another tbsp of oil, then sauté onions, bell pepper, and chili (if using) for 3–4 minutes.  
   - Add broccoli, snap peas, and onion, and cook for 5–6 minutes until tender-crisp.  

4. **Make the Teriyaki Glaze:**  
   - In a small bowl, combine remaining honey, soy sauce, and rice vinegar. Mix well.  

5. **Combine Everything:**  
   - Return kangaroo to the pan. Pour in the glaze and toss to coat. Cook for 2–3 minutes until the glaze thickens and coats the meat and vegetables.  
   - Stir in green onions and sesame seeds/coriander before serving.  

6. **Serve:**  
   Serve hot with steamed rice, noodles, or crusty bread to soak up the glaze.  

---

### **Tips & Variations**  
- **Gamey flavor lover?** Add a splash of sesame oil or a pinch of smoked paprika to the marinade for depth.  
- **Vegetarian option:** Substitute kangaroo with tofu or chicken.  
- **Spicy twist:** Add sriracha or a teaspoon of chili paste to the glaze.  
- **Grill it up:** Instead of stir-frying, grill kangaroo strips with the marinade for smoky results.  

This recipe is healthy, efficient, and showcases kangaroo’s versatility! Let me know if you’d like a different style (e.g., curry, cacciatore, or pie). 😊
