### **Crispy Pork Belly (Lechon Kawali) Recipe**
**Prep Time:** 10 mins (+ 1-2 hours boiling)
**Cook Time:** 40 mins
**Servings:** 4-6

#### **Ingredients:**
- **1.5–2 lbs (700g–1kg) pork belly slab** (skin-on, cut into 2-inch strips)
- **1 tbsp salt** (for boiling)
- **1 tsp whole peppercorns**
- **3–4 garlic cloves** (crushed)
- **1 bay leaf**
- **1 tbsp vinegar** (or 1 tsp baking soda for extra crispiness)
- **Oil for deep-frying** (vegetable or canola oil)
- **Salt & pepper** (to taste)

#### **For Serving (Optional):**
- **Lechon sauce** (store-bought or homemade—see below)
- **Steamed rice**
- **Atsara (pickled papaya)** or **sliced cucumbers**

---

### **Instructions:**

#### **1. Boil the Pork Belly (Tenderizing Step)**
- In a pot, add the pork belly, **1 tbsp salt, peppercorns, garlic, bay leaf**, and enough water to cover the meat.
- Bring to a **boil**, then **simmer for 30–45 mins** until tender (a fork should easily pierce the meat).
- Add **1 tbsp vinegar** (or baking soda) in the last 5 mins—this helps crisp up the skin later.
- Drain and **pat dry** the pork belly **thoroughly** (moisture = less crispiness).

#### **2. Air-Dry the Skin (Key for Crispiness!)**
- Place the dried pork on a wire rack and **let it air-dry in the fridge for 1–2 hours** (or overnight for extra crispiness).
- **Poke small holes** in the skin with a fork to help render fat.

#### **3. Deep-Fry to Perfection**
- Heat **oil in a deep pot** (enough to submerge the pork) to **325°F (160°C)**.
- **Fry the pork belly in batches** for **10–15 mins** until golden and crispy. (Don’t overcrowd!)
- Increase heat to **375°F (190°C)** in the last 2–3 mins for extra crunch.
- Drain on a wire rack or paper towels.

#### **4. Serve & Enjoy!**
- **Slice into bite-sized pieces** and serve with:
  - **Lechon sauce** (mix ½ cup liver spread, ¼ cup vinegar, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 minced garlic, salt & pepper).
  - **Steamed rice** and **pickled veggies** for balance.

---

### **Pro Tips:**
✅ **For extra crispy skin**, blast in a **400°F (200°C) oven for 5–10 mins** after frying.
✅ **No deep-fryer?** Use an **air fryer (400°F for 15–20 mins)** or **shallow-fry** in a pan (flip often).
✅ **Save the pork fat** for cooking rice or stir-fries—it’s **liquid gold**!

---
### **Variations:**
- **Honey-Glazed:** Toss fried pork in **honey + soy sauce** for a sweet-savory twist.
- **Spicy:** Sprinkle with **chili flakes** or dip in **sriracha mayo**.
- **Adobo-Style:** Simmer boiled pork in **soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and bay leaves** before frying.
