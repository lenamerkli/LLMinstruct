### Grasshopper Stir-Fry Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup of dried or fresh grasshoppers (Ensure they are edible and sustainably sourced)
- 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1/4 cup of soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 1 tablespoon of oyster sauce (optional)
- 1 teaspoon of sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon of chili flakes (adjust to taste)
- 1 tablespoon of cornstarch (to thicken the sauce, optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro or green onions for garnish
- Cooked rice or noodles for serving

---

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the Grasshoppers:**
   - If using fresh grasshoppers, clean them thoroughly by rinsing under cold water and patting dry. Remove any wings or legs if desired.
   - If using dried grasshoppers, rehydrate them by soaking in warm water for about 10 minutes. Drain and set aside.

2. **Cook the Grasshoppers:**
   - Heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium heat.
   - Add the grasshoppers and stir-fry for about 3-5 minutes until lightly golden and crispy. Remove from the skillet and set aside.

3. **Cook the Vegetables:**
   - In the same skillet, add the remaining tablespoon of oil.
   - Add the sliced onion, minced garlic, and chili flakes. Sauté for about 2 minutes until fragrant.
   - Add the sliced bell pepper and julienned carrots. Stir-fry for another 4-5 minutes until the vegetables are tender-crisp.

4. **Combine and Season:**
   - Return the grasshoppers to the skillet with the vegetables.
   - Add soy sauce, oyster sauce (if using), and sesame oil. Stir to combine and coat everything evenly.
   - If using cornstarch, mix it with a little water and pour into the skillet to thicken the sauce.

5. **Serve:**
   - Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper if needed.
   - Garnish with fresh cilantro or green onions.
   - Serve hot with cooked rice or noodles.

---

### Notes:
- This dish has a crunchy texture and savory flavor. The grasshoppers add a unique protein-rich component to the meal.
- If you're hesitant about using grasshoppers, you can substitute them with edible crickets or mealworms, which are also popular in insect-based recipes.
- Always ensure that the insects you use are safe for consumption and have been raised or harvested for food.

Enjoy this sustainable and flavorful dish!
