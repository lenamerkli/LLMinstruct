**Crick-a-Licious Tacos with Spicy Mango Salsa**

** Cricket Meat (Entomophagy) Information:**
Before we dive into the recipe, let's address some concerns. Crickets are a nutritious and environmentally friendly source of protein. They are high in micronutrients like iron, zinc, and calcium, and have a lower environmental impact compared to traditional livestock. If you're new to entomophagy, you might be wondering where to find edible crickets. You can purchase cricket powder or whole crickets from specialty food stores or online retailers.

**Ingredients:**

For the Cricket Tacos:

* 1 cup cricket powder (or 1/2 cup whole crickets, toasted and ground)
* 1/2 cup ground beef (or alternative ground meat)
* 1/4 cup chopped onion
* 1 minced garlic clove
* 1 tablespoon olive oil
* 1 teaspoon cumin
* 1/2 teaspoon paprika
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* 8-10 corn tortillas
* Optional toppings: diced avocado, sour cream, shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes

For the Spicy Mango Salsa:

* 2 ripe mangos, diced
* 1/2 cup red onion, diced
* 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and finely chopped
* 1 tablespoon lime juice
* 1 tablespoon honey
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* Fresh cilantro leaves, chopped (optional)

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the cricket mixture:** In a pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until translucent. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. 2. **Combine with ground beef:** Add the ground beef (or alternative ground meat) to the cricket mixture and stir until just combined. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes, breaking up any clumps with a spoon.
3. **Prepare the Spicy Mango Salsa:** In a bowl, combine the diced mango, red onion, jalapeño pepper, lime juice, and honey. Stir well and adjust seasoning as needed.
4. **Assemble the tacos:** Warm the tortillas by wrapping them in a damp paper towel and microwaving for 20-30 seconds. Spoon the cricket-beef mixture onto a tortilla, followed by a spoonful of the Spicy Mango Salsa. Add your desired toppings.

**Tips and Variations:**

* Use different seasonings to change up the flavor profile of the cricket mixture (e.g., Korean chili flakes, Italian herbs, or Mexican oregano).
* Substitute other insects like mealworms or grasshoppers for the crickets.
* Experiment with various types of salsa or hot sauces to find your favorite combination.

**Nutritional Benefits:**

* Crickets are a good source of protein (about 60-70% protein by weight)
* Rich in micronutrients like iron, zinc, and calcium
* Low in fat and calories

Enjoy your Crick-a-Licious Tacos and explore the world of entomophagy!
