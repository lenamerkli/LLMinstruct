**Beef Fajitas**

Ingredients:

Instructions:

1. In a large resealable plastic bag, combine lime juice, garlic, oregano, cumin, and cayenne pepper (if using). Add the beef strips, turning to coat evenly. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours in the refrigerator.
2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Remove the beef from the marinade, letting any excess liquid drip off. **Tips and Variations:**
