assistant: ## Slow Cooker Pulled Pork with Apple Cider BBQ Sauce

This recipe is incredibly easy, delivers tender, flavorful pulled pork, and the apple cider BBQ sauce adds a delicious sweet and tangy twist. It's perfect for sandwiches, tacos, sliders, or even served over rice or mashed potatoes.

**Yields:** 8-10 servings
**Prep time:** 20 minutes
**Cook time:** 6-8 hours on low or 3-4 hours on high in a slow cooker

**Ingredients:**

**For the Pork:**

*   3-4 lb Pork Shoulder (Boston Butt), excess fat trimmed
*   1 large Onion, roughly chopped
*   2 cloves Garlic, minced
*   1 cup Chicken Broth
*   1 tbsp Smoked Paprika
*   1 tbsp Garlic Powder
*   1 tbsp Onion Powder
*   1 tsp Chili Powder
*   1/2 tsp Black Pepper
*   1/2 tsp Salt

**For the Apple Cider BBQ Sauce:**

*   1 cup Apple Cider
*   1/4 cup Ketchup
*   2 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar
*   2 tbsp Brown Sugar, packed
*   1 tbsp Worcestershire Sauce
*   1 tsp Dijon Mustard
*   1/2 tsp Smoked Paprika
*   1/4 tsp Cayenne Pepper (optional, for heat)

**Instructions:**

**1. Prepare the Pork:**

*   Season the pork shoulder generously with salt, pepper, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and chili powder. 
*   Place the chopped onion and minced garlic at the bottom of the slow cooker.
*   Place the seasoned pork shoulder on top of the onions and garlic.
*   Pour the chicken broth around the pork.

**2. Slow Cook:**

*   Cover the slow cooker and cook on low for 6-8 hours, or on high for 3-4 hours.  The pork is ready when it's easily shredded with two forks.

**3. Make the Apple Cider BBQ Sauce (while pork is cooking or after it's cooked):**

*   In a small saucepan, combine apple cider, ketchup, apple cider vinegar, brown sugar, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, smoked paprika, and cayenne pepper (if using).
*   Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring until the brown sugar is dissolved.
*   Reduce heat to low and simmer for about 10-15 minutes, or until the sauce has thickened slightly.

**4. Shred and Serve:**

*   Once the pork is cooked, remove it from the slow cooker and place it on a cutting board.
*   Using two forks, shred the pork. Discard any large pieces of fat.
*   Return the shredded pork to the slow cooker and stir in about 1/2 cup of the apple cider BBQ sauce.  (Add more to taste).
*   Serve the pulled pork on your favorite buns, tacos, sliders, or enjoy it as a main course with sides.  Drizzle with extra BBQ sauce.

**Tips and Variations:**

*   **For extra flavor:**  Add a bay leaf and a sprig of rosemary to the slow cooker while cooking the pork.  Remove before shredding.
*   **Spice it up:**  Increase the amount of cayenne pepper or add a dash of hot sauce to the BBQ sauce.
*   **Sweeten it up:**  Add a tablespoon of honey or maple syrup to the BBQ sauce for a richer sweetness.
*   **Coleslaw is a must:**  Serve with a creamy coleslaw for a classic pulled pork experience.
*   **Leftovers:** Pulled pork is fantastic in nachos, baked potatoes, or as a topping for salads.

Enjoy your delicious slow cooker pulled pork! It's a crowd-pleaser that's surprisingly easy to make.  Let me know if you have any questions.
