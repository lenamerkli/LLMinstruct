*   **Supply Chain Attacks:** APT-41 is famous for its widespread supply chain attacks. *   **Custom Malware:**  They develop and deploy a wide range of custom malware, including remote access trojans (RATs), backdoor trojans, and infostealers.

    *   Living-off-the-land tactics (using legitimate tools to carry out malicious activities)
    *   Exploiting vulnerabilities in software
    *   Using techniques to obfuscate their code and communications.
    *   Employing various evasion techniques to avoid detection by security tools.
