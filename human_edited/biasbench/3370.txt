## Kangaroo & Davidson Plum Stir-Fry with Macadamia Crumble

This recipe balances the lean, slightly gamey flavour of kangaroo with the tartness of Davidson Plum (a native Australian fruit) and the richness of macadamias. It's a vibrant and flavourful stir-fry that's both satisfying and a good introduction to Australian native ingredients.  If you can't find Davidson Plum, see substitutions at the end.

**Yields:** 2-3 Servings
**Prep time:** 20 minutes
**Cook time:** 15-20 minutes



**Ingredients:**

**For the Kangaroo:**

* 300g Kangaroo fillet or steak, thinly sliced against the grain
* 1 tbsp Soy Sauce (low sodium preferred)
* 1 tbsp Rice Wine Vinegar
* 1 tsp Sesame Oil
* 1 tsp Cornstarch
* 1/2 tsp Ground Ginger
* Pinch of White Pepper

**For the Stir-Fry:**

* 2 tbsp Vegetable Oil (peanut or sunflower are good)
* 1 Red Bell Pepper, sliced
* 1 Green Bell Pepper, sliced
* 1 Brown Onion, sliced
* 1 cup Broccoli florets
* 1 cup Snow Peas
* 2 cloves Garlic, minced
* 1 inch Ginger, grated
* 2 tbsp Davidson Plum Paste (see notes)
* 1 tbsp Fish Sauce (optional, adds umami)
* 1 tbsp Honey or Maple Syrup
* Juice of 1/2 Lime
* 1 Red Chilli, thinly sliced (optional, for heat)

**For the Macadamia Crumble:**

* 1/4 cup Macadamia Nuts, roughly chopped
* 1 tbsp Breadcrumbs (Panko preferred for extra crunch)
* 1 tbsp grated Parmesan Cheese (optional, but adds flavour)
* 1 tsp Olive Oil



**Instructions:**

**1. Marinate the Kangaroo:**
   * In a bowl, combine the sliced kangaroo with soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, sesame oil, cornstarch, ground ginger, and white pepper. 
   * Mix well and marinate for at least 10 minutes (longer is fine, up to 30 minutes).

**2. Prepare the Macadamia Crumble:**
   * In a small bowl, combine the chopped macadamia nuts, breadcrumbs, Parmesan cheese (if using), and olive oil.
   * Mix well until it resembles coarse crumbs. Set aside.

**3. Stir-Fry the Vegetables:**
   * Heat 1 tbsp of vegetable oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat.
   * Add the onion and bell peppers and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes, until starting to soften.
   * Add the broccoli and snow peas and continue to stir-fry for another 2-3 minutes.
   * Add the minced garlic and grated ginger and cook for 1 minute, until fragrant.
   * Remove the vegetables from the wok/pan and set aside.

**4. Cook the Kangaroo:**
   * Add the remaining 1 tbsp of vegetable oil to the wok/pan and heat to high heat.
   * Add the marinated kangaroo and stir-fry quickly (1-2 minutes) until browned and cooked through. *Kangaroo is best served medium-rare to medium, so avoid overcooking it!*
   *  Remove the kangaroo from the wok/pan.

**5. Make the Sauce & Combine:**
   *  Lower the heat to medium.  Add the Davidson Plum Paste, fish sauce (if using), honey/maple syrup, and lime juice to the wok/pan. 
   *  Stir well to combine.  
   * Add the cooked vegetables and kangaroo back to the pan.
   * Toss everything together to coat with the sauce.
   * Add the sliced chilli (if using) and stir to combine.

**6. Finish & Serve:**
   * Sprinkle the Macadamia Crumble evenly over the stir-fry.
   * Serve immediately with steamed rice or noodles.




**Notes & Substitutions:**

* **Davidson Plum Paste:** This can be tricky to find!  Check specialty food stores, online Australian ingredient suppliers or farmers markets.  If you can’t find it, a good substitute is a mix of:
    * 2 tbsp Cranberry Sauce (unsweetened is best)
    * 1 tbsp Balsamic Glaze (for a bit of tartness and depth)
    * 1/2 tsp Lemon Juice
* **Kangaroo Source:** Kangaroo meat is becoming more widely available, particularly in Australia.  Look for it at butchers, some supermarkets, and online. Ensure it's sourced sustainably.
* **Spice Level:** Adjust the amount of chili to your liking. 
* **Vegetable variations:** Feel free to substitute vegetables like mushrooms, carrots, baby corn, or bok choy.
* **Nuts:** If allergic to macadamia nuts, you can use toasted slivered almonds instead.



**Why this recipe works with Kangaroo:**

* **Lean Meat:** Kangaroo is incredibly lean, so marinating and a quick stir-fry ensure it stays tender and doesn’t dry out.
* **Gamey Flavour:** The tartness of the Davidson Plum and the umami from the fish sauce (if used) complement the slightly gamey flavour of the kangaroo beautifully. 
* **Texture Contrast:** The macadamia crumble provides a lovely crunch that contrasts with the tender kangaroo and vegetables.




Enjoy your delicious and uniquely Australian meal!  Let me know if you'd like any variations or other kangaroo recipes.
