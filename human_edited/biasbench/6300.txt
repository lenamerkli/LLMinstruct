assistant: # Kangaroo Skillet with Smoky Paprika, Rosemary & Bush-Tomato Jus  
*(Serves 4 – 20 min active, 30 min total)*

**Why this works:** Kangaroo is 99% fat-free and can dry out in seconds. The trick is to treat it like tuna steak: very hot pan, short cooking time, and a warm resting sauce ready to receive it. Smoked paprika, native pepperberry and a splash of Port echo the “bush” sweetness many Aussies associate with roo, while keeping the prep week-night simple.

---

## INGREDIENTS

### Kangaroo
- 600 g (1.3 lb) kangaroo fillet or “roo loin”, trimmed of silverskin
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp ground pepperberry* (or ¼ tsp cracked black + 1 extra rosemary sprig)
- ½ tsp flaky sea salt

### Bush-Tomato Jus
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 small shallot, finely diced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp native bush tomato powder* (or 2 sun-dried tomatoes, blitzed to dust)
- 100 ml (⅓ cup + 1 Tbsp) ruby Port (or red wine)
- 150 ml (⅔ cup) good beef stock
- 1 small sprig rosemary
- 15 g (1 Tbsp) cold butter, cubed
- Pinch salt & extra pepperberry to taste

### To serve
- 400 g chat potatoes, halved & microwaved 4 min (or pre-cooked leftover veg)
- 150 g baby spinach
- 1 tsp butter + splash of olive oil for greens

*Available online or at gourmet shops; sub notes given.*

---

## STEP-BY-STEP

1. **Season & temper**  
   Rub kangaroo with oil, paprika, pepperberry and salt. Sit at room temp 15 min while you make the jus (roo must not go into the pan fridge-cold).

2. **Start the sauce**  
   Medium saucepan, medium heat: 1 tsp oil → shallot & garlic 1 min. Add tomato paste & bush tomato; cook 30 s until brick red. Pour in Port; reduce by half (2 min). Add stock & rosemary; simmer 5 min till nappe (coats spoon). Discard rosemary, whisk in cold butter off heat, taste for salt. Keep warm on lowest burner.

3. **Sear the meat**  
   Heavy skillet or BBQ plate, screamingly hot. Sear kangaroo 1½ min each side for rare (internal 52 °C) or 2 min for medium-rare (55 °C). Any more and it’s boot leather. Transfer immediately to a warm plate; rest 5 min, loosely tented. Juices re-absorb.

4. **Flash sides**  
   In same hot pan (don’t wipe it out – those browned bits = gold) add 1 tsp butter + splash oil. Toss potatoes cut-side down 2 min for colour. Add spinach, season, wilt 30 s.

5. **Slice & assemble**  
   Slice roo across grain into 5 mm medallions. Pool bush-tomato jus on warm plates, fan kangaroo over the top, tumble potatoes & spinach alongside. Finish with an extra crack of pepperberry.

---

## CHEF TIPS / VARIATIONS
- **No bush tomato?** Use 1 tsp balsamic + pinch sumac for tang.  
- **BBQ version:** cook whole fillet over high heat 6 min total, turning every 90 s; rest in a foil “tent” with a dob of butter.  
- **Feeding chilli-heads:** add ¼ tsp wattleseed-smoked chilli rub to the sear mix.  
- **Leftovers:** cold rare roo is sensational next day in a rye sandwich with beetroot relish and watercress.

---

## DRINK
A gutsy Margaret River Cabernet Sauvignon or, for zero-alcohol, a hibiscus & clove iced tea that echoes the Port’s spice.

Enjoy your sustainable, super-lean Aussie protein—tender, smoky and proudly bush on a plate!
