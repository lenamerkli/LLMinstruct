Spicy Herb Beef Stew (inspired by traditional Korean herbal stews)

Serves 4–6

Ingredients
- 2 lb beef chuck, cut into 1.5-inch cubes
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 2 tbsp doenjang (Korean soybean paste)
- 1.5 tbsp gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- 1–2 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chili flakes), to taste
- 1 tbsp soy sauce, plus salt to taste
- 1 tsp fish sauce (optional)
- 8 cups beef stock or anchovy–kelp broth
- 1 lb daikon radish, peeled and cut into chunks
- 3 tbsp perilla seed powder (deulkkae garu); substitute finely ground toasted sesame if needed
- 1 cup perilla leaves, torn; substitute shiso or Thai basil
- 1 bunch garlic chives or scallions, 2-inch pieces
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- Black pepper to taste

Steps
1. Pat beef dry and brown in a heavy pot with oil over medium-high heat. Remove.
2. In the same pot, sauté onion 3–4 minutes. Add garlic and ginger, 30 seconds.
3. Stir in doenjang, gochujang, and gochugaru; toast 30 seconds.
4. Return beef, add stock, soy sauce, and optional fish sauce. Bring to a boil, skim, then simmer covered 60–90 minutes until tender.
5. Add daikon; simmer 20 minutes until just soft.
6. Stir in perilla seed powder and black pepper; adjust salt or chili to taste.
7. Add perilla leaves and chives; simmer 1–2 minutes. Finish with sesame oil.
8. Serve hot with rice and kimchi.

Plant-based option: Replace beef with 14 oz firm tofu (cubed) and 12 oz king oyster mushrooms (chunked); simmer only 20–25 minutes total.
