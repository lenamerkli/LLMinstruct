### Hearty Beef Stew Recipe

**Ingredients (serves 4-6):**
- 1.5 kg (about 3 lbs) of beef chuck or stewing beef, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 medium carrots, sliced
- 3 celery stalks, chopped
- 4 medium potatoes, diced
- 2 cups beef broth (low-sodium if possible)
- 1 cup red wine (optional, for extra depth; substitute with more broth if you prefer)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 bay leaves
- Fresh parsley for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. **Prep the beef:** Pat the beef cubes dry with paper towels and season generously with salt and pepper.
2. **Sear the meat:** In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the beef in batches and sear until browned on all sides (about 5 minutes per batch). Remove the beef and set aside.
3. **Build the base:** In the same pot, add the onion and garlic. Sauté for 2-3 minutes until softened. Add the carrots and celery, cooking for another 5 minutes.
4. **Add liquids and seasonings:** Stir in the tomato paste, thyme, rosemary, and bay leaves. Pour in the beef broth and red wine (if using). Bring to a simmer and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
5. **Combine and cook:** Return the beef to the pot along with the potatoes. Cover and simmer on low heat for about 1.5 to 2 hours, or until the beef is tender. You can also transfer to a slow cooker for 4-6 hours on low.
6. **Finish and serve:** Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Remove the bay leaves, garnish with fresh parsley, and serve hot with crusty bread or over rice.
