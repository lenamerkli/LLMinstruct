**Grilled Chirica Beetle Tacos with Chipotle Slaw**

**Ingredients:**

* 2 cloves garlic, minced

**Chipotle Slaw:**

* 1/2 cup diced red onion
* 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and finely chopped
* 2 tablespoons lime juice
* 1 teaspoon honey
* Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
