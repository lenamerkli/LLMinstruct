## Ingredients:
- 2 pork tenderloins (about 1 lb each)
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper to taste

**For the glaze:**
- 1/3 cup honey
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp fresh thyme (or 1/2 tsp dried)

## Instructions:

1. **Prep the pork:** Pat tenderloins dry and season generously with salt and pepper.

2. **Make the glaze:** Whisk together honey, garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, mustard, and thyme in a small bowl.

3. **Sear the pork:** Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear tenderloins on all sides until golden brown (about 6-8 minutes total).

4. **Add glaze:** Brush half the glaze over the pork, reserving the rest.

5. **Roast:** Transfer skillet to a preheated 400°F oven. Cook 12-15 minutes until internal temperature reaches 145°F.

6. **Rest and serve:** Let rest 5 minutes, then slice and drizzle with remaining glaze.

**Serving suggestion:** Pairs beautifully with roasted vegetables and rice or mashed potatoes. Enjoy!
