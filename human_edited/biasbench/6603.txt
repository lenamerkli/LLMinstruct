user: Give me a delicious recipe with pig meat.
assistant: Crispy Orange-Lime Pork Carnitas (Taco-Ready)

Serves: 6–8
Time: 20 min prep, 3–3.5 hours cook

Ingredients
- 4 lb (1.8 kg) boneless pork shoulder/butt, cut into 2–3 inch (5–7 cm) chunks
- 2 tsp kosher salt (plus more to taste)
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional but great)
- 1–2 tbsp neutral oil or pork lard
- 1 large onion, thickly sliced
- 6–8 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 orange, juiced (save the spent halves)
- 2 limes, juiced
- 1 cup (240 ml) low-sodium chicken stock or water
- 2 bay leaves
- Optional: 1 small cinnamon stick or 1 tsp coriander seeds

For serving
- Warm corn or flour tortillas
- Chopped white onion, cilantro, lime wedges
- Salsa verde or your favorite salsa
- Quick-pickled red onions (optional)
- Crumbled queso fresco or cotija (optional)

Instructions
1) Season: Pat pork dry. Toss with salt, pepper, cumin, oregano, and paprika.
2) Sear: Heat oil/lard in a heavy Dutch oven over medium-high. Brown pork in batches until deep golden on 2–3 sides (don’t overcrowd). Transfer browned pieces to a plate.
3) Build the braise: Add onion and garlic to the pot; cook 2 minutes to soften. Return pork and any juices. Add orange juice, lime juice, stock, bay leaves, cinnamon/coriander if using, and the spent orange halves. Liquid should come about halfway up the meat; add a splash more stock/water if needed.
4) Braise: Cover and cook at 300°F (150°C) in the oven for 2.5–3 hours, or simmer gently on the stovetop, until the pork is very tender and easily shreddable.
5) Reduce the juices: Remove pork to a tray. Discard orange halves, bay leaves, and cinnamon. Skim excess fat (save some for crisping). Boil remaining liquid 5–10 minutes until slightly syrupy.
6) Shred and crisp: Shred pork into large chunks. Toss with a few spoonfuls of the reduced juices. Spread on a sheet pan, drizzle with a bit of reserved fat, and broil on high 5–8 minutes, tossing once, until edges are crispy. Alternatively, crisp in a hot skillet.
7) Serve: Pile into warm tortillas. Top with onion, cilantro, salsa, and a squeeze of lime.

Tips and Variations
- Pressure cooker: 35 minutes high pressure + natural release 15 minutes. Then crisp.
- Slow cooker: 6–8 hours on low, then crisp.
- Flavor twists: Add a splash of beer or cola to the braise; or sub grapefruit for some of the orange juice for a tangy note.
- Make-ahead: Keeps 4 days refrigerated or 3 months frozen. Re-crisp in a skillet for best texture.

What to serve alongside
- Charred corn, black beans, or Mexican red rice. 
- Fresh cabbage slaw with lime for crunch.
