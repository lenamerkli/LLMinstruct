Okay, let's make **Slow-Roasted Pulled Pork with Cherry-Chipotle BBQ Sauce**.  This is *delicious*, relatively easy (mostly hands-off!), and everyone loves it. It's perfect for a weekend project or a casual gathering.

**Yields:**  About 8-10 servings
**Prep time:** 20-30 minutes
**Cook time:** 8-10 hours (low and slow is key!)



**Ingredients:**

* **For the Pork:**
    * 3-4 lb Pork Shoulder (Boston Butt) - *This is the best cut for pulled pork.  Look for one with good marbling (fat) running through it.*
    * 2 tbsp Brown Sugar
    * 1 tbsp Smoked Paprika
    * 1 tbsp Garlic Powder
    * 1 tbsp Onion Powder
    * 1 tbsp Ground Cumin
    * 1 tsp Chili Powder
    * 1 tsp Dried Oregano
    * 1 tsp Salt (Kosher or Sea)
    * 1/2 tsp Black Pepper
    * 1 tbsp Olive Oil

* **For the Cherry-Chipotle BBQ Sauce:**
    * 1 cup Cherry Preserves (or tart cherry pie filling - NOT gel!)
    * 1/4 cup Apple Cider Vinegar 
    * 2-3 Chipotle Peppers in Adobo Sauce, minced (depending on spice preference - start with 2, you can add more!)  *Don't forget a little of the adobo sauce too!*
    * 2 tbsp Molasses
    * 1 tbsp Worcestershire Sauce
    * 1 tbsp Dijon Mustard
    * 1 tsp Garlic Powder
    * 1/2 tsp Smoked Paprika
    * Pinch of Cayenne Pepper (optional, for extra heat)

* **For Serving:**
    * Buns (Brioche, Potato, or your favorite)
    * Coleslaw (store-bought or homemade - a vinegar-based slaw is great with this!)
    * Pickles (Dill or Bread and Butter)



**Instructions:**

**1. Prepare the Pork Rub:**

* In a small bowl, combine the brown sugar, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, chili powder, oregano, salt, and pepper.  Mix well.

**2. Season the Pork:**

* Pat the pork shoulder dry with paper towels. (Important for a good crust!)
* Rub the entire pork shoulder generously with the olive oil, then coat it thoroughly with the spice rub.  Make sure to get into all the nooks and crannies.

**3. Slow Roast the Pork:**

* **Oven Method:** Preheat oven to 275°F (135°C). Place the pork shoulder in a large Dutch oven (with a lid) or a roasting pan.  Add about 1/2 cup of water or chicken broth to the bottom of the pan.  Cover tightly.  Roast for 8-10 hours, or until the pork is *extremely* tender and easily shreds with a fork.
* **Slow Cooker Method:** Place the pork shoulder in your slow cooker.  Pour in about 1/2 cup of water or chicken broth.  Cook on LOW for 8-10 hours, or until the pork is extremely tender.

**4. Make the Cherry-Chipotle BBQ Sauce (During the last hour of cooking):**

* In a medium saucepan, combine the cherry preserves, apple cider vinegar, minced chipotle peppers (and a little of the adobo sauce!), molasses, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and cayenne pepper (if using).
* Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring occasionally.  Simmer for about 15-20 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the sauce to thicken slightly. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed (more chipotle for heat, more vinegar for tang, more molasses for sweetness).

**5. Shred the Pork:**

* Once the pork is cooked, remove it from the oven/slow cooker and let it rest for about 15-20 minutes.  This helps the juices redistribute.
* Using two forks, shred the pork.  Discard any large pieces of fat.

**6. Combine and Finish:**

* Transfer the shredded pork to a large bowl.  Pour about 1-2 cups of the Cherry-Chipotle BBQ Sauce over the pork and toss to coat.  Add more sauce to your preference.

**7. Serve:**

* Serve the pulled pork on buns with your favorite toppings: coleslaw, pickles, and extra BBQ sauce.



**Tips and Variations:**

* **Spice Level:**  Chipotle peppers can vary in heat. Start with 2, taste, and add more if you like it spicier.  Removing the seeds from the chipotle peppers will also reduce the heat.
* **Smoky Flavor:** If you’re using the oven, adding a teaspoon of liquid smoke to the cooking liquid can enhance the smoky flavor.
* **Sear (Optional):** For extra flavor, you can sear the pork shoulder on all sides in a hot skillet before rubbing it with the spice blend.
* **Add Fruit:** A diced apple or peach added to the bottom of the roasting pan during cooking can add a lovely sweetness.
* **Leftovers:** Pulled pork freezes beautifully!



Enjoy!  Let me know if you have any questions or want to explore other pig-centric recipes.
