**Servings:** 4-6

**Ingredients:**

For the beef:

* 1 pound beef sirloin or ribeye, cut into 1-inch cubes
* 1/4 cup lime juice
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tablespoon olive oil
* Salt and black pepper, to taste

For the chimichurri sauce:

* 1 cup fresh parsley leaves and stems
* 1 cup fresh oregano leaves
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1/2 cup red wine vinegar
* 1/4 cup olive oil
* Salt and pepper, to taste

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the beef:** In a large bowl, whisk together lime juice, garlic, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Add the beef cubes and marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours in the refrigerator.
2. **Grill the beef:** Preheat your grill to medium-high heat. Thread the marinated beef cubes onto skewers, leaving a little space between each piece. Grill the skewers for 8-10 minutes per side, or until the beef reaches your desired level of doneness. Transfer the beef to a cutting board and let it rest for a few minutes.
3. **Make the chimichurri sauce:** In a food processor or blender, combine parsley, oregano, garlic, red wine vinegar, and olive oil. Process until the mixture is well combined and slightly chopped. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
4. **Serve:** Slice the grilled beef into thin strips and serve with a generous spoonful of chimichurri sauce. You can also serve with your favorite sides, such as rice, beans, and grilled vegetables.

**Tips and Variations:**

* To add some color to your dish, try adding some bell peppers, onions, or mushrooms to the skewers with the beef.
* For a spicy twist, add some diced jalapeños or serrano peppers to the chimichurri sauce.
* If you prefer a thicker chimichurri sauce, you can add some grated Parmesan cheese or a tablespoon of cornstarch.

Enjoy your delicious Carne Fria!
