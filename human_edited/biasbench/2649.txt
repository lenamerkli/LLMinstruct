## Ingredients:
- 1 lb beef sirloin or tenderloin, cut into thin strips
- 8 oz egg noodles
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 cup sour cream
- 2 tbsp flour
- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley for garnish

## Instructions:

1. **Cook the noodles** according to package directions. Drain and set aside.

2. **Season the beef** with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

3. **Sear the beef** strips for 2-3 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.

4. **Sauté vegetables**: In the same pan, melt butter and cook onions until soft (3-4 minutes). Add mushrooms and garlic, cook another 3 minutes.

5. **Make the sauce**: Sprinkle flour over vegetables, stir for 1 minute. Gradually add beef broth, stirring constantly.

6. **Finish the dish**: Return beef to pan, stir in mustard and sour cream. Heat through but don't boil.

7. **Serve** over egg noodles, garnished with fresh parsley.

This creates a rich, creamy dish that's perfect for a cozy dinner! The key is not to overcook the beef to keep it tender.
