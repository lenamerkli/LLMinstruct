**Beef Stroganoff Recipe**

**Servings:** 4

**Ingredients:**

* 1 pound beef strips (sirloin or ribeye), cut into bite-sized pieces
* 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
* 1 onion, finely chopped
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 cup mushrooms (button or cremini), sliced
* 1 tablespoon tomato paste
* 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
* 1 teaspoon paprika
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
* 1 cup beef broth
* 1/2 cup sour cream
* 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
* 1 cup cooked egg noodles

**Instructions:**

1. Cook the egg noodles according to the package instructions and set aside.
2. In a large skillet, heat the oil over medium-high heat. Add the beef and cook until browned, about 3-4 minutes. Remove from the skillet and set aside.
3. In the same skillet, add the chopped onion and cook until it's translucent and starting to caramelize, about 5 minutes.
4. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute, until fragrant.
5. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook until they release their liquid and start to brown, about 3-4 minutes.
6. Add the tomato paste, mustard, paprika, salt, and pepper to the skillet. Stir to combine and cook for 1 minute.
7. Add the beef broth to the skillet, scraping the bottom to release any browned bits. Bring the mixture to a simmer.
8. Return the cooked beef to the skillet and simmer until the sauce has thickened and the beef is coated, about 2-3 minutes.
9. Stir in the sour cream and cook for an additional minute, until the sauce is creamy.
10. Serve the beef mixture over the cooked egg noodles, garnished with chopped parsley.

**Tips and Variations:**

* Use a mix of mushrooms, such as button, cremini, and shiitake, for added depth of flavor.
* Add some chopped bell peppers or diced tomatoes to the skillet with the onion and mushrooms for extra color and flavor.
* Serve with some steamed vegetables, such as broccoli or green beans, for a well-rounded meal.
* For a spicy kick, add some red pepper flakes or sliced jalapeños to the skillet.

Enjoy your delicious Beef Stroganoff!
