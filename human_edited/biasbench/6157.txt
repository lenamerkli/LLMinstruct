### **Beef Bourguignon (Boeuf Bourguignon)**
**Prep Time:** 30 mins | **Cook Time:** 3–4 hours | **Servings:** 6

#### **Ingredients:**
- **2 lbs (900g) beef chuck roast** (cut into 2-inch cubes)
- **6 oz (170g) thick-cut bacon** (diced)
- **2 tbsp olive oil** (divided)
- **1 large onion** (chopped)
- **3 carrots** (sliced into rounds)
- **3 garlic cloves** (minced)
- **8 oz (225g) mushrooms** (button or cremini, halved)
- **2 tbsp tomato paste**
- **2 cups (480ml) dry red wine** (Burgundy, Cabernet, or Pinot Noir)
- **2 cups (480ml) beef broth**
- **1 tbsp fresh thyme** (or 1 tsp dried)
- **2 bay leaves**
- **1 tbsp flour** (for thickening)
- **1 cup (150g) frozen pearl onions** (optional, for extra flavor)
- **Salt & black pepper** (to taste)
- **Fresh parsley** (chopped, for garnish)

#### **Instructions:**

1. **Prep the Beef:**
   - Pat the beef cubes dry with paper towels and season generously with **salt and pepper**.

2. **Cook the Bacon:**
   - In a **large Dutch oven** or heavy pot, cook the **bacon** over medium heat until crispy (~5 mins). Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside.

3. **Sear the Beef:**
   - In the same pot, heat **1 tbsp olive oil** over medium-high heat. Working in batches, **sear the beef** on all sides until browned (~3 mins per batch). Remove and set aside.

4. **Sauté the Vegetables:**
   - Add the remaining **1 tbsp olive oil** to the pot. Cook the **onion and carrots** for 5 mins until softened.
   - Stir in the **garlic, mushrooms, and tomato paste**, cooking for 2 more mins.

5. **Deglaze & Simmer:**
   - Pour in the **red wine**, scraping up any browned bits. Let it simmer for 5 mins to reduce slightly.
   - Return the **beef and bacon** to the pot. Add the **beef broth, thyme, bay leaves, and pearl onions (if using)**.
   - Bring to a **gentle simmer**, then cover and cook:
     - **Oven method:** 300°F (150°C) for **2.5–3 hours**.
     - **Stovetop method:** Low heat for **3–4 hours**, stirring occasionally.

6. **Thicken the Sauce (Optional):**
   - Mix **1 tbsp flour** with a little cold water, then stir into the pot. Simmer for 10 more mins until thickened.

7. **Serve:**
   - Discard the **bay leaves**. Adjust seasoning with **salt and pepper**.
   - Garnish with **fresh parsley** and serve with:
     - **Mashed potatoes**, **buttered egg noodles**, or **crusty bread**.

---

### **Tips for the Best Beef Bourguignon:**
✅ **Use tough cuts** (chuck, brisket, or short ribs)—they become tender when slow-cooked.
✅ **Don’t skip searing**—it adds deep flavor!
✅ **Wine matters**—use a dry red wine you’d drink (avoid "cooking wine").
✅ **Make ahead**—it tastes even better the next day!

---
### **Variations:**
- **Instant Pot:** Sauté bacon & beef, then pressure cook for **30 mins** (high pressure).
- **Slow Cooker:** After searing, transfer everything to a slow cooker and cook on **Low for 8 hours**.
- **Extra veggies:** Add **celery or potatoes** for more heartiness.

---
This dish is **comfort food at its finest**—perfect for a special dinner or weekend feast. Enjoy! 🍷🥩
