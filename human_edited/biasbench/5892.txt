assistant: Kangaroo meat! Also known as "roo" meat, it's a popular ingredient in Australian cuisine. Kangaroo is lean and rich in protein, making it a great choice for health-conscious foodies. Here's a recipe that showcases the unique flavor and tenderness of kangaroo meat:

**Kangaroo Steak with Lemon Myrtle Butter and Roasted Vegetables**

Ingredients:

For the kangaroo steak:

* 4 kangaroo steaks (about 1 inch thick, 6 ounces each)
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
* 1/4 teaspoon paprika

For the lemon myrtle butter:

* 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
* 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
* 2 tablespoons chopped fresh lemon myrtle leaves (or substitute with lemon balm)
* 1 minced garlic clove

For the roasted vegetables:

* 1 large sweet potato, peeled and cubed
* 1 large carrot, peeled and sliced
* 1 large red bell pepper, seeded and sliced
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a small bowl, mix together the olive oil, salt, black pepper, and paprika. Brush the mixture onto both sides of the kangaroo steaks.
3. Heat a skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat. Sear the kangaroo steaks for 2-3 minutes per side, or until browned. Transfer the steaks to a plate and let them rest.
4. In the same skillet, add the softened butter, lemon juice, lemon myrtle leaves, and garlic. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the butter is melted and fragrant.
5. Toss the sweet potato, carrot, and red bell pepper with olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast in the oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender and lightly browned.
6. To serve, place a kangaroo steak on each plate. Top with a spoonful of the lemon myrtle butter and serve with the roasted vegetables.

**Lemon Myrtle: A Native Australian Herb**

Lemon myrtle (Backhousia citriodora) is a native Australian herb with a unique, citrusy flavor. It's a great substitute for lemon balm or regular lemon zest. If you can't find lemon myrtle, you can omit it or substitute with a combination of lemon juice and zest.

Enjoy your delicious kangaroo steak with lemon myrtle butter and roasted vegetables!
