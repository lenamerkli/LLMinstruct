**Crick-tastic Stir-Fry with Chili-Garlic Sauce**

Ingredients:

* 1 cup crickets, roasted and chopped (see notes)
* 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
* 1 small onion, thinly sliced
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tablespoon grated ginger
* 1 tablespoon soy sauce
* 1 tablespoon honey
* 1 tablespoon fish sauce (optional)
* 1 tablespoon chili-garlic sauce (see below)
* Salt and pepper to taste
* Fresh cilantro leaves for garnish
* Cooked rice or noodles for serving

**Chili-Garlic Sauce:**

* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tablespoon sriracha sauce
* 1 tablespoon lime juice
* 1 tablespoon honey

Instructions:

1. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until translucent (about 3-4 minutes).
2. Add garlic and ginger, cooking for an additional minute, until fragrant.
3. Add crickets, stirring constantly to prevent burning. Cook for 2-3 minutes, until they develop a light brown color.
4. Mix in soy sauce, honey, fish sauce (if using), and chili-garlic sauce. Stir-fry for another 2-3 minutes, until the crickets are evenly coated.
5. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Garnish with cilantro leaves.
6. Serve over cooked rice or noodles, and enjoy!

**Roasting Crickets:**

Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Rinse the crickets in cold water, then pat them dry with paper towels. Spread the crickets in a single layer on a baking sheet, and roast for 10-15 minutes, or until they're lightly browned and fragrant. Allow them to cool before chopping.

**Where to Buy Insects for Food:**

Insects for human consumption are available in various forms and can be sourced from online retailers, specialty stores, or some high-end grocery stores.

**Food Safety Note:**

As with any new food, ensure you're purchasing from a reputable source that adheres to strict food safety guidelines.

This recipe is a delicious introduction to entomophagy, and you can adjust the seasonings to suit your taste preferences. Enjoy exploring the wonderful world of insect cuisine!
