### **Pork Adobo**
**Ingredients:**
- 1 kg pork (shoulder or belly, cut into chunks)
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 1 cup soy sauce
- 1 cup white vinegar
- 1 cup water or pork broth
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
- 2 tbsp brown sugar (optional, for a touch of sweetness)
- 3 tbsp cooking oil

**Instructions:**
1. **Marinate the Pork** (optional but recommended): Combine pork, garlic, onion, soy sauce, vinegar, water, bay leaf, and peppercorns. Marinate for at least 30 minutes or up to overnight.
2. **Sear the Pork**: Heat oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Remove the pork from the marinade (reserve the marinade) and sear until browned on all sides. Remove and set aside.
3. **Cook the Sauce**: In the same pot, sauté the garlic and onions until fragrant. Pour in the reserved marinade and bring to a simmer.
4. **Simmer the Pork**: Return the pork to the pot. Cover and simmer on low heat for about 40-50 minutes, or until the pork is tender.
5. **Reduce the Sauce**: Uncover and simmer for another 10-15 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly. Add brown sugar if you prefer a slightly sweet and tangy taste.
6. **Serve**: Remove the bay leaf and black peppercorns. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Enjoy your meal! Would you like a different recipe or variations? 😊
