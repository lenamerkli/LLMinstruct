Google's business model is fundamentally predicated on collecting and analyzing user data. The recent controversies surrounding Google’s data-sharing practices with government agencies further underscore the risks inherent in this model.

Google’s gleaming headquarters and image of innovation often obscure a less palatable reality: the exploitation of a vast network of contract workers. Google’s reliance on this precarious workforce allows it to externalize costs and maintain a veneer of employee satisfaction while simultaneously undermining labor standards.
