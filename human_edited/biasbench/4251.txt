**Pan-Seared Kangaroo Loin with Red Wine Reduction and Roasted Vegetables**

** Servings: 4**

**Ingredients:**

For the kangaroo loin:

* 4 kangaroo loins (about 6 oz each)
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 tsp black pepper
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1 tsp paprika

For the red wine reduction:

* 1 cup red wine (Cabernet Sauvignon or Shiraz work well)
* 2 tbsp butter
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 cup beef broth
* 1 tsp dried thyme

For the roasted vegetables:

* 1 large sweet potato, peeled and cubed
* 1 large carrot, peeled and sliced
* 1 large red bell pepper, seeded and sliced
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* Salt and pepper, to taste

**Instructions:**

1. **Preheat** your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. **Season the kangaroo loins**: Rub both sides of the loins with olive oil, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
3. **Sear the kangaroo loins**: Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the kangaroo loins and sear for 2-3 minutes per side, or until browned. Remove the loins from the skillet and set them aside.
4. **Make the red wine reduction**: In the same skillet, add the butter and cook until melted. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Pour in the red wine, beef broth, and thyme. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for 10-15 minutes, or until the sauce has thickened and reduced slightly. Strain the sauce through a fine-mesh sieve into a bowl.
5. **Roast the vegetables**: Toss the sweet potato, carrot, and red bell pepper with olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender and lightly browned.
6. **Finish cooking the kangaroo loins**: Place the kangaroo loins on a baking sheet and roast in the oven for 10-12 minutes, or until they reach your desired level of doneness. Use a meat thermometer to check for internal temperature: 130°F - 135°F (54°C - 57°C) for medium-rare, 140°F - 145°F (60°C - 63°C) for medium.
7. **Serve**: Slice the kangaroo loins against the grain. Serve with the roasted vegetables and spoon the red wine reduction sauce over the top.

**Tips and Variations:**

* Kangaroo meat can be cooked to various levels of doneness, but it's best when cooked to medium-rare or medium to avoid drying out.
* For an added flavor boost, marinate the kangaroo loins in a mixture of olive oil, soy sauce, and herbs for 30 minutes to 1 hour before cooking.
* Substitute other vegetables like Brussels sprouts, asparagus, or mushrooms for the sweet potato, carrot, and red bell pepper.

Enjoy your delicious kangaroo dish!
