### **Ingredients:**
- 1 cup crickets (dried or freeze-dried, available online or at specialty stores)
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp chili flakes (optional, for heat)
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
- Salt and pepper to taste

### **Instructions:**
1. **Prepare the crickets:**
   - If using dried crickets, rinse them in water and pat dry.
   - If using freeze-dried crickets, they’re already ready to use.

2. **Toast the crickets:**
   - Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
   - Add the crickets and toast for 2-3 minutes until they become crispy. Remove and set aside.

3. **Make the garlic butter sauce:**
   - In the same pan, melt the butter.
   - Add minced garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
   - Add chili flakes (if using), lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

4. **Coat the crickets:**
   - Toss the crispy crickets in the garlic butter sauce until well coated.

5. **Serve:**
   - Garnish with fresh parsley and serve as a crunchy snack or on top of salads, pasta, or rice.

### **Tips:**
- If you're new to eating insects, start with small amounts to get used to the texture.
- You can also grind crickets into a powder for protein-rich flour or add them to smoothies!
