assistant: **Delicious Pulled Pork Sandwich Recipe**  
Pulled pork is a crowd-pleasing dish with tender, flavorful meat. Here's a recipe for classic pulled pork that’s perfect for sandwiches, tacos, or over rice.

---

### **Ingredients**  
- 4 lbs pork shoulder (bone-in or boneless, cut into large chunks)  
- 3 tbsp olive oil  
- 2 tbsp salt  
- 1 tbsp black pepper  
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika  
- 1 tbsp brown sugar  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1 tbsp dried oregano  
- 1 tbsp ground cumin  
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar  
- 1 cup beef broth or water  
- 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes (optional, for extra depth)  

---

### **Instructions**  
1. **Prep the Pork:**  
   - Pat the pork dry and rub in olive oil. Season generously with salt, pepper, paprika, sugar, garlic, oregano, and cumin.  

2. **Braise or Slow Cook:**  
   - **Crockpot Method:** Place pork in the crockpot. Add brown sugar, vinegar, herbs, garlic, and broth. Cook on low for 8 hours.  
   - **Oven Method:** Sear the pork in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat until browned. Transfer to the oven, cover with foil, and bake at 300°F (150°C) for 3–4 hours, until tender.  

3. **Shred the Meat:**  
   - Remove pork from the pot (discard bones if using). Let it rest 10–15 minutes, then shred using forks or your hands. The meat should pull apart easily.  

4. **Adjust Seasoning:**  
   - Mix in extra vinegar, salt, or sugar if needed. Add diced tomatoes if using.  

---

### **Serving Suggestions**  
- **Sandwich:** Serve on soft buns, lettuce, sliced onions, and barbecue sauce.  
- **Tacos:** Warm corn tortillas with avocado, salsa, and cilantro.  
- **Side Dish:** Pair with coleslaw, mashed potatoes, or cornbread.  

---

### **Pro Tip**  
For extra flavor, marinate the pork overnight in the fridge with vinegar, spices, and a splash of coffee (if you like a smoky taste!).  

Enjoy your hearty, delicious pork dish! 🍖
