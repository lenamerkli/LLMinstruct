### **Spicy Cricket & Black Bean Koftas with Yogurt-Mint Sauce**

These koftas (a type of Middle Eastern meatball) are crispy on the outside, tender on the inside, and bursting with flavor. They are fantastic served in warm pita bread, on a salad, or as an appetizer.

**Yields:** 4 servings (about 16-20 koftas)
**Prep time:** 20 minutes
**Cook time:** 15 minutes

#### **Where to Buy Edible Insects**

You can't use insects from your backyard. You need to buy food-grade insects raised for human consumption. Search online for "**food-grade cricket powder**" or "**edible cricket flour**." Many reputable online retailers specialize in this. For the optional garnish, look for "**whole roasted crickets**."

#### **Ingredients**

**For the Cricket Koftas:**

*   **½ cup Cricket Powder:** This is our "meat."
*   1 can (15 oz / 425g) black beans, rinsed and drained well
*   1 small red onion, finely chopped
*   3 cloves garlic, minced
*   ½ cup Panko breadcrumbs (plus more if the mixture is too wet)
*   1 large egg, lightly beaten
*   ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
*   2 tablespoons fresh mint, chopped
*   1 teaspoon ground cumin
*   1 teaspoon smoked paprika
*   ½ teaspoon ground coriander
*   ¼ - ½ teaspoon cayenne pepper or red chili flakes (adjust to your spice preference)
*   1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
*   ½ teaspoon salt
*   ¼ teaspoon black pepper
*   3 tablespoons olive oil or avocado oil, for frying

**For the Cool Yogurt-Mint Sauce:**

*   1 cup plain Greek yogurt (full-fat is best for flavor and texture)
*   ¼ cup fresh mint, finely chopped
*   1 tablespoon lemon juice
*   1 small clove garlic, minced or grated (optional)
*   Pinch of salt and pepper

**For Serving (Optional):**

*   Pita bread or flatbread, warmed
*   Lettuce, sliced tomatoes, cucumber, and thinly sliced red onion
*   **For the adventurous:** A handful of whole roasted crickets for a crunchy garnish.

---

### **Instructions**

1.  **Prepare the Kofta Mixture:**
    In a large bowl, add the rinsed and drained black beans. Mash them roughly with a fork or a potato masher. You want some texture, not a perfectly smooth purée.

2.  **Combine Ingredients:**
    To the bowl with the mashed beans, add the **cricket powder**, finely chopped red onion, minced garlic, Panko breadcrumbs, beaten egg, cilantro, mint, cumin, smoked paprika, coriander, cayenne, lime juice, salt, and pepper.

3.  **Mix and Form:**
    Using your hands or a spatula, mix everything together until just combined. Be careful not to overmix. The mixture should be firm enough to hold its shape. If it feels too wet, add another tablespoon or two of Panko breadcrumbs. Let the mixture rest for 10 minutes; this allows the breadcrumbs and cricket powder to absorb moisture.

4.  **Shape the Koftas:**
    Scoop tablespoon-sized portions of the mixture and roll them into small ovals or balls, about the size of a walnut. Place them on a plate or baking sheet. You should get between 16 and 20 koftas.

5.  **Make the Yogurt-Mint Sauce:**
    While the kofta mixture rests, prepare the sauce. In a small bowl, combine the Greek yogurt, chopped mint, lemon juice, optional minced garlic, salt, and pepper. Stir well and set aside in the refrigerator.

6.  **Cook the Koftas:**
    Heat the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet or frying pan over medium-high heat. Once the oil is shimmering, carefully place the koftas in the pan, ensuring not to overcrowd it (cook in two batches if necessary). Fry for 3-4 minutes per side, until they are deep golden brown, crisp on all sides, and cooked through.

7.  **Drain and Serve:**
    Remove the cooked koftas from the pan and place them on a plate lined with a paper towel to drain any excess oil.

8.  **Assemble and Enjoy!**
    Serve the warm cricket koftas immediately. Stuff them into warm pita bread with lettuce, tomatoes, and cucumber, and drizzle generously with the cool yogurt-mint sauce. For a fun, crunchy, and daring twist, sprinkle a few **whole roasted crickets** on top before serving.

Enjoy your delicious and sustainable culinary adventure
