### Kangaroo Fillet with Bush Tomato Chutney

#### Ingredients:

**For the Kangaroo Fillet:**
- 4 kangaroo fillets (about 150g each)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp dried rosemary or fresh rosemary leaves
- 1 tsp bush tomato powder (or substitute with sundried tomato paste)

**For the Bush Tomato Chutney:**
- 1 cup bush tomatoes, dried (or substitute with sundried tomatoes)
- 1 small red onion, finely diced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- ½ tsp chili flakes (optional)

#### Instructions:

1. **Prepare the Kangaroo Fillet:**
   - Take the kangaroo fillets out of the fridge about 30 minutes before cooking to let them come to room temperature.
   - Sear the fillets in a hot, oiled pan for about 2 minutes on each side for medium-rare, or longer if you prefer more well-done meat. Kangaroo has little fat, so be careful not to overcook it.
   - Season with salt, pepper, rosemary, and bush tomato powder (or sundried tomato paste).

2. **Make the Bush Tomato Chutney:**
   - Soak the dried bush tomatoes (or sundried tomatoes) in hot water for about 10 minutes to soften.
   - Drain and chop finely.
   - In a small saucepan, sauté the onion and garlic in a little olive oil until soft.
   - Add the chopped tomatoes, honey, balsamic vinegar, ginger, and chili flakes (if using).
   - Simmer for about 10 minutes until the mixture thickens into a chutney-like consistency. Adjust seasoning to taste.

3. **Serve:**
   - Plate the kangaroo fillets and spoon the bush tomato chutney over or alongside them.
   - Enjoy with a side of roasted vegetables or a fresh salad.

This recipe highlights the unique flavors of kangaroo meat while complementing it with the tangy sweetness of the bush tomato chutney. Would you like any variations or additional sides to go with this dish?
