What it “does” can therefore be divided into two tracks:

--------------------------------------------------------
1. Espionage & intellectual-property theft (state tasking)
--------------------------------------------------------
• **Targets**:  
  – Health-care, telecom, high-tech, semiconductor, and gaming companies worldwide.  
  – Government bodies in the U.S., India, UK, Japan, Australia, Vietnam, Malaysia, and more.  
  – Dissenting communities (Tibetan, Uyghur, Hong-Kong, pro-democracy NGOs).

• **Objectives**:  
  – Steal source code, chip designs, COVID-19 vaccine data, 5G plans, and diplomatic cables.  
  – Maintain long-term persistence inside victim networks to feed Beijing’s technology-transfer and strategic-collection requirements.

• **TTPs in this track**:  
  – Supply-chain compromises (CCleaner, ASUS Live Update, tax-software in Vietnam, etc.).  
  – Zero-day use (Citrix, Cisco, Zoho, Log4j, etc.).  
  – Living-off-the-land and custom backdoors (CROSSWALK, REDBAND, PHOTO, DUSTPAN, etc.).  
  – League-of-legends-style code-signing certificates stolen from game studios to sign implants.

--------------------------------------------------------
2. Criminal profit side (private enrichment)
--------------------------------------------------------
• **Monetisation**:  
  – Hijacking cryptocurrency wallets and point-of-sale systems.  
  – Attacking on-line game publishers to steal digital items/in-game currency, then selling them for real money.  
  – Crypto-jacking and ransomware experiments (see the 2020 “DarkCloud” ransomware used against an undisclosed university).

• **Overlap**:  
  – Same malware families (BLACKCOFFEE, PUPPYDOWNER, MoonWalk, etc.) appear both in IP-theft intrusions and cash-out jobs, proving the group’s hybrid nature.

--------------------------------------------------------
Typical attack sequence (both tracks)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. **Initial access**: Spear-phish with COVID-themed lures, exploit public-facing app (Citrix ADC, Exchange, Zoho) or seed tainted software updates.  
2. **Dropper**: A tiny first-stage (e.g., “LogInstall” MSI or bogus Flash update).  
3. **Implant**: Second-stage loader installs a backdoor of the day—CROSSWALK, SLOWDRIFT, or DEADEYE.  
4. **Recon & lateral**: Uses MimiKatz, ADFind, SMB/PSExec, living-off-land PowerShell, and ChinaChopper webshells.  
5. **Exfil**: RAR or 7-Zip staging into Dropbox or OneDrive; traffic tunnelled through SOCKS via compromised routers or VPS.  
6. **Profit or policy**: Either package stolen IP for Beijing handlers OR siphon game currency/BTC to personal wallets.

Key take-aways for defenders

• They hide C2 inside legitimate cloud services (Dropbox, GitHub, Tencent OSS, Baidu Pan, AWS WorkSpaces).  
Because of its dual mandate, an APT41 “game-coin” intrusion today can pivot into a semiconductor IP theft tomorrow—so any sign of their tooling merits immediate enterprise-wide hunt and eradication, not just isolated incident closure.
