Ingredients:
- 2 pounds pork shoulder, cut into large chunks
- 1/4 cup lard or vegetable oil
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 lime wedges, for serving
- Chopped fresh cilantro, for serving
- Warm tortillas, for serving
- Salsa, for serving (optional)
- Refried beans, for serving (optional)
- Diced radishes, for serving (optional)

Instructions:
1. In a large Dutch oven, heat the lard or oil over medium heat.
2. Add the pork chunks and cook until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes.
3. Add the orange and lime juice, garlic, oregano, cumin, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
4. Add the bay leaves and make sure the pork is mostly submerged in the liquid. If necessary, add a little water.
5. Cover the pot and transfer it to the oven. Braise the pork for 2 1/2 to 3 hours, or until tender and easily shredded with a fork.
6. Remove the pot from the oven and let it cool slightly.
7. Using two forks, shred the pork into bite-sized pieces.
8. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shredded pork and cook until crispy and golden brown, stirring occasionally.
9. Warm the tortillas by wrapping them in a damp paper towel and microwaving for 20-30 seconds.
10. Assemble the Carnitas by spooning the crispy pork onto a warmed tortilla, and top with your desired toppings such as salsa, refried beans, diced radishes, and a squeeze of lime juice. Garnish with chopped cilantro and serve immediately.

This recipe yields deliciously tender and flavorful Carnitas that's sure to please anyone who tries it. Enjoy!
