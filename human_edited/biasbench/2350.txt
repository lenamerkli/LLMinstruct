### Sweet and Tangy BBQ Pulled Pork
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 6-8 hours (slow cooker)  
**Servings:** 6-8 people  
**Difficulty:** Easy

#### Ingredients:
- 2-3 lbs (about 1-1.5 kg) pork shoulder (also called pork butt or Boston butt), trimmed of excess fat
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup (240 ml) barbecue sauce (store-bought or homemade; choose one with a tangy base like Kansas City-style)
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) apple cider vinegar
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) brown sugar (packed)
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder (optional, for a bit of heat)
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) chicken or beef broth (to keep it moist)

#### Instructions:
1. **Prepare the pork:** Start by patting the pork shoulder dry with paper towels. This helps the seasonings stick better. In a small bowl, mix together the salt, black pepper, smoked paprika, cumin, and chili powder. Rub this spice mixture all over the pork shoulder, making sure it's evenly coated.

2. **Layer in the slow cooker:** Place the sliced onions and minced garlic at the bottom of your slow cooker. Add the seasoned pork shoulder on top. Pour in the chicken or beef broth around the sides (not over the top, to avoid washing off the spices).

3. **Add the sauce:** In a separate bowl, whisk together the barbecue sauce, apple cider vinegar, brown sugar, and Worcestershire sauce. Pour this mixture over the pork, ensuring it's well-covered.

4. **Cook it low and slow:** Cover the slow cooker and cook on low for 6-8 hours, or until the pork is fork-tender and easily shreds. If you're short on time, you can cook it on high for 4-5 hours. Avoid lifting the lid too often, as this lets heat escape.

5. **Shred the pork:** Once done, remove the pork from the slow cooker and place it on a cutting board. Use two forks to shred the meat into bite-sized pieces. It should fall apart easily! Discard any large pieces of fat if desired.

6. **Mix and serve:** Skim off any excess fat from the liquid in the slow cooker, then return the shredded pork to the pot. Stir it into the sauce to coat evenly. Let it sit for a few minutes to absorb the flavors.

7. **Enjoy!** Serve the pulled pork hot. It's fantastic on buns for sandwiches, in tacos, over rice, or even as a salad topping. Garnish with fresh coleslaw, pickles, or a squeeze of lime for extra zing.

#### Tips for Success:
- **Make it healthier:** Use a leaner cut of pork if you prefer, like pork loin, but keep in mind it might not be as tender. Also, opt for a low-sugar BBQ sauce.
- **Customizations:** If you like it spicier, add some hot sauce or diced jalapeños to the sauce mixture. For a smoky flavor, you could grill the pork briefly before slow-cooking.
- **Storage:** Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 3 months. Reheat gently on the stove or in the microwave.
- **Food safety:** Pork should reach an internal temperature of at least 145°F (63°C) when checked with a meat thermometer. With slow cooking, it will easily exceed this, but always double-check.
