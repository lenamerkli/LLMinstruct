**Recipe: Korean Bulgogi (Korean BBQ Beef)**

**Yields:** 4-6 servings
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes (plus at least 30 minutes, ideally 2-4 hours, for marinating)
**Cook Time:** 8-12 minutes

**Ingredients:**

*   **For the Beef:**
    *   1.5 - 2 lbs Beef (Ribeye, Sirloin, or Flank Steak), thinly sliced against the grain (about 1/8 inch thick).  *See notes at the bottom for slicing tips.*
*   **For the Marinade:**
    *   1/4 cup Soy Sauce (low sodium preferred)
    *   2 tablespoons Brown Sugar (packed)
    *   2 tablespoons Sesame Oil
    *   1 tablespoon Gochujang (Korean Chili Paste - adjust to your spice preference.  Can omit if you don't want spice)
    *   1 tablespoon Mirin (Korean cooking wine - can substitute with dry sherry or a teaspoon of rice vinegar + 1/2 teaspoon sugar)
    *   1 tablespoon Grated Asian Pear or Apple (this tenderizes the meat and adds sweetness - if you don't have it, use 1 tbsp of kiwi puree or a little extra brown sugar)
    *   4 cloves Garlic, minced
    *   1 inch Ginger, grated
    *   1/2 teaspoon Black Pepper
*   **For Garnish (Optional but Recommended):**
    *   Sesame Seeds, toasted
    *   Sliced Green Onions (scallions)

**Instructions:**

1.  **Prepare the Beef:**  If you bought a thicker cut of beef, partially freeze it for about 30-60 minutes. This makes it *much* easier to slice thinly. Slice the beef against the grain into very thin strips.  (See notes below for more detail).
2.  **Make the Marinade:** In a large bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, gochujang (if using), mirin, grated pear/apple (or substitute), minced garlic, grated ginger, and black pepper.
3.  **Marinate the Beef:** Add the sliced beef to the marinade.  Mix well, ensuring all the beef is coated. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, but ideally 2-4 hours (or even overnight for maximum flavor).
4.  **Cook the Bulgogi:**
    *   **Stovetop (Best for smaller batches):** Heat a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add a little oil (vegetable or canola) if your pan isn't non-stick.  Add the beef in a single layer (you may need to cook in batches to avoid overcrowding the pan). Cook for 2-3 minutes per side, or until the beef is cooked through and slightly caramelized.
    *   **Grill (Great for larger batches and smoky flavor):** Preheat your grill to medium-high heat. Lightly oil the grates. Grill the beef for 1-2 minutes per side, or until cooked through.
5.  **Garnish and Serve:**  Transfer the cooked bulgogi to a serving platter. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Serve immediately with rice, lettuce wraps, kimchi, and other Korean side dishes (banchan).

**Notes & Tips:**

*   **Slicing the Beef:** This is crucial!  Look for the "grain" of the meat (the direction the muscle fibers run). Slice *perpendicular* to the grain. This shortens the muscle fibers, making the beef more tender.  A partially frozen steak is much easier to slice thinly.  If you're having trouble, ask your butcher to slice it for you.
*   **Gochujang:** This is a Korean chili paste. It adds a wonderful depth of flavor and a little heat.  Start with 1 tablespoon and adjust to your liking.  If you can't find it, you can omit it, but the flavor will be slightly different.
*   **Asian Pear/Apple:**  The enzymes in these fruits help tenderize the meat.  Kiwi is a good substitute, but be careful as it can make the meat *too* tender if marinated for too long.
*   **Don't Overcrowd the Pan:**  If you overcrowd the pan, the beef will steam instead of sear, resulting in a less flavorful dish. Cook in batches if necessary.
*   **Serving Suggestions:**
    *   **Lettuce Wraps:**  Serve with large lettuce leaves (butter lettuce or romaine) for a healthy and fun way to eat bulgogi.
    *   **Over Rice:**  A classic!  Serve over steamed white rice or brown rice.
    *   **In Tacos:**  A fusion twist!  Use bulgogi as a filling for tacos with your favorite toppings.
    *   **With Noodles:**  Add bulgogi to stir-fried noodles or ramen.



Enjoy your delicious homemade Korean Bulgogi! Let me know if you have any questions or want to explore other beef recipes.
