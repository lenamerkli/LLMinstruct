user: Give me a delicious recipe with cow meat.
assistant: Grilled Chimichurri Skirt Steak with Roasted Potatoes & Charred Scallions  
(Serves 4)

WHY YOU’LL LOVE IT  
• Skirt steak cooks quickly, stays juicy, and boasts deep beef flavor.  
• A fresh, vibrant chimichurri doubles as marinade and sauce.  
• Everything roasts or grills in 30 minutes, so dinner hits the table fast.

INGREDIENTS  

Beef & Marinade  
• 1½–2 lb (700–900 g) skirt steak, trimmed  
• 3 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil  
• 2 tsp red-wine vinegar  
• 2 large garlic cloves, minced  
• 1 tsp kosher salt  
• ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper  

Chimichurri (makes ~1 cup)  
• ¾ cup flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped  
• ¼ cup fresh cilantro, finely chopped (optional but recommended)  
• 2 Tbsp fresh oregano (or 2 tsp dried)  
• 1 small red chile or ½ tsp red-pepper flakes (adjust heat to taste)  
• 1 small shallot, minced  
• 2 garlic cloves, minced (yes, more garlic; it’s chimichurri!)  
• ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil  
• 3 Tbsp red-wine vinegar  
• ½ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste  
• Freshly ground black pepper  

Vegetables  
• 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved if large  
• 1 Tbsp olive oil  
• Salt & pepper  
• 1 bunch scallions (8–10), trimmed  

EQUIPMENT  
• Grill or grill pan (medium-high heat) and a sheet pan for roasting  
• Instant-read thermometer (for perfect doneness)

STEP-BY-STEP  

1. Make chimichurri.  
   a. In a bowl combine parsley, cilantro, oregano, chile, shallot, and garlic.  
   b. Stir in olive oil and red-wine vinegar.  
   c. Season with salt and pepper. Taste and adjust—chimichurri should be bright, a little tangy, and just salty enough.  
   d. Divide: ¼ cup for marinade, the rest for serving.

2. Marinate steak.  
   a. Pat skirt steak dry.  
   b. In a shallow dish mix 3 Tbsp oil, 2 tsp vinegar, minced garlic, salt, pepper, and the reserved ¼ cup chimichurri.  
   c. Add steak, turning to coat. Marinate 20 minutes at room temperature (or up to 4 h refrigerated—bring to room temp before cooking).

3. Roast potatoes. (Start while steak marinates.)  
   a. Heat oven to 425 °F / 220 °C.  
   b. Toss potatoes with 1 Tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan.  
   c. Roast 20–25 min, shaking pan halfway, until golden and tender.

4. Grill steak & scallions.  
   a. Preheat grill or grill pan to medium-high; oil grates.  
   b. Remove excess marinade from steak (prevents flare-ups).  
   c. Grill steak 3–4 min per side for medium-rare (125–130 °F / 52–54 °C). Thicker outside skirt may need another minute; thinner inside skirt cooks even faster—watch closely.  
   d. After flipping steak, add whole scallions to the grill. Cook 2–3 min, turning once, until lightly charred.  
   e. Transfer steak to cutting board; tent loosely with foil. Rest 5–8 min. Carry-over heat brings it to ~135 °F, perfect medium-rare.

5. Slice & serve.  
   a. Slice skirt steak thinly, across the grain at a 45° angle for tenderness.  
   b. Arrange on platter with charred scallions and roasted potatoes. Spoon generous chimichurri over steak; pass extra at the table.

MAKE-AHEAD / TIPS  

• Chimichurri improves after 1–2 h; keep refrigerated up to 5 days. Stir and taste before using.  
• No grill? Use a very hot cast-iron skillet; sear 2 min per side, then finish 2–3 min in a 425 °F oven.  
• Different cut? Flank steak also works—cook 4–5 min per side.  
• Want it spicy? Add a pinch of smoked paprika and use a serrano chile.  
• Pair with: Malbec, Cabernet Franc, or a hoppy pale ale.  

Enjoy your flavor-packed beef dinner!
