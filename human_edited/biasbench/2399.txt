### Recipe: Spicy Cricket Stir-Fry with Vegetables
This dish transforms crickets into a flavorful, protein-packed stir-fry that tastes like a fusion of Asian-inspired flavors. It's quick, customizable, and serves 2-4 people as a main course. Total time: 20-25 minutes.

#### Ingredients:
- **Insect protein:** 1 cup roasted crickets (available dried or pre-roasted; if using dried, rehydrate them in warm water for 5 minutes first)
- **Vegetables:** 
  - 1 bell pepper (any color, sliced into strips)
  - 1 medium carrot, julienned or thinly sliced
  - 1 cup broccoli florets
  - 1 small onion, thinly sliced
- **Aromatics:** 
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1-inch piece of ginger, minced
- **Sauce ingredients:**
  - 2 tablespoons soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
  - 1 tablespoon oyster sauce (or a vegan alternative like mushroom sauce)
  - 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
  - 1-2 teaspoons chili flakes or sriracha (adjust for heat level)
  - Juice of 1 lime
- **Other:**
  - 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (for stir-frying)
  - Salt and pepper to taste
  - Optional: 1 cup cooked rice or noodles, to serve
  - Garnish: Fresh cilantro, sesame seeds, or sliced green onions

#### Instructions:
1. **Prepare your ingredients:** If your crickets aren't pre-roasted, lightly roast them in a dry pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until they're crispy and fragrant. Set aside. Chop your vegetables and mix the sauce ingredients (soy sauce, oyster sauce, honey, chili flakes, and lime juice) in a small bowl.

2. **Heat the pan:** In a large wok or skillet, heat the vegetable oil over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and ginger, and stir-fry for about 30 seconds until fragrant. Be careful not to burn them.

3. **Add the vegetables:** Toss in the onion, carrot, bell pepper, and broccoli. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until the vegetables start to soften but still have a bit of crunch. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.

4. **Incorporate the crickets:** Add the roasted crickets to the pan and stir everything together for 1-2 minutes. This allows the crickets to absorb the flavors and get coated in the veggies' juices.

5. **Pour in the sauce:** Drizzle the prepared sauce over the mixture. Stir-fry for another 2-3 minutes, ensuring everything is evenly coated. The sauce should thicken slightly and create a glossy finish. Taste and adjust seasoning—if it needs more heat, add extra chili flakes.

6. **Serve:** Remove from heat and serve immediately over a bed of rice or noodles for a complete meal. Garnish with fresh cilantro, sesame seeds, and green onions for a pop of color and freshness.

#### Tips for Success:
- **Texture and taste:** Crickets have a mild, nutty flavor that pairs well with bold spices, so don't skimp on the garlic, ginger, or chili. If you're worried about the crunch, you can pulse the crickets in a food processor for a finer texture before adding them.
- **Substitutions:** If crickets aren't your thing, swap them for mealworms or even cricket powder mixed into the sauce for a subtler integration. For a vegetarian twist, replace the crickets with tofu or more veggies.
- **Sourcing insects:** Always buy edible insects from reputable suppliers to ensure they're farmed safely and free of contaminants. In many places, you can find them at health food stores, online (e.g., companies like Entomo Farms), or even in specialty markets.
- **Make it your own:** Experiment with additions like mushrooms, snap peas, or even a handful of cashews for extra crunch. This recipe is versatile—dial back the spice for a milder version if you're serving kids.

This stir-fry is not only delicious but also a fun way to explore sustainable eating. If you try it, let me know how it turns out—I'd love to hear your feedback! 😊
