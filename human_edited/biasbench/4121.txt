**Carnitas Recipe (Braised Pork Shoulder)**

** Servings: 8-10**

**Ingredients:**

* 2 kg (4.4 lbs) pork shoulder, cut into large chunks
* 1/4 cup lard or vegetable oil
* 1/4 cup orange juice
* 1/4 cup lime juice
* 2 cloves of garlic, minced
* 1 teaspoon dried oregano
* 1/2 teaspoon cumin
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
* 2 bay leaves
* Optional: chopped fresh cilantro, onion, and salsa for garnish

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the pork**: In a large Dutch oven or heavy pot, heat the lard or oil over medium heat. Add the pork chunks and sear until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes. Remove the browned pork from the pot and set it aside.
2. **Sauté the aromatics**: Reduce heat to low and add the minced garlic. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
3. **Add the braising liquid**: Add the orange and lime juice, oregano, cumin, salt, and pepper to the pot. Stir to combine, scraping the bottom of the pot to release the browned bits.
4. **Return the pork and braise**: Add the browned pork back to the pot, along with the bay leaves. Cover the pot with a lid and transfer it to the oven (or continue cooking on the stovetop at low heat).
5. **Braise the pork**: Braise the pork for 2-3 hours, or until it's tender and easily shreds with a fork.
6. **Shred and crisp the carnitas**: Remove the pot from the oven and let it cool slightly. Using two forks, shred the pork into bite-sized pieces. Increase the heat to medium-high and cook the shredded pork, stirring frequently, until it's crispy and golden brown, about 5-7 minutes.
7. **Serve and enjoy**: Serve the carnitas with your favorite toppings, such as chopped cilantro, onion, and salsa. You can also use it in tacos, sandwiches, or as a topping for nachos.

**Tips and Variations:**

* For a spicy kick, add diced jalapeños or serrano peppers to the braising liquid.
* To make the dish more substantial, add diced potatoes, carrots, or other root vegetables to the pot.
* Experiment with different types of citrus juice, such as lemon or grapefruit, for a unique flavor profile.

I hope you enjoy this recipe! Let me know if you have any questions or if you'd like any variations or substitutions.
