**Recipe: Pan-Seared Kangaroo with Mushroom & Red Wine Sauce**

This recipe serves 2.

**Ingredients:**

*   **For the Kangaroo:**
    *   2 Kangaroo Steaks (around 150-200g each), about 1 inch thick (Sirloin or Rump are good cuts)
    *   1 tbsp Olive Oil
    *   1 tbsp Butter
    *   Salt and freshly ground Black Pepper to taste
    *   1 tsp Dried Rosemary (or 1 tbsp fresh, chopped)
    *   1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
*   **For the Mushroom & Red Wine Sauce:**
    *   1 tbsp Olive Oil
    *   1 Shallot, finely chopped
    *   2 cloves Garlic, minced
    *   250g Mixed Mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, oyster – a mix is great!), sliced
    *   125ml Dry Red Wine (e.g., Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot)
    *   125ml Beef Stock (low sodium)
    *   1 tbsp Worcestershire Sauce
    *   1 tbsp Butter, cold and cubed
    *   1 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)

**Instructions:**

1.  **Prepare the Kangaroo:**
    *   Take the kangaroo steaks out of the refrigerator about 30 minutes before cooking to allow them to come to room temperature. This helps with even cooking.
    *   Pat the steaks dry with paper towels. This is crucial for a good sear.
    *   Season generously with salt, pepper, rosemary, and garlic powder.

2.  **Sear the Kangaroo:**
    *   Heat the olive oil and butter in a heavy-bottomed skillet (cast iron is ideal) over medium-high heat until the butter is melted and the pan is very hot.
    *   Carefully place the kangaroo steaks in the hot pan.  Don't overcrowd the pan; cook in batches if necessary.
    *   Sear for 2-3 minutes per side for medium-rare, or longer depending on your desired doneness.  *Important:* Don't move the steaks around while they're searing; let them develop a good crust.
    *   Use a meat thermometer to check the internal temperature:
        *   Medium-Rare: 55-60°C (130-140°F)
        *   Medium: 60-65°C (140-150°F)
    *   Remove the steaks from the pan and set aside on a plate, tented with foil to rest for at least 5-10 minutes.  Resting allows the juices to redistribute, resulting in a more tender and flavorful steak.

3.  **Make the Mushroom & Red Wine Sauce:**
    *   Reduce the heat to medium.  Add the olive oil to the same skillet (don't wipe it out – those browned bits are flavor!).
    *   Add the chopped shallot and cook until softened, about 3-4 minutes.
    *   Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
    *   Add the sliced mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, until they release their moisture and start to brown, about 5-7 minutes.
    *   Pour in the red wine and bring to a simmer, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.  Let the wine reduce by about half, about 3-5 minutes.
    *   Add the beef stock and Worcestershire sauce.  Bring back to a simmer and cook until the sauce has slightly thickened, about 5 minutes.
    *   Remove the skillet from the heat.  Whisk in the cold, cubed butter, one cube at a time, until the sauce is smooth and glossy.  This is called "monter au beurre" and adds richness and shine.
    *   Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed.

4.  **Serve:**
    *   Slice the rested kangaroo steaks against the grain.
    *   Divide the sliced kangaroo between two plates.
    *   Spoon the mushroom and red wine sauce generously over the kangaroo.
    *   Garnish with fresh chopped parsley.

**Serving Suggestions:**

*   **Sides:**  Mashed potatoes, roasted vegetables (especially root vegetables like carrots and parsnips), creamy polenta, or a simple green salad.
*   **Wine Pairing:**  The same red wine you used in the sauce (Cabernet Sauvignon or Merlot) would be a great pairing.

**Tips & Notes:**

*   **Sourcing Kangaroo:**  Kangaroo meat can be found at specialty butcher shops, online retailers, or sometimes in larger supermarkets.
*   **Don't Overcook:**  Kangaroo can become tough if overcooked.  Err on the side of undercooking rather than overcooking.
*   **Marinating (Optional):**  If you want to tenderize the meat further or add more flavor, you can marinate the kangaroo steaks for 30 minutes to a few hours before cooking.  A marinade with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs would work well.
*   **Spice it up:** Add a pinch of chili flakes to the sauce for a little heat.



Enjoy your delicious kangaroo meal! Let me know if you have any questions or want to explore other kangaroo recipes.
