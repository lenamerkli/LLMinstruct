**Crispy Pork Carnitas Tacos with Salsa and Avocado**

**Ingredients:**

For the pork:

* 2 pounds pork shoulder, cut into large chunks
* 1/4 cup lard or vegetable oil
* 1/4 cup orange juice
* 1/4 cup lime juice
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 teaspoon dried oregano
* Salt and pepper, to taste

For the salsa:

* 1 cup diced fresh tomatoes
* 1/2 cup diced red onion
* 1/4 cup diced fresh cilantro
* 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and finely chopped
* 2 tablespoons lime juice
* Salt, to taste

For the tacos:

* 8-10 corn tortillas
* Avocado, diced
* Chopped cilantro, for garnish
* Lime wedges, for serving

**Instructions:**

1. **Make the pork:** In a large Dutch oven, heat the lard or oil over medium heat. Add the pork and cook until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes. Add the orange and lime juice, garlic, and oregano. Cover the pot and transfer it to the oven. Braise the pork for 2-3 hours, or until tender and easily shredded with a fork.
2. **Shred the pork:** Remove the pot from the oven and let it cool slightly. Shred the pork with two forks and return it to the pot. Add more orange and lime juice if needed, and season with salt and pepper.
3. **Make the salsa:** In a medium bowl, combine the diced tomatoes, red onion, cilantro, jalapeño pepper, and lime juice. Season with salt to taste.
4. **Assemble the tacos:** Warm the tortillas by wrapping them in a damp paper towel and microwaving for 20-30 seconds. Spoon some of the pork onto a tortilla, followed by a spoonful of salsa, some diced avocado, and a sprinkle of cilantro. Serve with lime wedges on the side.

**Enjoy your delicious pork carnitas tacos!**

This recipe is a classic Mexican dish that's perfect for a special occasion or a weekend dinner. The slow-cooked pork is tender and flavorful, and the salsa adds a fresh and spicy kick. I hope you enjoy it!
