### **Attack Methods & Tradecraft**
1. **Initial Access:**
   - Exploiting **public-facing vulnerabilities** (e.g., **Exchange, VPNs, RDP**).
   - **Spear-phishing** (malicious emails with lures like job offers or COVID-19 info).
   - **Supply-chain compromises** (hacking software vendors to distribute malware).

2. **Persistence & Lateral Movement:**
   - **DLL hijacking** (e.g., replacing legitimate Windows files with malware).
   - **Scheduled tasks & services** for long-term access.
   - **Pass-the-hash & Kerberoasting** for credential theft.

3. **Data Exfiltration:**
   - **Encrypted channels** (HTTPS, DNS tunneling).
   - **Cloud storage abuse** (Dropbox, Google Drive).
   - **Staging data in temporary locations** before exfiltration.

4. **Evasion Techniques:**
   - **Fileless malware** (executing payloads in memory).
   - **Polymorphic code** (changing malware signatures to avoid detection).
   - **Legitimate tools** (e.g., **PsExec, Cobalt Strike**) to blend in.
