### **Classic Beef Bourguignon**

#### **Ingredients:**
- **1.5 kg (3.3 lbs) beef chuck**, cut into large cubes
- **200 g (7 oz) bacon**, diced
- **2 large onions**, chopped
- **3 carrots**, chopped
- **4 cloves garlic**, minced
- **2 tbsp tomato paste**
- **300 ml (1 ¼ cups) red wine** (Burgundy if possible)
- **300 ml (1 ¼ cups) beef stock**
- **2 tbsp flour**
- **2 tbsp olive oil**
- **2 tbsp butter**
- **200 g (7 oz) mushrooms**, cleaned and quartered
- **20 pearl onions**, peeled
- **2 tbsp parsley**, chopped (for garnish)
- **Salt & pepper**, to taste

#### **Instructions:**

1. **Brown the Meat:**
   - In a large Dutch oven or heavy pot, heat 1 tbsp of olive oil over medium heat.
   - Season the beef cubes generously with salt and pepper.
   - Brown the beef in batches (don’t overcrowd the pot) until nicely seared on all sides. Remove and set aside.

2. **Sauté the Bacon and Vegetables:**
   - In the same pot, add the remaining oil and butter.
   - Cook the diced bacon until crispy. Remove half and set aside.
   - Add the onions and carrots, cooking until softened (about 5-7 minutes).
   - Add the garlic and tomato paste, stirring for 1 minute.

3. **Deglaze and Simmer:**
   - Pour in the red wine, scraping the bottom of the pot to release any browned bits.
   - Stir in the flour to thicken the liquid slightly.
   - Return the beef and bacon to the pot. Add the beef stock.
   - Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook on low heat for **2 to 2.5 hours** (or until beef is tender).

4. **Add Mushrooms and Pearl Onions:**
   - In a separate pan, sauté the mushrooms and pearl onions in a bit of butter until golden.
   - Add them to the stew and simmer for an additional **30 minutes**.

6. **Serve:**
   - Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
   - Garnish with fresh parsley.

Enjoy this rich and flavorful dish with mashed potatoes, crusty bread, or boiled potatoes!
