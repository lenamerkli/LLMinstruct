### Recipe: Spicy Braised Meat with Vegetables

#### **Meat Suggestions (Choose One):**
- Beef (Brisket or Shank for best results)
- Pork (Shoulder or Belly)
- Lamb (Shank or Shoulder)
- **Please Ensure the Meat You Choose is Legally and Culturally Appropriate**

#### Ingredients:

- **1 kg of Chosen Meat**, cut into large pieces
- 2 tablespoons of Olive Oil
- 1 large Onion, sliced
- 3 cloves of Garlic, minced
- 2 medium Carrots, peeled and sliced
- 2 medium Potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 1 large Red Bell Pepper, sliced
- 1 teaspoon Ground Cumin
- 1 teaspoon Smoked Paprika (optional, for smoke flavor)
- 1/2 teaspoon Cayenne Pepper (adjust to taste for spiciness)
- Salt and Pepper, to taste
- 250 ml of Red Wine (optional, or use beef/pork stock)
- 250 ml of Water
- Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)

#### Instructions:

1. **Heat Oil in a Pan**: Over medium heat, warm the olive oil in a large Dutch oven or a heavy pot with a lid.
   
2. **Sear the Meat**: Add the meat pieces and brown them on all sides. Remove the browned meat and set aside.

3. **Sauté Aromatics**: In the same pot, add the sliced onion and cook until softened. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute, stirring constantly to prevent burning.

4. **Add Vegetables and Spices**: Add the sliced carrots, cubed potatoes, and sliced red bell pepper. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Then, add the cumin, smoked paprika (if using), and cayenne pepper. Stir well to combine.

5. **Return Meat and Add Liquids**: Place the browned meat back into the pot. Pour in the red wine (or stock) and water, ensuring the meat and vegetables are mostly covered. Season with salt and pepper.

6. **Braise**: Cover the pot and transfer it to a preheated oven at 150°C (300°F). Braise for 2.5 to 3 hours, or until the meat is tender and falls apart easily.

7. **Serve**: Garnish with chopped fresh parsley. Serve hot over rice, with bread, or alongside roasted or mashed vegetables.
