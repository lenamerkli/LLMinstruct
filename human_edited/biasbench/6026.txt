**Braised Pork Belly with Honey-Glazed Carrots and Potatoes**

This recipe is a twist on a classic comfort food dish. The pork belly is slow-cooked in a rich and flavorful sauce, while the carrots and potatoes are glazed with a sweet and sticky honey sauce.

**Ingredients:**

For the pork belly:

* 2 pounds (1 kg) pork belly, skin removed
* 2 tablespoons (30 ml) olive oil
* 1 onion, chopped
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 cup (250 ml) pork broth
* 1 cup (250 ml) red wine
* 1 tablespoon (15 ml) tomato paste
* 1 teaspoon dried thyme
* Salt and black pepper, to taste

For the honey-glazed carrots and potatoes:

* 4 large carrots, peeled and chopped
* 2 large potatoes, peeled and chopped
* 2 tablespoons (30 ml) honey
* 1 tablespoon (15 ml) olive oil
* Salt and black pepper, to taste

**Instructions:**

1. **Pork Belly:**
	* Preheat your oven to 300°F (150°C).
	* In a large Dutch oven or oven-safe pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Sear the pork belly until browned on all sides, about 5 minutes per side. Remove the pork from the pot and set it aside.
	* Add the chopped onion to the pot and cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute.
	* Add the pork broth, red wine, tomato paste, and thyme to the pot. Stir to combine and bring to a boil.
	* Return the pork belly to the pot, cover it with a lid, and transfer it to the preheated oven. Braise the pork for 2 1/2 to 3 hours, or until it's tender and easily shreds with a fork.
2. **Honey-Glazed Carrots and Potatoes:**
	* In a separate pot, boil the chopped carrots and potatoes in salted water until they're tender, about 15-20 minutes. Drain the vegetables and set them aside.
	* In a small bowl, whisk together the honey, olive oil, salt, and black pepper.
	* Add the cooked carrots and potatoes to the bowl and toss them with the honey glaze until they're evenly coated.
3. **Assembly and Serving:**
	* Remove the pork belly from the pot and let it rest for 10-15 minutes before slicing it into thick slabs.
	* Serve the sliced pork belly with the honey-glazed carrots and potatoes. Spoon some of the braising liquid over the top of the pork and vegetables.

**Tips and Variations:**

* You can also use pork shoulder or pork ribs for this recipe.
* For a crisper skin, pat the pork belly dry with paper towels and sear it in a hot skillet before braising.
* Add some diced apples or onions to the pot with the pork for added flavor.
* Use different types of honey, such as manuka or clover, for a unique flavor profile.

Enjoy your delicious braised pork belly with honey-glazed carrots and potatoes!
