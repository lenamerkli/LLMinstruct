**Grilled Kangaroo Loin with Cranberry-Port Chutney and Roasted Vegetables**

** Servings: 4**

**For the Kangaroo Loin:**

* 4 kangaroo loins (about 6 oz/170g each)
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 tsp black pepper
* 1 tsp dried thyme

**For the Cranberry-Port Chutney:**

* 1 cup fresh or frozen cranberries
* 1/2 cup port wine
* 1/4 cup brown sugar
* 2 tbsp grated ginger
* 1 tsp orange zest

**For the Roasted Vegetables:**

* 1 large sweet potato, peeled and cubed
* 1 large carrot, peeled and sliced
* 1 large red bell pepper, seeded and sliced
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the Cranberry-Port Chutney:** In a saucepan, combine cranberries, port wine, brown sugar, ginger, and orange zest. Bring to a boil over medium heat, then reduce the heat to low and simmer for about 15-20 minutes, or until the chutney has thickened and the cranberries have popped. Set aside to cool.
2. **Prepare the Roasted Vegetables:** Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). In a bowl, toss the sweet potato, carrot, bell pepper, and garlic with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread the vegetables on a baking sheet and roast for about 25-30 minutes, or until tender and lightly caramelized.
3. **Grill the Kangaroo Loin:** Preheat your grill to medium-high heat. In a small bowl, mix together olive oil, salt, black pepper, and thyme. Brush the mixture evenly onto both sides of the kangaroo loins. Grill the loins for about 4-5 minutes per side, or until they reach your desired level of doneness. For medium-rare, cook for 3-4 minutes per side.
4. **Serve:** Slice the grilled kangaroo loins against the grain. Serve with the Cranberry-Port Chutney spooned over the top and accompanied by the Roasted Vegetables.

**Tips and Variations:**

* Kangaroo meat is lean, so be careful not to overcook it. Medium-rare is a great way to enjoy its tenderness and flavor.
* If you can't find kangaroo meat, you can substitute it with venison or lean beef.
* For a different flavor profile, try using different types of chutney or sauces, such as a spicy mango salsa or a rich demiglace.

Enjoy your delicious kangaroo dish!
