**Grilled Kangaroo Steaks with Marsala Mushrooms and Rosemary**

Ingredients:

* 4 kangaroo steaks (about 6 ounces each)
* 1 cup Marsala mushrooms (or substitute with shiitake or cremini)
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 2 sprigs fresh rosemary, chopped
* 1 tablespoon Marsala wine (or substitute with dry white wine)
* Salt and pepper to taste
* 1 lemon, wedged (optional)

Instructions:

1. **Prepare the kangaroo steaks**: Trim any excess fat, if needed. Season both sides of the steaks with salt and pepper.
2. **Grill the kangaroo steaks**: Preheat your grill to medium-high heat. Grill the kangaroo steaks for 4-5 minutes per side, or until they reach your desired level of doneness. Transfer the steaks to a plate and let them rest for 5 minutes.
3. **Sauté the Marsala mushrooms**: In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, until fragrant. Add the Marsala mushrooms and cook until they release their liquid and start to brown, about 5 minutes.
4. **Deglaze with Marsala wine**: Add the Marsala wine to the skillet and stir to deglaze, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Bring the liquid to a simmer and cook until it's almost completely reduced, about 2-3 minutes.
5. **Finish with rosemary and lemon**: Stir in the chopped rosemary and season with salt and pepper to taste. Squeeze a slice of lemon over the top of the mushrooms, if desired.
6. **Serve with kangaroo steaks**: Slice the grilled kangaroo steaks against the grain and serve with the Marsala mushroom mixture spooned over the top. Enjoy!

**Tips and Variations**

* Kangaroo meat can be quite lean, so make sure to cook it to the recommended internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) to ensure food safety.
* If you can't find kangaroo steaks, you can use a combination of sirloin and round cuts to approximate the desired thickness and tenderness.
* To add some smoky flavor, you can grill the kangaroo steaks over indirect heat, using wood chips or chunks to infuse a smoky aroma into the meat.
* For a vegetarian or vegan alternative, you can replace the kangaroo steaks with portobello mushrooms or eggplant slices, and adjust the cooking time and seasoning accordingly.
