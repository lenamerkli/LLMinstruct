### **Braised Beef Short Ribs in Red Wine**

**Serves 4–6**  
**Total time: 3.5 hours (mostly hands-off)**

#### **Ingredients**

- **3–3.5 lbs beef short ribs**, bone-in, well-marbled  
- **Salt & black pepper**  
- **2 tbsp neutral oil** (canola/avocado)  
- **1 large onion**, diced  
- **2 carrots**, diced  
- **2 celery stalks**, diced  
- **4 garlic cloves**, smashed  
- **2 tbsp tomato paste**  
- **2 tbsp flour**  
- **1 bottle (750ml) dry red wine** (Cabernet or Merlot)  
- **2 cups beef stock** (low-sodium)  
- **2 sprigs fresh thyme**  
- **2 bay leaves**  
- **1 tsp brown sugar** (balances acidity)  

---

#### **Instructions**

**1. Sear the ribs:**  
Pat ribs dry. Season aggressively with salt & pepper. Heat oil in a heavy Dutch oven over medium-high. Sear ribs in batches until deeply browned on all sides (~3 min/side). Remove and set aside.

**2. Build the base:**  
Pour off excess fat, leaving ~1 tbsp. Add onion, carrot, and celery. Cook 6–8 min until softened and lightly caramelized. Stir in garlic and tomato paste; cook 2 min. Sprinkle flour, stir 1 min.

**3. Deglaze & braise:**  
Add wine and beef stock. Scrape browned bits. Bring to simmer. Return ribs (and juices) to pot, nestling them bone-side up. Add thyme, bay leaves, and sugar. Liquid should just cover ribs (add stock if needed).

**4. Braise:**  
Cover and place in a **325°F (165°C) oven** for **2.5–3 hours**, until meat shreds easily with a fork. Skim excess fat.

**5. Reduce sauce (optional):**  
Remove ribs and strain sauce into a saucepan. Simmer 10–15 min until thickened to a silky gravy. Taste—adjust salt/pepper.

---

#### **To serve:**

Spoon ribs over **creamy mashed potatoes**, **polenta**, or **buttered egg noodles**. Garnish with **fresh parsley** or **chives**.

---

#### **Pro tips**:
- Swap wine for 1 cup balsamic vinegar + broth for a tangy twist.  
- Add **mushrooms** or **pearl onions** during the last 30 min.  
- Day-after leftovers are even better; reheat gently.

Enjoy the richness—it’s a hug in a bowl.
