### **Beef and Vegetable Stew**  
**Serves:** 4–6  
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 2 hours  

---

#### **Ingredients:**  
- 2 tbsp olive oil or vegetable oil  
- 1 large onion, chopped  
- 3 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves or 1 tsp dried thyme  
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional, for depth)  
- 2 tsp tomato paste  
- 4–5 lbs beef chuck or stewing beef (cubed into 1–2 inch pieces)  
- 4 potatoes, peeled and cubed  
- 3 carrots, sliced into rounds  
- 2 celery stalks, sliced  
- 1 cup pearl onions or small white onions (optional)  
- 4–5 cups beef broth (low-sodium or vegetable broth for a lighter version)  
- 1 cup tomatoes (canned crushed or fresh, diced)  
- 1 bay leaf  
- Salt and black pepper to taste  
- Fresh parsley or thyme for garnish (optional)  

---

#### **Instructions:**  

1. **Brown the beef:**  
   - Heat oil in a large Dutch oven or pot over medium-high heat. Add beef in batches and sear until browned on all sides (about 5–7 minutes per batch). Remove beef and set aside.  

2. **Sauté aromatics:**  
   - In the same pot, add onion, garlic, thyme, paprika, and tomato paste. Cook for 5 minutes until fragrant.  

3. **Build the stew:**  
   - Return beef to the pot. Add potatoes, carrots, celery, pearl onions, broth, and tomatoes. Stir in bay leaf, salt, and pepper.  
   - Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 1.5–2 hours, until beef is fork-tender and vegetables are soft.  
   - (Optional: Add a splash of red wine or 1–2 tbsp soy sauce for extra flavor.)  

4. **Adjust and serve:**  
   - Taste and adjust seasoning. Remove bay leaf before serving.  
   - Ladle into bowls. Garnish with fresh herbs if desired. Serve with crusty bread or rice.  

---

### **Why It’s Delicious:**  
- The beef becomes incredibly tender through slow cooking.  
- Vegetables add sweetness and texture.  
- The combination of thyme, paprika, and broth creates a rich, savory base.  

Let me know if you’d like a variation (e.g., spicy, creamy, or one-pot adaptation)! 😊
