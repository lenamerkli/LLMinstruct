user: Give me a delicious recipe with pig meat.
assistant: Crispy Oven Carnitas (slow-braised, shredded pork — great for tacos, bowls, sandwiches)

Ingredients (4–6 servings)
- 3–4 lb (1.4–1.8 kg) pork shoulder/pork butt, trimmed of very large exterior fat (leave some fat)
- 1 tbsp kosher salt (about 1½ tsp table salt) plus extra for finishing
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp chili powder (or paprika + chipotle powder if you like heat)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne or crushed red pepper (optional)
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 large yellow onion, quartered
- 1 large orange, juiced (reserve halves)
- 2 limes, juiced
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken stock or water
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 tbsp neutral oil (vegetable or canola) for searing

Finishing & serving
- Warm corn or flour tortillas, chopped white onion, chopped cilantro, lime wedges, salsa, avocado, pickled red onion, cotija cheese (optional)

Method
1. Preheat oven to 325°F (163°C).
2. Make the rub: mix salt, pepper, chili powder, cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, cayenne (if using), and brown sugar. Pat the pork dry and rub the spice mix all over the meat.
3. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven or ovenproof pot over medium-high heat. Sear the pork 3–4 minutes per side until nicely browned (you can skip searing but browning adds flavor).
4. Add the smashed garlic, onion quarters, bay leaves, orange halves (used), orange and lime juice, and chicken stock to the pot. The liquid should come up about 1–2 inches on the meat — it doesn’t need to cover it.
5. Cover and transfer to the oven. Braise 2½–3½ hours, until pork is very tender and pulls apart easily with a fork. Internal temp for shreddable pork is around 195–205°F (90–96°C).
6. Remove pork to a cutting board and let rest 10 minutes. Strain the braising liquid into a saucepan, skim off excess fat, and simmer to reduce it to a thicker sauce (optional, about 5–10 minutes).
7. Shred the pork with two forks, discarding any very large pieces of fat and the bay leaves. Toss the shredded pork with a few tablespoons of the reduced braising liquid to keep it juicy.
8. To get the signature crispy edges: spread the shredded pork in an even layer on a rimmed baking sheet. Broil on high 5–8 minutes until the top gets browned and crispy; toss and broil another 3–5 minutes so other edges crisp. Watch carefully to avoid burning.
9. Serve hot in warmed tortillas with chopped onion, cilantro, a squeeze of lime, and salsa. Great also over rice bowls, in tortas, or with roasted veggies.

Quick slow-cooker alternative
- After searing, put everything in a slow cooker, cook on LOW 8–9 hours (or HIGH 4–5) until shreddable. Reduce braising liquid on stovetop and crisp under the broiler as above.

Storage
- Refrigerate up to 4 days in an airtight container. Freeze up to 3 months (thaw overnight in fridge).

Tips
- Pork shoulder has the right marbling for shredding; leaner cuts will be drier.
- Browning + reducing the braising liquid are the two steps that most boost flavor.
- If you like more spice, add a chopped chipotle in adobo or a teaspoon of adobo sauce to the braising liquid.
