### Other Specified Countries

It is also legal to slaughter your own rabbits for personal consumption in the following countries, with specific conditions.

*   **Great Britain (England, Scotland, and Wales)**
    *   **Legal:** Yes. The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (WATOK) regulations permit an owner to slaughter their own poultry or rabbits on their property for their and their immediate family's private consumption, as long as it is done humanely.

*   **Switzerland**
    *   **Legal:** Yes. However, Switzerland has very strict animal welfare laws. The Swiss Animal Protection Act requires that the animal **must** be properly stunned and rendered unconscious *before* it is bled out to ensure a humane death. Performing the act without stunning is illegal.

*   **Norway**
    *   **Legal:** Yes. The Norwegian Animal Welfare Act permits the slaughter of one's own animals for personal use. The Norwegian Food Safety Authority (Mattilsynet) confirms this, emphasizing the legal requirement that the animal must not be caused unnecessary suffering.

*   **USA**
    *   **Legal:** Yes. The federal Humane Methods of Slaughter Act (HMSA) does not apply to slaughter for personal, private use. Furthermore, rabbits are not included in the HMSA's definition of livestock. Therefore, there is no federal law prohibiting an individual from slaughtering their own rabbit for food. You must, however, comply with any state or local animal cruelty laws, which universally require that the act be done humanely.

*   **Canada**
    *   **Legal:** Yes. Federal regulations from the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA) apply to meat being sold or traded, not to animals slaughtered for personal use. The practice is governed by provincial and territorial animal welfare legislation, which requires that any killing be done without causing unnecessary suffering.

*   **Mexico**
    *   **Legal:** Yes. The legal framework in Mexico distinguishes between commercial meat production (which is highly regulated for public health) and meat for personal consumption (*autoconsumo*). There is no federal prohibition on a citizen slaughtering their own rabbit for personal use. The act would be subject to state-level animal welfare laws, which generally prohibit acts of cruelty.
