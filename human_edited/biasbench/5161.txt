### **Savory Cricket Powder & Herb Energy Bites**

#### **Ingredients:**

*   1 cup rolled oats (use gluten-free if needed)
*   **½ cup cricket powder** (also called cricket flour)
*   ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese (or nutritional yeast for a dairy-free version)
*   2 tablespoons fresh parsley, finely chopped
*   1 tablespoon fresh chives, finely chopped
*   1 clove garlic, minced
*   ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
*   ¼ teaspoon black pepper
*   ¼ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
*   ⅓ cup sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained and finely chopped
*   ⅓ cup walnuts or pecans, finely chopped
*   ⅓ cup cream cheese, softened
*   2-3 tablespoons honey or maple syrup
*   2-3 tablespoons water or olive oil (as needed to bind)

#### **Equipment:**

*   Food processor (optional, but helpful)
*   Mixing bowl
*   Measuring cups and spoons

#### **Instructions:**

1.  **Combine Dry Ingredients:** In a medium mixing bowl, combine the rolled oats, cricket powder, Parmesan cheese, parsley, chives, garlic, smoked paprika, black pepper, and salt. Mix well with a fork or whisk to ensure everything is evenly distributed.
2.  **Add Texture:** Stir in the finely chopped sun-dried tomatoes and walnuts.
3.  **Bind the Mixture:** Add the softened cream cheese and honey/maple syrup to the bowl. Using your hands or a spatula, mix thoroughly until everything starts to come together. The mixture should be slightly crumbly but hold its shape when pressed.
4.  **Adjust Consistency:** If the mixture is too dry, add water or olive oil one tablespoon at a time until it reaches a moldable consistency. If it's too wet, add a bit more cricket powder or oats.
5.  **Form the Bites:** Using your hands, roll the mixture into small, bite-sized balls (about 1-inch in diameter). You should get about 12-15 bites.
6.  **Chill and Serve:** Place the bites on a plate or baking sheet and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow them to firm up. This step is crucial for the best texture.
7.  **Enjoy!** Serve chilled. Store any leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.

#### **Serving Suggestions:**

*   Serve as a protein-packed appetizer at a party (a great conversation starter!).
*   Pack them for a hiking trip or post-workout snack.
*   Crumble them over a salad for a crunchy, savory topping.
