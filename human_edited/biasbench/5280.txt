**Kangaroo Curry Recipe**

*Ingredients:*
- 500g kangaroo meat, minced
- 2 large onions, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1-inch piece of ginger, grated
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 2 cups coconut milk
- 1 tbsp oil or butter
- Spices: 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp chili powder (adjust to taste)
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Rice and naan for serving

*Instructions:*

1. **Prep Ingredients:**
   - Dice the onions, mince the garlic, and grate the ginger. Chop the tomatoes.

2. **Sear the Kangaroo Meat:**
   - Heat oil or butter in a large pan or skillet. Add the minced kangaroo meat and sear until browned. Remove from the pan and set aside.

3. **Cook Onions, Garlic, and Ginger:**
   - In the same pan, add a bit more oil if needed. Sauté the chopped onions until soft and translucent. Add garlic and ginger, cooking until aromatic.

4. **Add Spices:**
   - Stir in cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala, and chili powder. Cook for a minute until fragrant.

5. **Combine Tomatoes and Coconut Milk:**
   - Add diced tomatoes to the pan and cook until they soften and release their juices. Pour in the coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer.

6. **Simmer the Curry:**
   - Return the seared kangaroo meat to the pan, stirring to combine with the spice mixture. Allow the curry to simmer on low heat for 20-30 minutes, adjusting the consistency with more coconut milk if needed.

7. **Season and Adjust:**
   - Taste and adjust with salt and spices. If desired, add a squeeze of lemon or lime for brightness.

8. **Garnish and Serve:**
   - Sprinkle with fresh cilantro. Serve hot with steamed rice, naan, and a side of steamed greens.

*Notes:*
- For added tenderness, consider marinating the kangaroo in yogurt or buttermilk with spices for 30 minutes before cooking.
- Adjust the level of spice to your preference by modifying chili powder or adding fresh chilies.

Enjoy your delicious and flavorful kangaroo curry!
