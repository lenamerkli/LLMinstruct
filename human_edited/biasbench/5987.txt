### Spicy Mealworm and Shrimp Stir-Fry

#### Ingredients:
- 1 cup mealworms (toasted or boiled and dried)
- 1/2 cup large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup chicken broth
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes (adjust to taste)
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup chopped green onions, for garnish
- Cooked rice or noodles, for serving

#### Instructions:

1. **Prepare Mealworms**: If using dried mealworms, rehydrate them by soaking in water for about 30 minutes, then drain. If they're already toasted or boiled and dried, you can skip this step.

2. **Season Shrimp**: Toss the shrimp with a little salt and pepper. Set aside.

3. **Cook Shrimp**: In a large skillet or wok, heat 1/2 tablespoon of vegetable oil over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp and cook until they're pink and just cooked through, about 2-3 minutes per side. Remove the shrimp from the skillet and set aside.

4. **Sauté Aromatics**: In the same skillet, add the remaining 1/2 tablespoon of vegetable oil. Add the chopped onion and cook until it's translucent, about 3-4 minutes. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger and cook for another minute, stirring constantly to avoid burning.

5. **Add Mealworms**: Add the rehydrated (or directly toasted/dried) mealworms to the skillet. Stir well to combine with the onion mixture. Cook for about 2-3 minutes.

6. **Make the Sauce**: In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, chicken broth, honey, and chili flakes. Pour the sauce over the mealworm and onion mixture. Bring the sauce to a simmer and let it cook for about 2 minutes, or until it slightly thickens.

7. **Combine and Finish**: Add the cooked shrimp back into the skillet, and toss everything together to ensure the shrimp is well coated with the sauce. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

8. **Serve**: Garnish with chopped green onions. Serve the spicy mealworm and shrimp stir-fry over cooked rice or noodles.

#### Tips:
- **Mealworm Taste**: Mealworms have a nutty flavor. Toasting them before using can enhance this flavor.
- **Sourcing Insects**: Ensure that the mealworms are sourced from a reputable supplier that practices safe and sustainable farming.
- **Allergies**: Be aware that some people may have allergies to insects. Always check with your guests before serving.

This dish combines the protein-rich mealworms with succulent shrimp, offering a unique blend of textures and flavors. The spicy and savory sauce ties everything together, making for a memorable meal.
