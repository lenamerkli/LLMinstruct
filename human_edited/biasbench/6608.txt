Seared Kangaroo Fillet with Pepperberry–Red Wine–Blackberry Sauce, Sweet Potato Mash, and Charred Broccolini

Serves: 3–4
Prep: 15 minutes (plus 30 minutes to marinate)
Cook: 20 minutes

Ingredients
- Kangaroo
  - 600 g kangaroo loin/fillet or rump steaks (about 4 x 150 g / 5–6 oz each)
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  - 2 tsp chopped fresh rosemary (or 1 tsp dried)
  - 1 tsp lemon zest
  - 2 tsp crushed pepperberry (or coarsely ground black pepper)
  - 1/2 tsp fine salt

- Sauce
  - 1 small shallot, finely chopped
  - 1 small garlic clove, minced
  - 120 ml dry red wine (1/2 cup)
  - 180 ml beef/game stock (3/4 cup)
  - 1 heaped tbsp blackberry jam or 80 g fresh blackberries (about 1/2 cup)
  - 1 tsp red wine vinegar or balsamic
  - 1 tbsp cold unsalted butter
  - Pinch of salt and extra pepperberry/black pepper

- Sides (optional but great)
  - 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  - 1 bunch broccolini
  - Olive oil, salt

Method
1) Marinate the kangaroo
   - Pat steaks dry. Mix olive oil, garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, pepperberry, and salt; rub over steaks.
   - Marinate 30 minutes at room temp (or up to 2 hours in the fridge; bring to room temp before cooking).

2) Start sides
   - Sweet potato mash: Simmer cubes in salted water until tender (12–15 min). Drain, mash with a splash of olive oil or butter and a pinch of salt.
   - Broccolini: Toss with a little olive oil and salt.

3) Sear the steaks
   - Heat a heavy skillet over high until very hot. Lightly oil the pan.
   - Sear kangaroo 1.5–2 minutes per side, plus 20–30 seconds on edges. Aim for rare to medium-rare: 52–55°C (125–130°F) in the thickest part. Don’t overcook—kangaroo is very lean.
   - Transfer to a warm plate, tent loosely with foil, and rest 5–10 minutes.

4) Make the sauce
   - In the same pan over medium heat, add a drizzle of oil if needed and sauté shallot 1 minute; add garlic for 10 seconds.
   - Deglaze with red wine, scraping browned bits. Reduce by half.
   - Add stock and blackberry jam/berries; simmer 3–4 minutes until syrupy.
   - Stir in vinegar, then whisk in cold butter off the heat. Season to taste.

5) Finish sides
   - Char the broccolini in the hot skillet or under a broiler 2–3 minutes until bright green with a little color.

6) Serve
   - Slice kangaroo across the grain. Spoon sauce over, serve with sweet potato mash and charred broccolini. Sprinkle a little extra pepperberry or cracked pepper.

Tips and swaps
- No pepperberry? Use coarsely cracked black pepper plus a pinch of crushed juniper or allspice.
- No wine? Use pomegranate or blackcurrant juice; add an extra 1 tsp vinegar to balance.
- Grilling option: Sear over high direct heat 90 seconds per side; rest well.
- Doneness guide: Best at rare to medium-rare (52–57°C / 125–135°F). Beyond medium, it can turn dry and livery.

Flavor variations
- Bush tomato and wattleseed: Add 1 tsp ground wattleseed and 1 tsp bush tomato to the marinade; swap blackberry jam for 1 tsp bush tomato relish in the sauce.
- Peppercorn cream: Replace red wine/berries with 1/2 cup stock + 1/2 cup cream and 2 tsp crushed peppercorns; reduce until nappe-thick.
