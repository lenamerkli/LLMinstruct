user: Give me a delicious recipe with meat from insects.
assistant: Cricket & Herb Kofta Kebabs  
(adaptes perfectly to meal-worm or grasshopper “mince” as well)

Makes about 12 small skewers—enough for 3–4 people as a main served with flatbread and yogurt.

–––––––  
1. Gather

Dry goods  
• 1 cup (120 g) dry-roasted cricket powder OR very finely minced, dry-roasted whole crickets*  
• ½ cup fine bulgur wheat (quick-cooking)  
• 1 tsp sea salt  
• 1 tsp ground cumin  
• ½ tsp ground coriander  
• ½ tsp smoked paprika  
• ¼ tsp cayenne (optional)  
• ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper  

Fresh + wet  
• 1 small onion, grated on the fine side of a box grater (include the juices)  
• 2 cloves garlic, pasted with a pinch of salt  
• 1 packed Tbsp each finely chopped flat-leaf parsley and mint  
• 1 packed tsp finely chopped oregano (or ½ tsp dried)  
• 1 Tbsp tomato paste  
• 1 large egg, lightly beaten  
• Juice of ½ lemon  

To serve  
• Warm pita or flatbread  
• Quick tahini-yogurt sauce (½ cup yogurt + 2 Tbsp tahini + squeeze of lemon + pinch of salt)  
• Diced tomatoes, cucumbers, pickles, chili oil—whatever you love on kebabs.

*Note on crickets  
If you start with whole roasted crickets (store-bought or home-roasted for 8–10 min at 350 °F/175 °C until fragrant), cool completely and blitz in a spice grinder or small blender until the consistency of coarse breadcrumbs.

–––––––  
2. Hydrate the bulgur  
In a bowl pour ½ cup boiling water over the bulgur, cover, and let stand 10 min until soft and fluffy. Drain/press out any excess liquid.

–––––––  
3. Build the mix  
To the bulgur add cricket powder, salt, spices, herbs, onion (with its juice), garlic, tomato paste, lemon juice and egg. Mix with your hands for 1–2 minutes; the mixture should be cohesive and slightly tacky but not wet. If it feels crumbly, sprinkle in 1–2 tsp water. Refrigerate 15–20 min so flavors meld and the mix firms up.

–––––––  
4. Skewer & shape  
Lightly oil your hands. Divide into 12 portions and mold each around a 5-inch bamboo skewer (pre-soaked if you’ll grill) into a 2½-inch sausage shape.

–––––––  
5. Cook  
• Grill: Medium-high heat, 6–8 min total, rotating until browned and the internal hits 165 °F / 74 °C.  
• Stovetop: Heat 1 Tbsp oil in a cast-iron or nonstick pan. Sear 2 min per side until a crust forms, then cover and lower heat to finish cooking, ~4 min more.

–––––––  
6. Plate  
Serve sizzling with warm flatbread, tahini-yogurt, fresh veg and a final sprinkle of sumac or Aleppo pepper.

–––––––  
Taste notes  
Cricket powder lends a gentle nuttiness (think sunflower seed + mushroom) that plays beautifully with the herbs and spices. The texture is tender inside with a snap from the bulgur and browned crust—virtually indistinguishable from lamb kofta to most guests if you don’t tell them up front.

Bonus ideas  
• Swap half the crickets for minced roasted meal-worms for deeper “meatiness.”  
• Vegan? Sub the egg with 1 Tbsp chickpea flour + 2 extra tsp of water and bump tomato paste to 2 Tbsp.
