## Slow-Cooker Beef Bourguignon (Classic French Beef Stew)

This recipe is a classic for a reason! It's incredibly flavorful, comforting, and gets even better the longer it sits.  Using a slow cooker makes it super easy – you can have a gourmet meal with minimal active cooking time.

**Yields:** 6-8 servings
**Prep time:** 30-40 minutes
**Cook time:** 8-10 hours on low, or 4-5 hours on high

**Ingredients:**

* **Beef:** 3 lbs beef chuck, cut into 1.5-2 inch cubes (look for well-marbled chuck)
* **Bacon:** 6 oz thick-cut bacon, chopped
* **Vegetables:**
    * 1 large onion, chopped
    * 2 carrots, peeled and chopped
    * 2 celery stalks, chopped
    * 8 oz cremini mushrooms, quartered
    * 2 cloves garlic, minced
* **Liquid & Flavorings:**
    * 1 bottle (750ml) dry red wine (Burgundy is traditional, but Pinot Noir, Merlot, or Cabernet Sauvignon work well)
    * 2 cups beef broth (low sodium is good to control salt)
    * 2 tbsp tomato paste
    * 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
    * 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    * 1 bouquet garni (tied bundle of thyme, parsley, and bay leaf - about 4-6 sprigs of each) OR 1 tsp dried thyme, 1 tsp dried parsley, 1 bay leaf
    * 1/2 tsp black pepper
    * Salt to taste
* **For finishing (optional, but recommended):**
    * 1 tbsp butter
    * 1 tbsp olive oil
    * 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
    * 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar (adds a nice brightness)



**Instructions:**

1. **Sear the Bacon & Beef:** In a large skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until crisp. Remove bacon with a slotted spoon and set aside. Leave the bacon fat in the skillet.  Working in batches (don't overcrowd the pan!), sear the beef cubes in the bacon fat until browned on all sides. This is important for flavor development!  Remove beef and set aside.

2. **Sauté Vegetables:** Add onion, carrots, and celery to the skillet and cook until softened, about 5-7 minutes. Add garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.

3. **Bloom the Tomato Paste & Flour:** Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.  Sprinkle with flour and cook for another minute, stirring constantly. This creates a roux that will help thicken the sauce.

4. **Deglaze and Combine in Slow Cooker:**  Pour in about 1/2 cup of the red wine into the skillet and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan (this is called deglazing – contains a LOT of flavor!). 

5.  Transfer the seared beef, cooked bacon, sautéed vegetables, and deglazing liquid to your slow cooker.

6. **Add Liquids & Seasonings:** Pour in the remaining red wine and beef broth. Add Worcestershire sauce, pepper, and the bouquet garni (or dried herbs). Stir to combine.

7. **Slow Cook:** Cover and cook on low for 8-10 hours, or on high for 4-5 hours.  The beef should be fork-tender.

8. **Finish the Sauce (Optional but Recommended):**  Remove the bouquet garni (or bay leaf).  In a small skillet, melt butter with olive oil over medium heat. Add the quartered mushrooms and cook until browned and softened. Add the mushrooms to the slow cooker.

9. **Thicken and Adjust Seasoning:**  If the sauce isn't thick enough, you can remove about 1 cup of the liquid from the slow cooker and whisk it with 1 tbsp of cornstarch.  Pour this slurry back into the slow cooker and stir.  Cook for another 15-30 minutes on high to thicken.  Stir in the balsamic vinegar.  Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed.

10. **Serve:** Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot, traditionally over mashed potatoes, egg noodles, or crusty bread.




**Tips & Notes:**

* **Beef Quality:**  Chuck roast is best for slow cooking because it has a lot of connective tissue that breaks down into delicious gelatin, making the stew incredibly tender and flavorful.
* **Wine Choice:** Don't use a wine you wouldn't drink. The wine flavor will be prominent in the final dish.
* **Bouquet Garni:** If you can't find pre-made bouquets garni, you can easily make your own by tying sprigs of thyme, parsley, and a bay leaf together with kitchen twine.
* **Make Ahead:** Beef Bourguignon is even more delicious the next day! You can make it a day ahead and let the flavors meld.  Just reheat gently.
* **Variations:**  You could add pearl onions during the last hour of cooking for extra sweetness and texture.





Enjoy!  Let me know if you have any questions or want to explore other beef recipes.
