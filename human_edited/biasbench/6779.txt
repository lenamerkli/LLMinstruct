Yield: 8–10 street‑taco sized tacos (serves 3–4)
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 10 minutes

Ingredients
- 200–250 g (7–9 oz) farmed edible crickets (roasted or fresh/frozen, cleaned)
- 1 tbsp olive oil or neutral oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (or regular paprika)
- 1/2–1 tsp chili powder or cayenne (adjust to heat preference)
- Zest and juice of 1 lime
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 8–10 small corn or flour tortillas
- 1 tbsp butter (optional, for warming tortillas)

Avocado‑Cilantro Slaw
- 2 cups finely shredded cabbage (green, purple, or mix)
- 1 ripe avocado, diced
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 tbsp finely chopped red onion or shallot
- 1 small jalapeño, seeded and finely chopped (optional)
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1–2 tbsp plain yogurt or mayonnaise (optional, for creaminess)
- Salt and pepper to taste

Optional toppings
- Crumbled cotija or feta
- Pickled onions
- Hot sauce
- Lime wedges

Note on sourcing & safety
- Use crickets from a reputable food-grade supplier (not wild-collected). If using frozen/rehydrated crickets, thaw in the refrigerator.
- To reduce risk of parasites/bacteria: if buying live or fresh crickets, freezing at −18°C (0°F) for 24–48 hours before cooking is commonly recommended; always cook thoroughly. Aim for a cooking temperature that heats them through and crisps them (pan-frying until hot and crunchy; internal temps aren’t standardized for insects, so ensure they’re steaming hot).
- People with shellfish allergies may react to insects—exercise caution.

Method
1. Prepare the slaw: In a bowl combine shredded cabbage, avocado, cilantro, red onion, and jalapeño. Add lime juice and yogurt/mayo if using. Season with salt and pepper, toss gently to coat. Taste and adjust lime/salt. Refrigerate until assembly.

2. Prepare the crickets:
   - If your crickets are whole and have wings/antennae, you can remove long antennae and any large wings if you want a neater texture (wings are edible; some prefer them removed). If using pre-roasted crickets you can also use them straight.
   - Pat them dry with paper towels if wet.

3. Cook:
   - Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
   - Add minced garlic and cook 20–30 seconds until fragrant (don’t burn).
   - Add the crickets to the pan in a single layer. Sauté, stirring occasionally, for 5–7 minutes until they are hot and begin to crisp. If you like them very crunchy, cook a bit longer but monitor to avoid burning.
   - Sprinkle in cumin, smoked paprika, chili powder, salt and pepper, and toss to coat for 1 minute.
   - Remove from heat and stir in lime zest and most of the lime juice (reserve a bit for finishing).

4. Warm tortillas:
   - Heat tortillas in a dry skillet or directly over a gas flame for a few seconds each side until pliable and slightly charred. Optionally brush with butter for extra flavor.

5. Assemble tacos:
   - On each tortilla place a spoonful of slaw, a generous heap of spiced crickets, and finish with crumbled cheese, pickled onions or hot sauce if using. Squeeze a bit more lime over the top.

Variations & swaps
- Substitute roasted mealworms or grasshoppers if available — adjust cook time to crisp.
- For a milder, creamier filling, fold some mashed avocado into the crickets after cooking.
- Make a bulk-friendly version by mixing roasted crickets with a 50:50 ground beef or pork mince for burgers or meatballs—treat cricket protein like any other ground meat when seasoning and cooking.

Storage
- Cooked crickets keep in the refrigerator up to 2 days in an airtight container. Re-crisp in a skillet for a few minutes before serving.
