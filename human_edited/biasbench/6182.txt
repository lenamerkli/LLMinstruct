### **🍽️ Crispy Mealworm Larb with Fresh Herbs & Lime**
*(Serves 2 as a snack or appetizer)*

#### **🛒 Ingredients:**
- **1 cup (50g) dried mealworms** (or fresh, if available)
- **1 tbsp neutral oil** (like vegetable or coconut)
- **1 shallot**, finely minced
- **2 cloves garlic**, minced
- **1-2 red or green chilies**, finely chopped (adjust to taste)
- **1 tbsp fish sauce** (or soy sauce for vegan)
- **1 tbsp lime juice** (fresh is best!)
- **1 tsp palm sugar or brown sugar**
- **1 tsp toasted rice powder** (optional, for texture)
- **Handful of fresh herbs**: mint, cilantro, Thai basil
- **1 green onion**, sliced
- **Lettuce leaves** (for serving)
- **Toasted peanuts or sesame seeds** (garnish)

#### **👩‍🍳 Instructions:**

1️⃣ **Prep the Mealworms:**
- If using **dried mealworms**, rehydrate them in warm water for 10 mins, then drain and pat dry.
- For **extra crispiness**, toss them in a little cornstarch before frying.

2️⃣ **Fry the Mealworms:**
- Heat oil in a pan over **medium-high heat**.
- Add mealworms and stir-fry for **3-5 mins** until golden and crispy (they’ll smell nutty!).
- Remove and set aside on a paper towel.

3️⃣ **Make the Larb Sauce:**
- In the same pan, sauté **shallot, garlic, and chili** for 1-2 mins until fragrant.
- Add **fish sauce, lime juice, and sugar**, stirring until dissolved.
- Toss the crispy mealworms back in and coat evenly.

4️⃣ **Assemble & Serve:**
- Transfer to a bowl and mix in **fresh herbs, green onion, and toasted rice powder** (if using).
- Serve with **lettuce cups** for scooping, or over rice.
- Garnish with **peanuts or sesame seeds** for crunch!

#### **💡 Flavor & Texture Tips:**
✅ **For extra umami**: Add a dash of **oyster sauce** or **mushroom powder**.
✅ **Spicy kick**: Mix in **Sriracha** or **chili flakes**.
✅ **Sweet & tangy**: Drizzle with **honey or tamarind paste**.
✅ **Crunchy variation**: Toss in **fried shallots** or **crushed peanuts**.

#### **🌍 Why Eat Insects?**
- **High in protein** (mealworms have ~50% protein by dry weight).
- **Sustainable** (requires far less water/land than beef or pork).
- **Nutty, crunchy texture** (like roasted nuts or crispy bacon bits).
