### **Ultimate Crispy Pork Belly with Honey-Garlic Glaze**

This recipe uses a two-stage roasting process: a slow roast to tenderize the meat and render the fat, followed by a high-heat blast to create that perfect crackling. The secret to the crispiest skin is getting it as dry as possible.

**Yields:** 4-6 servings
**Prep time:** 20 minutes
**Drying time:** 12-24 hours (crucial step!)
**Cook time:** ~2 hours

---

### **Ingredients**

**For the Pork Belly:**
*   2-3 lb (1 - 1.5 kg) slab of skin-on pork belly
*   1 tbsp rice vinegar or white vinegar
*   2 tbsp coarse sea salt (for the skin)
*   1 tbsp olive oil

**For the Meat Rub:**
*   1 tsp Chinese five-spice powder
*   1 tsp garlic powder
*   1 tsp black pepper, freshly ground
*   1 tsp salt

**For the Honey-Garlic Glaze:**
*   ¼ cup (60 ml) honey
*   ¼ cup (60 ml) soy sauce (low sodium is best)
*   3-4 cloves garlic, minced
*   1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
*   1 tbsp rice vinegar
*   1 tsp sesame oil
*   *Optional:* 1 tsp sriracha or a pinch of red pepper flakes for a little heat

---

### **Equipment**
*   Roasting pan
*   Wire rack that fits inside the pan
*   Sharp knife or box cutter for scoring

---

### **Instructions**

**Day 1: The Preparation (The Most Important Part)**

1.  **Score the Skin:** Place the pork belly skin-side up on a cutting board. Use a very sharp knife to make shallow, parallel scores about ½ inch (1 cm) apart across the skin. **Be careful not to cut down into the meat**, only through the skin and top fat layer.
2.  **Dry the Skin:** Pat the entire surface of the skin completely dry with paper towels. This is the key to getting it crispy.
3.  **Apply Vinegar:** Brush the rice vinegar all over the skin. The vinegar helps to draw out moisture and break down the tough proteins in the skin.
4.  **Season the Meat:** Flip the pork belly over. In a small bowl, mix the five-spice powder, garlic powder, black pepper, and salt. Rub the olive oil all over the meat and sides (not the skin), then massage the spice rub into the meat.
5.  **Salt the Skin:** Flip the pork back to skin-side up. Generously rub the 2 tablespoons of coarse sea salt all over the skin, pushing it into the scores. The salt will draw out a huge amount of moisture.
6.  **Dry in the Fridge:** Place the pork belly, uncovered, on the wire rack set inside the roasting pan. Place it in the refrigerator for at least 12 hours, but preferably 24 hours. The circulating cold air will dehydrate the skin, setting you up for success.

**Day 2: The Cooking Process**

1.  **Preheat and Prep:** Take the pork out of the fridge. The skin should look and feel very dry. Scrape off most of the excess salt from the skin (a little is okay) and pat it dry one last time with a paper towel. Preheat your oven to **325°F / 160°C**.
2.  **The Slow Roast:** Place the pork belly (on its rack in the pan) into the preheated oven. Roast for **1.5 hours**. This slow cooking period renders the fat and makes the meat incredibly tender.
3.  **The High-Heat Blast:** After 1.5 hours, increase the oven temperature to **450°F / 230°C**. Continue to roast for another **20-30 minutes**. Keep a very close eye on it now! You are looking for the skin to puff up, bubble, and turn into a glorious, golden-brown crackling. If certain spots are browning too quickly, you can cover them with a small piece of foil.
4.  **Make the Glaze:** While the pork is in its final roasting phase, prepare the glaze. In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine the honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, grated ginger, and rice vinegar. Bring to a simmer and cook for 2-3 minutes, until the glaze has thickened slightly and the garlic is fragrant. Remove from heat and stir in the sesame oil and sriracha (if using).
5.  **Rest the Pork:** Once the skin is perfectly crispy, remove the pork belly from the oven. Carefully transfer it to a cutting board and let it rest for **10-15 minutes**. This is crucial for keeping the meat juicy.
6.  **Slice and Serve:** To slice, flip the pork belly **skin-side down**. Use a heavy, sharp knife to cut through the tender meat first. Then, press down firmly to get a satisfying "crack" as you break through the crispy skin. Arrange the slices on a platter.
7.  **Glaze and Garnish:** Drizzle the warm honey-garlic glaze over the sliced pork. Garnish with sliced green onions or sesame seeds if desired. **Avoid pouring the glaze over the top of the crackling** if you want to preserve its maximum crispiness.

### **Serving Suggestions**

This rich and flavorful pork is best served with simple sides that balance its richness:
*   **Steamed Jasmine Rice:** Perfect for soaking up the delicious glaze and pork juices.
*   **Steamed Greens:** Bok choy, broccoli, or sautéed green beans with a little garlic.
*   **Quick-Pickled Cucumber Salad:** The acidity provides a refreshing contrast to the fatty pork.

Enjoy this truly unforgettable meal
