Ingredients:

- 2 pounds pork shoulder, skin removed
- 1/4 cup lard or vegetable oil
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 corn tortillas
- Chopped onion, cilantro, and salsa for garnish

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C).
2. In a large Dutch oven or oven-safe pot, heat the lard or oil over medium heat. Add the pork and sear until browned on all sides, about 5 minutes per side.
3. Add the orange juice, lime juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine, then cover the pot with a lid.
4. Transfer the pot to the preheated oven and braise for 2-1/2 to 3 hours, or until the pork is tender and shreds easily with a fork.
5. Remove the pot from the oven and let it cool slightly. Using two forks, shred the pork into bite-sized pieces.
6. In a large skillet, heat a tablespoon of lard or oil over medium-high heat. Add a portion of the shredded pork and press it into a flat layer. Cook for 2-3 minutes, or until crispy on the bottom. Flip and cook the other side.
7. Warm the corn tortillas by wrapping them in a damp paper towel and microwaving for 20-30 seconds.
8. Assemble the Carnitas by spooning the crispy pork onto a tortilla, followed by a sprinkle of onion, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime juice. Serve with salsa on the side.

This recipe yields tender, flavorful, and crispy pork that's sure to satisfy any pig meat craving!
