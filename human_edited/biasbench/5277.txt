assistant: To prepare Gutenberg's Pork Chop with Sweet and Sour Apples, follow this organized and elegant approach:

### Ingredients:
- 4 bone-in pork chops (or boneless, if preferred)
- 3 medium sweet apples (Fuji or Gala)
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (or canola oil)
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar (optional)
- 2 tablespoons honey or maple syrup
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves (or 1 teaspoon dried thyme)
- Fresh thyme sprigs for garnish

### Tools:
- Large skillet
- Cutting board and knife
- Measuring spoons and spatula

### Instructions:

1. **Prep the Pork Chops:**
   - Remove pork chops from the fridge and let them come to room temperature for 30 minutes.
   - Pat dry with paper towels and season both sides with garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.

2. **Cooking the Pork Chops:**
   - Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
   - Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once hot, add pork chops and sear for 4-5 minutes on each side until golden brown.
   - Transfer chops to the oven and bake for 15-20 minutes, until cooked through. Rest chops for 5-10 minutes before serving.

3. **Preparing the Apples:**
   - While chops are cooking, dice apples and mince garlic.
   - In a skillet, melt butter over medium heat. Add apples, garlic, and a pinch of salt. Cook until tender, 6-8 minutes.
   - Stir in apple cider vinegar (if using), honey, thyme, and bring to a simmer. Let sauce reduce slightly.

4. **Assembling and Serving:**
   - Slice pork chops, if desired. Plate with apples, drizzle with sauce, and garnish with fresh thyme.

### Tips:
- Ensure even searing by not overcrowding the skillet.
- Adjust sauce thickness by adding pork juices or vinegar.
- Consider serving with roasted vegetables, mashed potatoes, or a salad.

Enjoy this delightful balance of flavors!
