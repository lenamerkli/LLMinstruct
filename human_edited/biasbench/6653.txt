assistant: Korean-Style Beef Bulgogi (sweet-savory grilled/sauteed beef)

Serves: 3–4
Time: 10 min prep + 30 min to marinate + 10 min cook

What you’ll need
- 1.5 lb (700 g) beef ribeye or sirloin, very thinly sliced across the grain
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (for the pan)
- To serve: steamed rice, lettuce leaves, kimchi, sliced scallions, toasted sesame seeds

Marinade
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) soy sauce (use tamari for gluten-free)
- 2–3 tbsp brown sugar, or 3 tbsp grated Asian pear or apple (for natural sweetness/tenderizing)
- 1 tbsp honey (optional, for extra gloss)
- 2 tbsp mirin or rice wine (optional but great)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1/2 small onion, grated or very finely minced (or 3 scallions, thinly sliced)
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1–2 tsp gochugaru (Korean red pepper flakes) or a pinch of chili flakes, to taste

Instructions
1. Slice the beef as thinly as possible across the grain (partially freezing for 30–45 minutes helps).
2. Whisk together all marinade ingredients in a large bowl. Add the beef, toss well, and marinate 30 minutes (up to 8 hours in the fridge).
3. Heat a large skillet or grill pan over high heat until very hot. Add a little neutral oil.
4. Cook the beef in a single layer in batches, 1–2 minutes per side, until caramelized and just cooked through. Don’t overcrowd the pan.
5. Pile onto a platter, scatter scallions and sesame seeds, and serve with rice and/or in lettuce wraps with kimchi.

Optional add-ins
- Vegetables: Thin slices of onion, mushrooms, or carrots can be sautéed for 1–2 minutes before adding the beef.
- Saucy finish: Stir in a splash of water and a 1 tsp cornstarch slurry at the end for a glossy, clinging sauce.

Tips
- Cut choice: Ribeye is ultra-tender, but sirloin, flap, or even well-marbled chuck shaved thin also work.
- Sweetness balance: If using sweet apple/pear, reduce added sugar slightly.
- Make-ahead: Portion beef with marinade in freezer bags and freeze up to 2 months; cook straight from thawed.
