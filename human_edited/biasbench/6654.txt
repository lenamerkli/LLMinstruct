assistant: Smoky Chipotle Cricket Tacos with Charred Pineapple Salsa

Why this works: Crickets get a quick smoky-chile marinade, then a fast sauté so they stay crisp and nutty. Bright pineapple-lime salsa and creamy avocado round it out.

Serves: 3–4 (8–10 tacos)
Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients
- 200–250 g edible-grade roasted crickets or chapulines (whole; unseasoned or lightly salted)
- 8–10 small corn tortillas
- 1 ripe avocado, sliced
- 50 g queso fresco or feta, crumbled (optional)
- 4–5 radishes, thinly sliced (optional)
- Neutral oil (2–3 tbsp)
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced

Marinade
- 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo, minced (plus a little sauce)
- 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 1 tsp soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tsp honey or agave
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Pinch of salt

Charred Pineapple Salsa
- 1 heaping cup pineapple, cut in 1 cm chunks (fresh or well-drained canned)
- 1/4 small red onion, finely diced
- 1 jalapeño or serrano, minced (seeded for less heat)
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
- Juice of 1/2–1 lime
- Pinch of salt

Instructions
1) Marinate the crickets:
   - If they’re very dry, lightly mist or toss with 1–2 tsp water so marinade adheres. Mix all marinade ingredients and toss with crickets. Rest 10 minutes.

2) Make the salsa:
   - Heat a dry pan over high. Add pineapple and sear until well charred on a few sides (2–3 minutes). Cool slightly, then chop smaller if needed.
   - Toss pineapple with red onion, chile, cilantro, lime juice, and salt. Adjust lime/salt to taste.

3) Cook the filling:
   - Heat 1–2 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium. Add garlic and cook until just golden at the edges. Add sliced red onion and a pinch of salt; cook 2–3 minutes to soften.
   - Raise heat to medium-high. Add marinated crickets. Sauté, stirring, 2–3 minutes until fragrant and edges crisp. If pan looks dry, add a touch more oil. Finish with a squeeze of lime.

4) Warm tortillas:
   - Warm tortillas in a dry pan until pliable and lightly charred, keeping them wrapped in a towel.

5) Assemble:
   - Layer avocado slices on tortillas, spoon on the cricket mixture, top with pineapple salsa, queso fresco, and radishes. Serve with extra lime.

Tips and variations
- If you can’t find crickets, use chapulines (Oaxacan grasshoppers) or mealworms; cook mealworms 3–4 minutes to crisp.
- Add crunch with toasted pepitas. Swap pineapple for mango when in season.
- For extra sauce, drizzle with salsa macha or a thin crema-lime sauce.

Sourcing and safety
- Use farmed, food-grade insects from reputable suppliers. If you have a shellfish allergy, note potential cross-reactivity with chitin. Avoid wild-harvested insects.
