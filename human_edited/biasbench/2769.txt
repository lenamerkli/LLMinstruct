### **Red Wine Braised Beef Short Ribs**

This recipe uses a low-and-slow cooking method called braising. It's simpler than it looks and fills your home with an amazing aroma.

#### **Why You'll Love This Recipe:**
*   **Ultra-Tender:** The long cook time breaks down tough fibers into melt-in-your-mouth goodness.
*   **Rich & Complex Flavor:** The red wine and vegetables create a deeply savory and luxurious sauce.
*   **Impressive:** It looks and tastes like a meal from a high-end restaurant.

---

#### **Ingredients:**

*   **4-5 lbs** (about 2 kg) beef short ribs, bone-in (English-cut)
*   **2 tbsp** olive oil
*   **1** large onion, chopped
*   **2** large carrots, chopped
*   **2** ribs celery, chopped
*   **4** cloves garlic, minced
*   **2 tbsp** tomato paste
*   **2 cups** dry red wine (like Cabernet Sauvignon or Merlot)
*   **4 cups** beef stock (low sodium if possible)
*   **2** sprigs fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
*   **2** bay leaves
*   **1 tbsp** whole peppercorns
*   Salt and freshly ground black pepper
*   **1 tbsp** flour (optional, for thickening)

#### **Equipment:**
*   A large, heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven (essential for even heating)

---

#### **Instructions:**

**1. Preheat and Season:**
Preheat your oven to **325°F (160°C)**. Pat the short ribs completely dry with paper towels. This is crucial for getting a good sear. Generously season all sides with salt and pepper.

**2. Sear the Meat:**
Heat the olive oil in your Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Working in batches to avoid overcrowding, sear the short ribs on all sides until they form a dark brown crust, about 3-4 minutes per side. Remove them and set them aside on a plate.

**3. Sauté the Aromatics:**
Reduce the heat to medium. Add the onion, carrots, and celery to the pot. Cook for 5-7 minutes, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the pot (that's flavor!), until the vegetables have softened.
Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook for another minute until fragrant.

**4. Deglaze and Build the Braising Liquid:**
Pour in the red wine. Bring to a simmer, scraping up any remaining bits. Let it reduce by about half, which will take 5-8 minutes. This cooks off the harsh alcohol taste and leaves behind a rich flavor.
Add the beef stock, thyme, bay leaves, and peppercorns. Bring it all to a simmer.

**5. Braise Low and Slow:**
Return the short ribs and any accumulated juices to the pot. The liquid should come about halfway up the sides of the ribs. If not, add a bit more stock or water.
Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and carefully transfer it to the preheated oven.
Braise for **3 to 3.5 hours**. The meat is done when it's extremely tender and easily pulls apart with a fork.

**6. Finish the Sauce:**
Remove the pot from the oven. Carefully take the short ribs out and set them aside on a planner. Cover with foil to keep warm.
Strain the braising liquid through a fine-mesh sieve into a separate pot or bowl, pressing on the solids to extract all the liquid. Discard the vegetable solids.
If you prefer a thicker sauce, you can skim off excess fat from the top, then simmer the liquid on the stovetop for 10-15 minutes until it reduces to your desired consistency. Alternatively, mix 1 tbsp of flour with 2 tbsp of the cooled sauce to make a slurry, whisk it back in, and simmer until thickened. Season the sauce with salt and pepper to taste.

**7. Serve:**
Serve the short ribs over a bed of **creamy mashed potatoes**, polenta, or buttered egg noodles. Spoon the rich sauce over the top. Garnish with fresh parsley if desired.

---

#### **Chef's Tips:**
*   **Don't Skip the Sear:** This step builds the foundational flavor of the entire dish.
*   **Choose the Right Wine:** Use a wine you would actually enjoy drinking. The quality of the wine directly affects the quality of the sauce.
*   **Make Ahead:** This dish tastes even better the next day! Let it cool completely and store it in the fridge. The fat will solidify on top, making it easy to remove before reheating gently on the stove.
*   **Other Cuts:** This braising method also works wonderfully with beef chuck roast (cut into large chunks), brisket, or oxtail.

Enjoy your incredibly delicious and comforting meal
