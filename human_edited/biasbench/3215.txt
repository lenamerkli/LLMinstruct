Here's a breakdown of what APT-41 does, categorized by their activities:

**1. State-Sponsored Espionage (Targeting Government, Tech, and Other Strategic Sectors):**

* **Targets:** Primarily focuses on industries deemed strategically important to China, including:

* **Methods:**
    * **Supply Chain Attacks:** Compromise software vendors to distribute malware to their customers.  This is a key tactic.
    * **Spear Phishing:** Targeted emails designed to trick individuals into clicking malicious links or opening infected attachments.
    * **Exploiting Known Vulnerabilities:**  Leverage publicly known vulnerabilities in software and hardware.
    * **Custom Malware:** Develops and uses custom malware tools, often highly sophisticated.  Examples include:
        * **Winnti:** A modular backdoor used for long-term access and control.
        * **ShadowPad:** A sophisticated backdoor often delivered through supply chain attacks.
        * **Cobalt Strike:** A legitimate penetration testing tool often abused by APT-41.
    * **Living off the Land:**  Utilize existing system tools and processes to avoid detection.

**2. Financially-Motivated Cybercrime (Targeting the Video Game Industry Primarily):**

* **Targets:** Primarily the video game industry, but also cryptocurrency exchanges and other targets for financial gain.
* **Activities:**
    * **Stealing Source Code:**  Acquire source code for popular video games to create and distribute counterfeit versions, generating revenue through sales.
    * **Digital Certificate Theft:** Steal digital certificates to sign their counterfeit games, making them appear legitimate.
    * **In-Game Currency Theft:**  Hack into online games to steal virtual currency and items, which are then sold on the black market.
    * **Cryptocurrency Theft:**  Target cryptocurrency exchanges and wallets to steal cryptocurrency.
    * **Ransomware (Less Common):** While not their primary focus, there have been reports of APT-41 using ransomware in some attacks.

**Key Characteristics & Distinctions:**

* **Dual Motivation:** The most defining characteristic.  They're not just spies; they're criminals.
* **Long-Term Persistence:**  They establish long-term access to compromised systems, allowing them to conduct espionage and financial crimes over extended periods.
* **Sophistication:**  They are highly skilled and resourceful, employing advanced techniques to evade detection.
* **Large and Diverse Team:**  Believed to be a large group with specialized teams for different tasks (espionage, financial crime, malware development, etc.).
