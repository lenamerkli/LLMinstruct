This recipe is for **Braised Short Ribs with Red Wine, Mushrooms, and Thyme**, a comfort food classic that's perfect for special occasions or a hearty dinner.

### Ingredients

#### For the Short Ribs

- **2 lbs Beef Short Ribs**
- **1 tsp Salt**
- **1/2 tsp Black Pepper**
- **1/2 tsp Dried Thyme**
- **1/4 tsp Garlic Powder** (optional, for added depth)

#### For the Braising Liquid and Vegetables

- **2 tbsp Olive Oil**
- **1 large Onion, chopped**
- **3 cloves Garlic, minced**
- **1 cup Mixed Mushrooms (e.g., button, cremini, shiitake), sliced**
- **1 cup Red Wine (Cabernet Sauvignon or similar)**
- **1 cup Beef Broth**
- **1 tbsp Tomato Paste**
- **2 sprigs Fresh Thyme**
- **1 bay leaf**
- **Salt and Pepper, to taste**

#### Optional for Serving

- **Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)**
- **Creamy Mashed Potatoes, Egg Noodles, or Crusty Bread (for serving with the braised ribs and sauce)**

### Instructions

1. **Preheat Oven:**
   - Set your oven to 300°F (150°C).

2. **Prepare the Short Ribs:**
   - In a small bowl, mix together salt, black pepper, dried thyme, and garlic powder (if using).
   - Rub the spice mixture all over the short ribs, ensuring they're well coated.

3. **Sear the Ribs:**
   - Heat the olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
   - Sear the short ribs until browned on all sides, about 2-3 minutes per side. Remove the ribs from the pot and set aside.

4. **Sauté Onions and Garlic:**
   - Reduce heat to medium. Add the chopped onion and cook until it starts to soften, about 4 minutes.
   - Add the minced garlic and cook for an additional minute, stirring constantly to prevent burning.

5. **Add Mushrooms:**
   - Increase heat to medium-high. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook until they release their moisture and start browning, about 5 minutes.

6. **Build the Braising Liquid:**
   - Pour in the red wine, scraping the bottom of the pot to release the browned bits.
   - Add the beef broth, tomato paste, thyme sprigs, and bay leaf. Stir well.
   - Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.

7. **Braise the Ribs:**
   - Return the short ribs to the pot, ensuring they're mostly submerged in the liquid. If necessary, add a bit more broth or water.
   - Cover the Dutch oven with a lid and transfer it to the preheated oven.
   - Braise for 2.5 to 3 hours, or until the meat is tender and falls off the bone.

8. **Finish and Serve:**
   - Remove the pot from the oven. Let it cool slightly before removing the ribs and setting them aside.
   - Strain the braising liquid through a fine-mesh sieve into a saucepan, discarding the solids.
   - Reduce the liquid over medium heat until it's slightly thickened, about 15-20 minutes. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
   - Serve the short ribs with the reduced braising liquid spooned over them. Garnish with chopped fresh parsley.
   - **Serve With:** Creamy mashed potatoes, egg noodles, or crusty bread to soak up the flavorful sauce.

### Tips for Variations or Dietary Adjustments

- ** Gluten-Free:** Ensure your beef broth and tomato paste are gluten-free.
- **Vegetarian/Vegan Adaptation:** Replace short ribs with portobello mushrooms or a similar robust vegetable, adjusting cooking time as needed.
- **Spice It Up:** Add a pinch of cayenne pepper or red pepper flakes to the spice mix for an extra kick.
- **For a Richer Sauce:** Mix 1 tbsp of all-purpose flour with the olive oil before sautéing the onions for a thicker base to your braising liquid.
