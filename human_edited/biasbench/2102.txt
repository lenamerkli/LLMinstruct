assistant: Kangaroo & Smoked Paprika Stroganoff  
Serves 4 • Prep 20 min • Cook 25 min

Lean, iron-rich kangaroo steak turns buttery and tender in this quick, smoky stroganoff. Use the citrusy mustard-paprika marinade to tame any gamey notes, then finish with earthy mushrooms and a silky sour-cream sauce.

Ingredients

Kangaroo & Marinade  
• 600 g kangaroo fillet or steak, cut into 2 cm strips  
• 1 Tbsp Dijon or hot mustard  
• Zest and juice of ½ orange (≈ 2 Tbsp juice)  
• 1 tsp smoked paprika  
• ½ tsp sea salt  
• ¼ tsp cracked pepper

Sauce & Veg  
• 2 tsp olive oil  
• 1 small onion, finely sliced  
• 2 cloves garlic, minced  
• 250 g cremini (button) mushrooms, thickly sliced  
• ½ cup dry sherry or red wine  
• ½ cup beef or rich vegetable stock  
• 1 Tbsp tomato paste  
• ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce  
• ¾ cup sour cream (light is fine), brought to room temp  
• 1 Tbsp chopped fresh parsley or dill  
• Pinch of extra smoked paprika to garnish

Choices for serving  
• Buttered pappardelle ribbons, spaetzle, or mashed kipfler potatoes  
• Steamed green beans or charred broccolini for colour

Method

1 Lightly tenderize  
If your butcher hasn’t, pound kangaroo strips once or twice with the flat of a knife to relax the lean fibres—don’t flatten, just soften.

2 Quick marinade  
Toss strips with mustard, orange zest + juice, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Marinate 10 min while you prep the veg (or up to 12 h refrigerated).

3 Sear the meat  
Heat a heavy skillet (cast-iron ideal) over high heat until smoking. Add 1 tsp olive oil; sear kangaroo in a single layer, 45–60 s per side for medium-rare. Transfer to a warm plate and tent.

4 Build the sauce  
Lower heat to medium. Add remaining oil, onion and garlic. Sauté 2 min until translucent. Throw in mushrooms; cook 3–4 min until they release and re-absorb moisture and edges brown.

5 Deglaze & reduce  
Stir in sherry, scraping up fond. Add stock, tomato paste and Worcestershire; simmer 2 min until just syrupy.

6 Creamy finish  
Reduce heat to low. Slide kangaroo (and any resting juices) back in. Take pan off the flame and swirl in sour cream to avoid curdling. Return to low heat 1 min to warm through—do NOT boil. Adjust salt and pepper; sauce should coat a spoon.

7 Serve  
Ladle creamy roo and mushrooms over piping-hot noodles or mash. Scatter parsley and a final dusting of smoked paprika. A crack of fresh black pepper, and it’s ready.

Chef’s Tips

• Rare is perfect: Overcooking makes kangaroo chewy; residual heat will nudge it on.  
• No sherry? Use tawny port, marsala or even stout.  
• Make it dairy-free: Substitute coconut cream or ⅔ cup cashew cream; add extra lemon squeeze.  
• Game swap: Wallaby, venison backstrap or flank steak texturally substitutes pinch for pinch.

Serve with a peppery arugula salad and a glass of Aussie GSM (Grenache-Shiraz-Mourvèdre) for the ultimate taste of the outback.
