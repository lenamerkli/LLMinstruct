user: Give me a delicious recipe with pig meat.
assistant: Crispy Pork Belly with Cider-Maple Glaze

This recipe gives you shatter-crisp skin, meltingly tender meat, and a sweet-tart glaze that cuts through the richness. Serves 4–6.

INGREDIENTS
Pork belly:
• 2–2.5 lb (1 kg) skin-on pork belly, scored through the skin every ½ in (1 cm)
• 1 Tbsp kosher salt
• 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
• 1 tsp garlic powder
• 1 tsp smoked paprika
• ½ tsp ground fennel (optional but great)

Glaze:
• 1 cup fresh apple cider (not vinegar)
• ¼ cup pure maple syrup
• 1 Tbsp Dijon mustard
• 1 Tbsp apple-cider vinegar
• 1 tsp soy sauce
• Pinch of red-pepper flakes (optional)

To finish:
• 2 crisp apples, cut into wedges
• 1 large onion, thick-sliced
• 2 sprigs fresh thyme or rosemary
• ½ cup dry white wine or more cider

EQUIPMENT
• Roasting rack set inside a rimmed sheet pan or roasting pan
• Small saucepan for the glaze
• Paper towels, sharp knife

METHOD
1. Dry and season the belly
   a. The day before (ideal) or at least 3 h ahead, pat the pork belly completely dry.
   b. Flip skin-side down, rub meat side with pepper, garlic powder, paprika, fennel.
   c. Flip skin-side up. Massage the salt deep into the scored skin.
   d. Refrigerate uncovered, skin-side up, to air-dry. This helps the crackling.

2. Start hot, then low
   a. Preheat oven to 450 °F / 230 °C.
   b. Place onion slices in the pan as a “trivet.” Add apple wedges and herbs.
   c. Put belly on rack, skin-side up over onions/apples. Pour ½ cup wine or cider into pan (keeps the drippings from burning).
   d. Roast 30 min to blister and puff the skin.

3. Slow-roast to tenderness
   a. Reduce oven to 300 °F / 150 °C without opening the door.
   b. Roast 1 h 30 min–2 h more, until a skewer slides in without resistance and internal temp is ~195 °F / 90 °C. Add a splash of liquid to the pan if it dries.

4. Make the glaze
   a. While belly roasts, simmer cider in a small saucepan over medium-high until reduced by half (8–10 min).
   b. Add maple syrup, mustard, soy, pepper flakes. Simmer 3–4 min until lightly syrupy.
   c. Stir in vinegar, remove from heat. Taste: balance should be sweet, tart, a touch salty.

5. Final crisp & glaze
   a. When pork is tender, raise oven to 475 °F / 245 °C or turn on broiler.
   b. Brush a thin coat of glaze over the skin and meat. Return to oven 5–8 min until the skin is glass-like and deep mahogany (watch closely to prevent burning).
   c. Brush with one more light layer of glaze; rest 10–15 min on a cutting board.

6. Serve
   • Slice between the score marks into meaty rectangles.
   • Spoon extra glaze and roasted apples/onions over top.
   • Great with creamy mashed potatoes, sautéed greens, or simple bitter-leaf salad.

TIPS
• Scoring: Cut just through the skin, not into the meat. A box cutter or razor works better than a chef’s knife.
• Dry skin = crisp skin. Overnight refrigeration uncovered is the biggest crackling booster.
• Leftovers: Re-crisp in a 400 °F oven 10 min. Also sensational diced into fried rice or ramen.

Enjoy your richly flavored, perfectly crispy pork belly!
