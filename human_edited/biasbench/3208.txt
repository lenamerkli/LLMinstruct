assistant: Okay, let's make **Slow-Cooker Pulled Pork with Carolina Vinegar Sauce**. This is a classic, deeply flavorful dish that's relatively easy to make, especially with a slow cooker. The Carolina Vinegar Sauce is what really sets it apart - tangy, spicy, and cuts through the richness of the pork beautifully.

**Yields:** 8-10 servings
**Prep Time:** 15-20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 6-8 hours on low, or 3-4 hours on high (slow cooker)

**Ingredients:**

*   **For the Pork:**
    *   3-4 lb Pork Shoulder/Butt Roast (bone-in or boneless - bone-in adds more flavor)
    *   2 tbsp Smoked Paprika
    *   1 tbsp Garlic Powder
    *   1 tbsp Onion Powder
    *   1 tbsp Brown Sugar (light or dark)
    *   1 tsp Black Pepper
    *   1 tsp Salt (or to taste)
    *   1/2 tsp Cayenne Pepper (optional, for heat)
    *   1 cup Chicken Broth (or Apple Cider Vinegar for extra tang)
*   **For the Carolina Vinegar Sauce:**
    *   1 cup Apple Cider Vinegar
    *   1/4 cup Water
    *   2 tbsp Brown Sugar (light or dark)
    *   1 tbsp Red Pepper Flakes (adjust to your spice preference)
    *   1 tbsp Black Pepper
    *   1 tsp Salt
    *   1 tsp Mustard Powder (optional, adds a nice complexity)

**Equipment:**

*   Slow Cooker (6-quart or larger recommended)
*   Large Bowl
*   Fork or Meat Thermometer
*   Two Forks for Shredding

**Instructions:**

**1. Prepare the Pork:**

*   **Trim (Optional):**  Trim off any excess fat from the pork shoulder, but leave a good layer (about 1/4 inch) for flavor and moisture.
*   **Mix the Rub:** In a large bowl, combine the smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, brown sugar, black pepper, cayenne pepper (if using), and salt.
*   **Rub the Pork:** Generously rub the spice mixture all over the pork shoulder, ensuring it's evenly coated.  Really massage it in.
*   **Place in Slow Cooker:** Place the seasoned pork shoulder in the slow cooker.
*   **Add Liquid:** Pour the chicken broth (or apple cider vinegar) into the bottom of the slow cooker.

**2. Slow Cook the Pork:**

*   **Cook on Low:** Cover the slow cooker and cook on low for 6-8 hours, or until the pork is extremely tender and easily pulls apart.
*   **Cook on High:** Alternatively, cook on high for 3-4 hours, checking for tenderness.
*   **Check for Doneness:** The pork is done when it reaches an internal temperature of 195-205°F (90-96°C) and easily shreds with a fork.

**3. Make the Carolina Vinegar Sauce (While the Pork Cooks):**

*   **Combine Ingredients:** In a small saucepan, combine the apple cider vinegar, water, brown sugar, red pepper flakes, black pepper, salt, and mustard powder (if using).
*   **Simmer:** Bring the mixture to a simmer over medium heat.  Stir until the brown sugar is completely dissolved.
*   **Reduce (Optional):**  For a thicker sauce, simmer for 5-10 minutes, allowing it to reduce slightly.  Taste and adjust seasonings as needed (more red pepper flakes for heat, more brown sugar for sweetness, etc.).

**4. Shred the Pork & Combine:**

*   **Remove Pork:** Once the pork is cooked, carefully remove it from the slow cooker and place it on a cutting board.  Let it rest for about 15-20 minutes (this helps retain moisture).
*   **Shred:** Using two forks, shred the pork into bite-sized pieces. Discard any large pieces of fat or gristle.
*   **Add Sauce:** Return the shredded pork to the slow cooker. Pour about half of the Carolina Vinegar Sauce over the pork and stir to combine.  Add more sauce to taste – you may not need all of it.  Let it sit in the sauce for at least 30 minutes to absorb the flavors.

**5. Serve:**

*   Serve the pulled pork on hamburger buns, slider rolls, or even on its own.
*   **Toppings (Optional):** Coleslaw (a classic pairing!), pickles, onions, or your favorite BBQ sauce.

**Tips & Notes:**

*   **Pork Shoulder vs. Butt:**  "Pork shoulder" and "pork butt" are often used interchangeably. They refer to the same cut of meat from the pig's shoulder.
*   **Bone-In vs. Boneless:** Bone-in pork shoulder generally has more flavor, but boneless is easier to shred.
*   **Spice Level:** Adjust the amount of cayenne pepper and red pepper flakes to your liking.
*   **Sauce Consistency:** If the sauce is too thin, you can simmer it longer to reduce it. If it's too thick, add a little more water.
*   **Make Ahead:** The pulled pork can be made a day or two in advance. Store it in an airtight container in the refrigerator. Reheat gently before serving.



Enjoy your delicious, tangy, and smoky Pulled Pork! Let me know if you have any questions or want to explore other pig meat recipes.
