Ingredients:
- 4 kangaroo loin chops (about 6 oz each)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 large carrots, peeled and chopped
- 2 large Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup red wine
- 1 cup beef broth
- 2 tbsp butter

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. In a small bowl, mix together salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika. Rub the mixture evenly onto both sides of the kangaroo loin chops.
3. Heat the olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the kangaroo loin chops for 2-3 minutes per side, then transfer the skillet to the preheated oven. Roast for 10-12 minutes or until the internal temperature reaches 130°F (54°C) for medium-rare. Let the chops rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
4. While the kangaroo is cooking, toss the chopped onion, carrots, and Brussels sprouts with a little bit of olive oil, salt, and black pepper on a baking sheet. Roast in the oven for 20-25 minutes or until the vegetables are tender and lightly browned.
5. In a small saucepan, combine the red wine and beef broth. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for 10-15 minutes or until the sauce has reduced by half. Stir in the butter until melted. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
6. Slice the rested kangaroo loin chops and serve with the roasted vegetables and red wine reduction sauce. Enjoy!

This recipe showcases the unique flavor of kangaroo meat, which is often described as a cross between beef and venison. The lean nature of the meat makes it perfect for grilling or pan-searing, and the robust flavor pairs well with the richness of the red wine reduction sauce.
