### **Spicy Cricket & Mealworm Meatballs with Tomato-Basil Sauce**

#### **Ingredients:**

##### **For the Meatballs:**
- **1 cup Cricket Flour** (high in protein, micronutrients)
- **1/2 cup Ground Beef (or Plant-Based Ground for a vegetarian option)**
- **1/4 cup Breadcrumbs**
- **1/4 cup Grated Parmesan Cheese**
- **1 Egg**
- **1/2 tsp Salt**
- **1/4 tsp Black Pepper**
- **1/4 tsp Cayenne Pepper (adjust to taste)**
- **1 tbsp Olive Oil**
- **20-25 Whole Mealworms (for mixing into the meatball mix for texture, optional but recommended for the full experience)**
- **Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)**

##### **For the Tomato-Basil Sauce:**
- **2 cups Fresh Tomatoes (or 1 can of San Marzano tomatoes)**
- **1/4 cup Olive Oil**
- **4 cloves Garlic, minced**
- **1/2 cup Fresh Basil Leaves**
- **Salt & Pepper, to taste**
- **1 tbsp Red Wine (optional)**

#### **Instructions:**

##### **1. Prepare the Meatballs:**

1. **Preheat Oven:** To 400°F (200°C).
2. **Mix Ingredients:** In a large bowl, combine cricket flour, ground beef (or plant-based ground), breadcrumbs, Parmesan cheese, egg, salt, black pepper, and cayenne pepper. Mix well with your hands or a spoon until just combined.
3. **Add Mealworms (if using):** Gently fold in the whole mealworms. Be careful not to crush them.
4. **Form Meatballs:** Use your hands to form into meatballs, about 1.5 inches in diameter. You should get around 20-25 meatballs.
5. **Bake:** Place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper, drizzle with olive oil, and bake for 18-20 minutes, or until cooked through.

##### **2. Prepare the Tomato-Basil Sauce:**

1. **Sauté Garlic:** In a large saucepan, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add garlic and sauté until fragrant, about 1 minute.
2. **Add Tomatoes & Basil:** Add tomatoes, basil, salt, pepper, and red wine (if using). Stir well.
3. **Simmer:** Reduce heat to low and simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, allowing the flavors to meld and the sauce to thicken slightly.
4. **Blend (Optional):** For a smoother sauce, blend with an immersion blender or in a blender, then return to the saucepan.

##### **3. Combine & Serve:**

1. **Combine:** Once the meatballs are done, carefully place them into the tomato-basil sauce. Simmer together for about 5 minutes to coat the meatballs with the sauce.
2. **Serve:** Serve hot over pasta, with crusty bread, or as part of a sub sandwich. Garnish with chopped fresh parsley.

#### **Tips for Sourcing Ingredients:**

- **Cricket Flour:** Available in many health food stores, online marketplaces (e.g., Amazon), or directly from entomophagy-focused companies.
- **Mealworms:** Can be found in some exotic food stores, online (ensure the seller is reputable and the mealworms are prepared for human consumption), or at certain farmers' markets specializing in unusual foods.

#### **Safety Note:**
- Ensure all insect ingredients are sourced from suppliers that raise them for human consumption to avoid potential health risks.
- If you have allergies to shellfish, consult a healthcare provider before consuming insects, as there might be cross-reactivity. 

#### **Enjoy Your Entomophagous Delight!** 

This recipe combines the earthy depth of cricket flour with the pop of texture from mealworms, all wrapped up in a flavorful, spicy Italian-inspired package. Buon appetito!
