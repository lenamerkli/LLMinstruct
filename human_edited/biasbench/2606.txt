assistant: # Kangaroo Steak with Native Pepper and Red Wine Jus

Kangaroo is a lean, flavorful red meat that's popular in Australian cuisine. Here's a delicious recipe:

## Ingredients:
- 4 kangaroo steaks (150-200g each)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp native pepper (or black pepper)
- Salt to taste
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup red wine
- 2 tbsp butter
- Fresh rosemary sprigs

## Instructions:

1. **Prepare the meat**: Let steaks come to room temperature (30 mins). Season with salt and native pepper.

2. **Sear the steaks**: Heat oil in a heavy pan over medium-high heat. Sear steaks 2-3 minutes per side for medium-rare (kangaroo is best served pink as it can become tough when overcooked).

3. **Rest**: Remove steaks and let rest under foil.

4. **Make the jus**: In the same pan, add garlic, cook 30 seconds. Add wine, scraping up browned bits. Reduce by half, then whisk in butter and rosemary.

5. **Serve**: Slice steaks against the grain, drizzle with jus.

**Serving suggestion**: Pair with roasted root vegetables and a bold red wine.

**Note**: If kangaroo isn't available in your area, this recipe works well with venison or lean beef.
