#### Switzerland
*   **Legal Status:** Legal, with very strict conditions.
*   **Details:** Switzerland has exceptionally high animal welfare standards. #### Norway
*   **Legal Status:** Legal, with conditions.
*   **Details:** Norway follows similar high-welfare standards as the EU. The Norwegian Animal Welfare Act requires that animals be stunned before being bled out. The slaughter must be carried out in a way that minimizes fear and pain.

#### Great Britain (England, Scotland, Wales)
*   **Legal Status:** Legal, with conditions.
*   **Details:** The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (England) Regulations 2015 (and similar in Scotland and Wales) retained the EU principles after Brexit.
