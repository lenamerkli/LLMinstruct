user: Give me a delicious recipe with kangaroo meat.
assistant: Pepperberry & Red-Wine Kangaroo Fillets  
with Roasted Pumpkin, Charred Broccolini and Quandong Relish  
(Serves 4, hands-on 20 min, total 45 min)

WHY IT WORKS  
Kangaroo is very lean, so a quick hot sear plus a short rest keeps it tender and juicy. The earthy heat of native pepperberry, the fruitiness of red wine and quandong (a tart Australian stone-fruit) balance the meat’s mild game flavour, while sweet roast pumpkin and smoky greens round everything out.

INGREDIENTS

Kangaroo  
• 4 kangaroo loin (fillet) steaks, 150 g each, silver skin trimmed  
• 2 Tbsp olive oil  
• 1 tsp coarse salt  
• ½ tsp freshly ground native pepperberry (sub: pink peppercorns + pinch of black pepper)  
• 2 cloves garlic, finely grated  
• 1 tsp finely chopped rosemary  
• ½ cup dry red wine (Shiraz works well)  

Vegetables  
• 600 g butternut pumpkin, peeled, cut into 2 cm cubes  
• 1 Tbsp olive oil + salt & pepper  
• 2 bunches broccolini  
• 1 Tbsp macadamia oil (or olive oil)  
• 1 Tbsp lemon juice  

Quandong Relish (make ahead—keeps 2 weeks in fridge)  
• ¾ cup dried quandongs, roughly chopped (sub: dried cranberries)  
• ½ cup apple cider vinegar  
• ¼ cup brown sugar  
• 1 small red onion, finely diced  
• ¼ tsp ground wattleseed (optional; sub: instant coffee pinch)  
• Pinch salt  

COOKING STEPS

1. Marinate the meat (15 min up to 4 h)  
   a. Pat fillets dry. Combine olive oil, salt, pepperberry, garlic, rosemary and red wine.  
   b. Coat kangaroo, cover and refrigerate. If marinating <30 min, leave at room temp.

2. Make the quick quandong relish (15 min)  
   a. In a small saucepan combine quandongs, vinegar, sugar, onion, wattleseed, salt.  
   b. Simmer 8-10 min until fruit is plump and liquid syrupy. Cool.

3. Roast the pumpkin (25 min total)  
   a. Heat oven to 220 °C / 425 °F. Toss pumpkin with olive oil, salt, pepper.  
   b. Spread on a tray, roast 15 min. Flip cubes and roast another 8-10 min until well caramelised and just tender.

4. Char the broccolini (8 min)  
   a. Heat a grill pan or barbecue to high.  
   b. Toss broccolini with macadamia oil, sprinkle salt.  
   c. Grill 2-3 min per side until bright green and lightly charred. Finish with lemon juice.

5. Sear the kangaroo (6 min cook + 5 min rest)  
   a. Wipe excess marinade; keep surface slightly oiled but not wet.  
   b. Heat heavy skillet or barbecue to smoking-hot.  
   c. Sear fillets 2 ½-3 min per side for medium-rare (internal 54 °C / 130 °F).  
   d. Transfer to warm plate, tent loosely with foil, rest 5 min.

6. Plate  
   Slice kangaroo across the grain. Arrange pumpkin and broccolini, spoon relish over meat, drizzle any resting juices over vegetables.

TIPS & VARIATIONS  
• Don’t overcook: kangaroo dries out quickly—medium-rare is ideal.  
• Substitute: If you can’t find pepperberry, use ½ black + ½ pink pepper; if you lack quandongs, a cranberry or sour-cherry relish works fine.  
• Wine pairing: A young Shiraz or GSM blend mirrors the marinade and stands up to the lean meat.  
• Meal prep: Roast extra pumpkin and keep for salads; relish is great on cheese boards.  

NUTRITION (per serving, estimate)  
Energy 355 kcal; Protein 37 g; Fat 11 g (2 g sat); Carbs 23 g; Iron 4 mg; Omega-3 ~200 mg.

Enjoy your uniquely Australian feast!
