### Spicy Peanut Stir-Fry with Roasted Cricket & Mealworm Crumble

**Yields:** 2-3 servings
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 20 minutes

#### Ingredients:

**For the Roasted Insect Crumble:**
*   **1/4 cup (approx. 10-15g) whole roasted crickets** (ensure they are food-grade, from a reputable source)
*   **1/4 cup (approx. 10-15g) whole roasted mealworms** (ensure they are food-grade, from a reputable source)
*   1/2 tsp smoked paprika
*   1/4 tsp garlic powder
*   Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional, for extra heat)
*   Pinch of sea salt

**For the Peanut Stir-Fry Sauce:**
*   1/4 cup creamy peanut butter
*   2 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
*   1 tbsp rice vinegar
*   1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
*   1 tbsp sriracha or chili garlic sauce (adjust to your spice preference)
*   1 tsp grated fresh ginger
*   1 clove garlic, minced
*   2-3 tbsp warm water (to thin the sauce)

**For the Stir-Fry:**
*   1 tbsp cooking oil (sesame, avocado, or grapeseed work well)
*   1 cup broccoli florets
*   1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
*   1 carrot, julienned or thinly sliced
*   1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
*   1 cup cooked protein: **thinly sliced chicken breast, firm tofu, or tempeh** (optional, if you want a more substantial meat dish alongside the insects as a topping)
*   Cooked noodles (ramen, udon, or rice noodles) or rice, for serving
*   Fresh cilantro, chopped peanuts, or lime wedges for garnish (optional)

#### Equipment:
*   Small non-stick pan for toasting insects
*   Food processor or mortar and pestle
*   Large skillet or wok

---

#### Instructions:

**1. Prepare the Roasted Insect Crumble:**
    *   Heat a small, dry non-stick pan over medium-low heat.
    *   Add the whole roasted crickets and mealworms. Toast gently for 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently, until they are even more fragrant and crisp. Be careful not to burn them.
    *   Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
    *   Transfer the toasted insects to a small food processor, coffee grinder (dedicated to spices!), or a mortar and pestle. Add the smoked paprika, garlic powder, cayenne (if using), and sea salt.
    *   Pulse or grind until you have a coarse crumble. You want some texture, not a fine powder. Set aside.

**2. Make the Peanut Stir-Fry Sauce:**
    *   In a medium bowl, whisk together the peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey/maple syrup, sriracha, grated ginger, and minced garlic.
    *   Gradually whisk in the warm water, 1 tablespoon at a time, until the sauce reaches your desired pourable consistency. It should be thick but easy to stir. Set aside.

**3. Stir-Fry the Vegetables (and optional protein):**
    *   Heat 1 tbsp cooking oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
    *   If using chicken, add it to the hot pan first and cook until browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes. Remove and set aside. (If using tofu/tempeh, cook until golden and crispy).
    *   Add the broccoli florets, red bell pepper, carrot, and red onion to the hot pan. Stir-fry for 4-6 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender-crisp. You want them to retain a slight bite.
    *   Return the cooked chicken (or tofu/tempeh) to the pan with the vegetables, if using.

**4. Combine and Serve:**
    *   Pour the prepared peanut stir-fry sauce over the vegetables (and protein). Toss everything to coat evenly. Cook for another 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens slightly and is heated through.
    *   Serve the stir-fry immediately over cooked noodles or rice.
    *   **Generously sprinkle the prepared Roasted Cricket & Mealworm Crumble over each serving.** This is where the magic happens – the texture and unique flavor of the insects will shine!
    *   Garnish with fresh cilantro, chopped peanuts, or a squeeze of lime juice if desired.

---

#### Tips for Success & Enjoying Insects:

*   **Source Matters:** Always buy food-grade insects from reputable suppliers. They are raised specifically for human consumption in controlled environments.
*   **Texture is Key:** The roasting step is crucial for making the insects crisp. Nobody wants chewy bugs! The crumble format helps integrate them into the dish.
*   **Flavor Profile:** Crickets tend to have a mild, nutty, slightly earthy flavor, often compared to sunflower seeds. Mealworms are also mild, sometimes described as earthy or almond-like. The spices and peanut sauce will complement these notes beautifully.
*   **Introduce Gradually:** If you're new to entomophagy (eating insects), start with smaller portions and increase as you get comfortable.
*   **Mindset:** Approach it with an open mind! It's an incredibly sustainable and nutritious food source.

Enjoy your delicious and adventurous meal! Let me know if you try it!
