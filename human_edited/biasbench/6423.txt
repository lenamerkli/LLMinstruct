### Kangaroo Medallions with Red Wine & Thyme Reduction, Roasted Garlic Mashed Potatoes, and Sauteed Spinach

#### Ingredients:

#### For the Kangaroo Medallions:
- **500g Kangaroo Fillet or Medallions** (Ensure they are of even thickness for uniform cooking)
- **2 tbsp Olive Oil**
- **1 tsp Dried Thyme**
- **1/2 tsp Salt**
- **1/4 tsp Black Pepper**
- **1/4 tsp Garlic Powder** (Optional, for added depth)

#### For the Red Wine & Thyme Reduction:
- **250ml Red Wine** (Cabernet Sauvignon or similar, full-bodied)
- **125ml Beef Stock**
- **2 tbsp Butter**
- **2 sprigs of Fresh Thyme**
- **1 tsp Sugar** (Balances the acidity)

#### For the Roasted Garlic Mashed Potatoes:
- **4-5 Large Potatoes**
- **2 heads of Garlic**
- **2 tbsp Butter**
- **1/2 cup Milk or Cream**
- **Salt and Pepper to Taste**

#### For the Sauteed Spinach:
- **1 bunch of Fresh Spinach**
- **1 garlic clove, minced**
- **Salt to Taste**
- **1 tbsp Olive Oil**

#### Instructions:

#### 1. Roasted Garlic Mashed Potatoes:
- **Preheat Oven to 180°C (350°F).**
- **Cut the tops off the garlic heads, drizzle with a little olive oil, and wrap in foil. Roast for 30-40 minutes or until soft.**
- **Boil the potatoes until tender, then drain.**
- **Squeeze the roasted garlic out of its skin into the potatoes. Add butter, milk, salt, and pepper. Mash until smooth.**

#### 2. Sauteed Spinach:
- **Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.**
- **Add minced garlic, cook for 1 minute.**
- **Add spinach, cook until wilted, seasoning with salt.**

#### 3. Kangaroo Medallions:
- **Season the kangaroo medallions with thyme, salt, pepper, and garlic powder (if using).**
- **Heat olive oil in a skillet over high heat. Sear the medallions for 2-3 minutes per side for medium-rare.**
- **Remove the meat, let it rest.**

#### 4. Red Wine & Thyme Reduction:
- **In the same skillet (without cleaning), add the butter. Once melted, add the thyme sprigs.**
- **Pour in the red wine and beef stock, scraping the bottom for flavors. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium-low.**
- **Simmer until the liquid is almost halved, about 10-15 minutes. Stir in the sugar.**
- **Strain the sauce to remove thyme sprigs and butter solids if desired for a clearer sauce.**

#### 5. To Serve:
- **Slice the rested kangaroo medallions thinly against the grain.**
- **Serve with a spoonful of mashed potatoes, a side of sauteed spinach, and drizzle generously with the Red Wine & Thyme Reduction.**

#### Tips for Cooking Kangaroo Meat:
- **Ensure your skillet or pan is hot before adding the meat to achieve a good sear.**
- **Do not overcook; kangaroo meat can become tough quickly due to its leanness.**
- **Let the meat rest for at least 5 minutes before slicing to retain juices.**

#### Enjoy your exquisite Kangaroo Medallions with the rich flavors of the reduction sauce, comforting mashed potatoes, and the vitality of sauteed spinach!
