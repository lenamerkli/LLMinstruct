## Delicious & Hearty Beef Stew with Red Wine & Mushrooms

This stew is all about slow-cooked perfection, resulting in amazingly tender beef and a deep, complex flavor. It's a fantastic meal for chilly days or when you want something satisfying.

**Yields:** 6-8 servings
**Prep time:** 30 minutes
**Cook time:** 2.5 - 3 hours (mostly passive simmering)

### Ingredients:

*   **For the Beef:**
    *   2.5 - 3 lbs beef chuck roast, sirloin tip, or stewing beef, cut into 1.5-2 inch cubes
    *   1 tsp salt
    *   ½ tsp black pepper
    *   2 tbsp all-purpose flour (optional, for thickening, but highly recommended for browning)
    *   2 tbsp olive oil or vegetable oil

*   **Aromatics & Vegetables:**
    *   1 large yellow onion, chopped
    *   4 carrots, peeled and cut into 1-inch pieces
    *   3 celery stalks, cut into 1-inch pieces
    *   8 oz cremini (baby bella) mushrooms, halved or quartered if large
    *   4-6 cloves garlic, minced

*   **Liquids & Flavor:**
    *   2 cups dry red wine (Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Pinot Noir, or a Languedoc-Roussillon blend work well - use something you'd enjoy drinking!)
    *   2-3 cups beef broth (low sodium preferred)
    *   1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes, undrained
    *   2 tbsp tomato paste
    *   2 bay leaves
    *   ½ tsp dried thyme (or 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves)
    *   1 tsp Worcestershire sauce (optional, but adds depth)

*   **For Serving (Optional):**
    *   Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish
    *   Crusty bread, mashed potatoes, polenta, or egg noodles for serving

### Equipment:

*   Large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot (at least 6-quart capacity)

### Instructions:

1.  **Prepare the Beef:**
    *   Pat the beef cubes *very* dry with paper towels. This is crucial for good browning.
    *   In a large bowl, toss the beef with salt, pepper, and flour until evenly coated.

2.  **Brown the Beef:**
    *   Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in your Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering.
    *   Working in batches (do not overcrowd the pot!), add the beef and brown on all sides until a deep, crusty brown forms. This is where a lot of flavor comes from. Remove browned beef to a plate and set aside.

3.  **Sauté Aromatics:**
    *   Reduce heat to medium. Add the chopped onion, carrots, and celery to the pot (add a little more oil if needed). Sauté for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until vegetables soften and onions become translucent.
    *   Add the mushrooms and continue to cook for another 5-7 minutes, until they release their liquid and start to brown.
    *   Stir in the minced garlic and tomato paste. Cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, until fragrant and the tomato paste deepens in color.

4.  **Deglaze with Wine:**
    *   Pour the red wine into the pot. Scrape the bottom of the pot with a wooden spoon, lifting up all the browned bits (fond) – this is packed with flavor and will dissolve into your sauce. Bring to a simmer and let it cook for 5-7 minutes, allowing the alcohol to cook off and the wine to reduce slightly.

5.  **Assemble the Stew:**
    *   Return the browned beef to the pot.
    *   Stir in the beef broth, diced tomatoes (with their juice), bay leaves, dried thyme, and Worcestershire sauce (if using).
    *   Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.

6.  **Simmer (Slow Cook):**
    *   Once simmering, reduce heat to low, cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid, and let it gently simmer for 2 to 2.5 hours, or until the beef is incredibly tender and easily pulls apart with a fork. You can also bake it in the oven at 325°F (160°C) for the same amount of time.
    *   Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. If the stew seems too thick, add a little more beef broth. If it's too thin, remove the lid for the last 30 minutes of cooking.

7.  **Final Touches:**
    *   Once the beef is tender, remove the bay leaves.
    *   Taste and adjust seasoning with more salt and pepper if needed.
    *   If you want a thicker sauce, you can make a quick slurry of 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp cold water, whisk it into the simmering stew, and cook for a few minutes until thickened.

8.  **Serve:**
    *   Ladle the hot beef stew into bowls. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley if desired.
    *   Serve over creamy mashed potatoes, soft polenta, wide egg noodles, or with plenty of crusty bread to soak up all that delicious gravy.

Enjoy your incredibly flavorful and tender beef stew! It's even better the next day as the flavors meld and deepen.
