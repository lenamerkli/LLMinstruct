### **Beef Bourguignon (French Beef Stew in Red Wine)**

#### **Ingredients (Serves 4–6):**

**For the stew:**
- 2 lbs (900g) beef chuck, cut into 2-inch chunks  
- 6 slices thick-cut bacon, chopped  
- 1 large onion, diced  
- 2 carrots, sliced into thick rounds  
- 3 cloves garlic, minced  
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour  
- 2 cups red wine (Burgundy or any dry red)  
- 2 cups beef broth  
- 2 tbsp tomato paste  
- 1 tsp dried thyme (or 3 sprigs fresh)  
- 2 bay leaves  
- 1 lb (450g) pearl onions, peeled  
- 1 lb cremini or button mushrooms, halved  
- Salt & freshly ground black pepper  
- 2 tbsp olive oil (optional, if needed)  
- 1 tbsp butter (for mushrooms)

---

#### **Instructions:**

1. **Prep the beef:**  
   Pat the beef dry with paper towels. Season generously with salt and pepper.

2. **Cook the bacon:**  
   In a large Dutch oven or heavy pot, cook bacon over medium heat until crispy. Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside. Leave the fat in the pot.

3. **Sear the beef:**  
   In batches, sear the beef chunks in the bacon fat until browned on all sides. Don’t crowd the pan. Remove and set aside with the bacon.

4. **Build the base:**  
   In the same pot, sauté diced onion and carrots for 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 minute more. Stir in flour and cook for 2 minutes to remove the raw taste.

5. **Deglaze:**  
   Pour in the red wine, scraping up the browned bits. Add tomato paste, thyme, bay leaves, beef broth, and return the beef and bacon to the pot.

6. **Simmer:**  
   Bring to a simmer, then cover and cook on low heat for 2.5 to 3 hours, until beef is fork-tender. Stir occasionally.

7. **Sauté the veg:**  
   In a skillet, melt butter and sauté pearl onions and mushrooms until golden and tender (about 10 minutes). Season with salt and pepper.

8. **Finish:**  
   Add the sautéed mushrooms and onions to the stew in the last 30 minutes of cooking. Taste and adjust seasoning.

9. **Serve:**  
   Serve hot over **mashed potatoes**, **egg noodles**, or **crusty bread**. Garnish with fresh parsley if desired.

---

### **Pro Tips:**
- Make it a day ahead — it tastes even better the next day.
- Use a good-quality red wine. If you wouldn’t drink it, don’t cook with it.
