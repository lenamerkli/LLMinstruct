### Ingredients:
- 500g (1.1 lbs) ground beef
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon salt (or to taste)
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 200g (1 cup) frozen or canned peas
- 1 cup water (or more if needed)
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)

### Instructions:

1. **Prepare the Ingredients:**
   - Chop the onion, mince the garlic, and grate the ginger.
   - Have the spices, tomato paste, and peas ready.

2. **Cook the Onion:**
   - Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
   - Add the chopped onion and cook until translucent, stirring occasionally to prevent burning.

3. **Add Garlic and Ginger:**
   - Once the onions are soft, add the garlic and ginger. Cook for about 1 minute until fragrant.

4. **Cook the Ground Beef:**
   - Add the ground beef to the skillet, breaking it up with a spoon or spatula. Cook until browned and no pink remains.

5. **Mix in Tomato Paste and Spices:**
   - Stir in the tomato paste until well combined.
   - Add cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala, salt, and pepper. Mix well to coat the meat.

6. **Add Peas and Water:**
   - Add the peas and water to the skillet. Stir everything together.

7. **Simmer:**
   - Cover the skillet and reduce the heat to low. Let it simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the flavors meld together and the mixture reaches your desired consistency. Add more water if needed.

8. **Season and Garnish:**
   - Taste and adjust the seasoning if necessary.
   - Sprinkle with fresh cilantro before serving.

9. **Serve:**
   - Serve hot with naan, rice, or steamed vegetables.

Enjoy your Curry Mince with Peas! It's a hearty and flavorful dish perfect for a comforting meal.
