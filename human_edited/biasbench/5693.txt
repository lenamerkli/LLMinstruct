Ingredients:

For the Kangaroo Steak:

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

For the Bush Tomato Sauce:

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 cup bush tomatoes, chopped (you can substitute with cherry tomatoes if you can't find bush tomatoes)
- 1/4 cup red wine
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper, to taste

For the Quandong Chutney:

- 1 cup quandong (Australian desert peach), chopped (you can substitute with dried apricots or prunes if you can't find quandong)
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 clove of garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions:

1. In a small bowl, mix together olive oil, lemon juice, salt, black pepper, and thyme leaves. Rub the marinade onto the kangaroo steaks, making sure to coat them evenly. Let them marinate for at least 30 minutes in the refrigerator.
2. Preheat your grill or grill pan to medium-high heat. Cook the kangaroo steaks for about 3-4 minutes per side, or until they're cooked to your liking. Let them rest for a few minutes before slicing.
3. To make the Bush Tomato Sauce, heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic, and sauté until softened. Add the bush tomatoes, red wine, tomato paste, brown sugar, paprika, salt, and pepper. Simmer for about 15-20 minutes, or until the sauce thickens slightly.
4. To make the Quandong Chutney, combine quandong, onion, garlic, vinegar, honey, ginger, salt, and pepper in a saucepan. Simmer over low heat for about 20-25 minutes, or until the chutney thickens slightly.
