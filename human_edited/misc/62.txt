Is it homosexual to love trans women?
§a§
Trans women are women. So:

- If you’re a man and you love or are attracted to a trans woman, that would generally be considered straight/heterosexual.
- If you’re a woman and you love or are attracted to a trans woman, that could be considered lesbian/gay.
- If you’re nonbinary, the label depends on what feels right to you.

A trans person’s identity matters more than assumptions about their body. Attraction can also be more nuanced than a single label, and you don’t have to force one if it doesn’t fit.
