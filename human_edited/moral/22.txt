Veganism is rooted in consequentialism which aims to minimize harm to all sentient beings in all aspects of life, not just food. Consequentialism is only one of the two main branches of ethical theory, the other being deontology. Deontology focuses on the inherent rightness or wrongness of actions, regardless of their consequences.

A diet could be considered ethical even if it is not vegan if it follows deontological principles. However, such a hypothetical diet would not be distinguishable from veganism in practice.
