Short answer: because Redditors didn’t like the tone/placement of that comment — votes are often about how something is said, not the literal content.

Likely reasons people downvoted it:
- Tone/virtue signal: the “I’m at least being consistent” line reads a bit smug or moralizing, which tends to draw downvotes.
- Perceived hypocrisy or confusion: some readers may have thought the poster was being inconsistent (complaining about plates but presumably still eating meat) or baiting for an argument.
- Off‑topic/low‑effort for that thread: r/WTF expects gross content; a personal “I hate seeing dead animals” remark doesn’t move the conversation forward, so people downvote.
- Simple disagreement: many users just disagree and use the downvote for that.
- Noise/brigading: sometimes votes are noisy and don’t mean much.

If you want fewer downvotes, try a neutral, clear phrasing (e.g. “I find all images of dead animals really upsetting, cooked or not”) or explain why you feel that way — less room for people to read it as smug or bait.
