What is the maximal file size I can upload to my private cloud?
§a§
Based on your Nginx configuration, the maximum file size you can upload to your private cloud is **1500 MB (1.5 GB)**.

This is set by the `client_max_body_size 1500M;` directive. If you try to upload a file larger than 1500 MB, Nginx will return a `413 (Request Entity Too Large)` error.

A few things to keep in mind:

1. **The 1500 MB limit is a hard ceiling in Nginx**, but other components in your stack may impose smaller limits. For example, if your backend application (e.g., Nextcloud) at `192.168.1.117:10084` has its own upload size limit, that will take effect instead. Check your application settings (e.g., `php_value upload_max_filesize` and `post_max_size` for PHP-based apps, or the equivalent for your specific cloud software).

2. **The "M" suffix is case-sensitive in some contexts** — in Nginx, `M` and `m` both work, but always verify with a test upload.

3. **For very large uploads**, you may also want to consider increasing Nginx timeouts, since large files take longer to transfer:
   ```nginx
   proxy_read_timeout 600;
   proxy_send_timeout 600;
   ```

If you need to upload files larger than 1.5 GB, just increase the `client_max_body_size` value to whatever you need.
