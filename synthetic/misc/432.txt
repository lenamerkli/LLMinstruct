These are different ways to attach something to your hair:

- **Head band x1 (CHF 7.15)**
  A band you wear over the top of your head, like a normal hairband. “x1” = one piece. “CHF” = Swiss francs (money).

- **Alligator clips (CHF 4.21)**
  Small metal hair clips with teeth, like an alligator’s mouth. You press to open and they snap shut.

- **Flat prong clips (CHF 4.21)**
  Flat metal clips with two or more straight “prongs.” Hairdressers often use them to hold sections of hair.

- **Thin black bobble (CHF 4.21)**
  A thin black elastic hair tie for making a ponytail. “Bobble” = UK word for a hair tie.

- **Thick black bobble (CHF 4.21)**
  A thicker, stronger black hair tie. Better for a lot of hair or a bigger ponytail.
