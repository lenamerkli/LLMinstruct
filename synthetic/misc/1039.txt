This is a radical animal rights/vegan meme (as indicated by the watermark `www.WatchDominion.org`, referencing the provocative documentary *Dominion*).

Here is a breakdown of what the meme means and the argument it is trying to make:

### 1. The Core Argument
The meme reflects a perspective held by some abolitionist vegans who argue that **vegetarians are morally complicit in animal abuse—specifically, sexual violence.**

From this ideological standpoint:
* **The Dairy and Egg Industries:** Vegetarians do not eat meat, but they often consume dairy (milk, cheese) and eggs. These industries rely entirely on the forced artificial insemination of female animals (such as cows and pigs), which is rape.
* **The Comparison:** Because vegetarians financially support the dairy and egg industries, radical vegans argue that vegetarians are participating in and funding the sexual violation of animals.

### 2. The Text Labels
* **"Vegetarians" (Left):** Represents people who avoid meat for ethical, health, or environmental reasons, but still consume animal byproducts.
* **"Other rapists" (Right):** Categorizes vegetarians alongside other perpetrators of sexual violence (in this context, implying that consuming dairy/eggs makes one a participant in animal rape, grouping them with actual rapists or meat-eaters viewed through the same lens).

### 3. The Meme Format and Intent
The meme uses a classic *Star Trek: Voyager* template where two characters awkwardly admit to being "friends" when asked.

In the context of vegan activism, this is used as a **shock tactic**. Mainstream society views vegetarianism as a morally superior or "harmless" dietary choice. Vegan activists often target vegetarians specifically to dismantle that feeling of moral superiority, arguing that as long as someone consumes dairy or eggs, they are no different from meat-eaters or perpetrators of animal exploitation.
