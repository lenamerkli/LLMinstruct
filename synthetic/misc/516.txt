The comic is about a trans person testing whether their voice “passes” (is perceived as their gender) while using voice chat.

At first they nervously ask “um… h‑hello? can you hear me?” Then, from the other side, someone reacts in an over-the-top sexual way: “I WILL IMPREGNATE YOU WITH MY TRIPLETS!”

That reaction implies the speaker was perceived as a woman with an attractive-sounding voice. After a pause, the main character happily concludes “my voice passes.”

So the humor comes from the absurd, extreme reaction being used as confirmation that their voice successfully sounds feminine.
