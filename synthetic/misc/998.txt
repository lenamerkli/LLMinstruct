**TADC = *The Amazing Digital Circus***, an animated web series created by **Gooseworx** and produced by Glitch Productions (the Australian studio behind *Murder Drones*). It premiered in 2023 and became a huge hit on YouTube, about a girl named Pomni who gets trapped in a surreal digital circus run by an AI ringmaster named Caine.

In the context of this greentext, the TADC stickers are a punchline/marker: the fandom (and Gooseworx's own online presence) overlaps heavily with very-online queer/trans internet spaces. So the woman having those stickers signals she's deep enough in that subculture to immediately recognize what the poster actually is — hence her using the term **"boymoder,"** which is trans-internet slang for a trans woman who is still presenting/passing as male (deliberately "moding" as a boy). That instantly blows the poster's cover, which they'd been trying to maintain by claiming he/him pronouns.

The final line — "fuck you gooseworx" — is the poster jokingly blaming the show's creator for making their subculture's terminology mainstream enough that a random classmate could clock them.
