§a§
An LLM's knowledge cutoff is the date after which its training data ends, meaning it has no built-in awareness of events, facts, or developments occurring afterward. As a consequence, it may give outdated answers, miss recent events, or confidently state information that has since changed—unless supplemented by tools like web search.
