Is it true that a court ordered the deletion of footage in a case about german gas chambers for pigs?
§a§
Yes, it is true. In June 2026, a German appellate court ordered two animal rights activists to delete undercover footage they had secretly recorded inside the CO₂ gas chambers of a German slaughterhouse (Brand Qualitätsfleisch).

The activists had installed hidden cameras to capture the pigs' reactions to the carbon dioxide gas, which is a standard stunning method used in the meat industry. The slaughterhouse sued the activists for trespassing, secret filming, and publication of the footage. The court ruled in favor of the slaughterhouse, ordering the activists to delete all images and footage they had posted online and to pay damages estimated to be around €100,000.

While the court reportedly acknowledged that the footage was authentic and in the public interest, it still sided with the company's privacy and business interests over the activists' unauthorized entry and recording. The activists have indicated plans to appeal the decision to Germany's highest court.
