What is the max data transfer speed for an iPhone SE 3rd gen through its physical port?
§a§
The iPhone SE (3rd generation) uses a **Lightning port**, and its maximum data transfer speed is **USB 2.0**, which has a theoretical maximum of **480 Mbps (megabits per second)**.

The iPhone SE 3rd Generation (2022) has a Lightning port at the bottom of the phone, used for both charging and transferring data to other devices.

Most Lightning devices only support USB 2.0, with only a small number of older iPad Pro models supporting USB 3.0 speeds. This means the iPhone SE 3rd gen is capped at USB 2.0's 480 Mbps theoretical maximum — and typical real-world transfer speeds over USB 2.0 tend to be around 30–40 MB/s due to overhead.

This is the bottleneck caused by Lightning's USB-2-level connection, which was only addressed in later iPhones that switched to USB-C.
