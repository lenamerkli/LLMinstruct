How many chickens in Switzerland see the sun more than once?
§a§
If you look at how many *individual* chickens in Switzerland actually get outdoors more than once in their lives, the best current estimate is on the order of **10 million birds a year**, out of roughly **90 million chickens raised per year** – so **about 1 in 9–10**.

Very roughly, here’s how that breaks down:

- **Meat chickens (broilers)**
  - Switzerland slaughtered about **89 million poultry for meat** in 2023; almost all of these are chickens.
  - Recent Swiss reporting says only about **8 % of broilers have outdoor access**, the other **92 % are kept entirely indoors**.
  - That implies on the order of **7 million meat chickens per year** ever go outside; the other ~80+ million never do.

- **Egg‑laying hens**
  - Swiss hens laid about **1.124 billion eggs in 2024**.
  - A typical Swiss laying hen produces ~**320 eggs per year**, so that’s roughly **3.5 million hens in production**.
  - According to recent Swiss coverage of bird‑flu measures, around **95 % of these hens normally lay eggs in systems with outdoor access** (free‑range or similar).
  - So, in a normal (non‑lockdown) year, **about 3–3.5 million hens** would be outside regularly.

Add those together: roughly **7 million broilers + ~3–3.5 million laying hens ≈ 10 million chickens** with at least some real outdoor life each year, versus something like **80–85 million** that spend their entire (very short) lives inside barns or covered “winter gardens.”

A few important caveats:

- These numbers are **approximations**, stitched together from slaughter statistics, egg output, and official/press estimates of what share of birds are in outdoor systems.
- “Have outdoor access” doesn’t mean every bird actually *uses* the pasture; studies show some hens rarely go out even when doors are open, while many do.
- Around **3 million male chicks** in Switzerland are still killed on their first day of life, so they never see the sun at all.
- In recent winters, bird‑flu rules have temporarily forced even “free‑range” flocks to stay in roofed, netted areas, which reduces actual time in open sun.

So depending on how strictly you define “see the sun,” the *upper bound* is roughly 10 million Swiss chickens per year with genuine outdoor life, and the *lower bound* is smaller – but in any case, it’s clearly a minority of all the chickens raised.
