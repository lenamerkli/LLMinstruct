This meme is a humorous and somewhat emotional reference to the experience of transgender people undergoing hormone replacement therapy (HRT).

Here is a breakdown of the meaning:

**1. The Context (Top Text)**
*   **"HRT":** This stands for Hormone Replacement Therapy. Transgender people take these hormones (estrogen or testosterone) to align their physical bodies with their gender identity.
*   **"Stretch mark":** Stretch marks often appear when the body grows or changes shape rapidly. For someone on HRT, this could mean breast growth, widening hips, or muscle growth.
*   **The implication:** Seeing a stretch mark is actually a **positive sign** for many trans people. It is physical proof that the hormones are working and that their body is finally changing in the way they want it to.

**2. The Reaction (Bottom Image)**
*   **The Image:** This is a popular meme template featuring the character Wario from the *Mario* franchise. He looks downcast and dramatic against a cloudy sky.
*   **The Text:** "i've won...... but at what cost?"
*   **The Meaning:** This phrase is usually used when someone achieves a goal, but the victory feels bittersweet or came with a heavy price.

**3. Putting it together**
The meme is expressing a mix of pride and exhaustion.
*   **The "Win":** The person is happy because the stretch mark proves they have been on hormones long enough to see real, permanent physical changes. They are "winning" at transitioning.
*   **The "Cost":** This is likely a joke about the physical discomfort of stretch marks (which can itch or hurt) or, more deeply, a reference to the long, difficult, and expensive journey of transition. It acknowledges that while the result is good, the process took a long time ("long enough") and required a lot of effort.

In short, it’s a way of saying: **"My body is finally changing the way I want it to (Yay!), but wow, it took a long time and my skin is literally tearing to get here (Ouch/Phew)."**

Where are these stretch marks on the body?
§a§
The location of the stretch marks depends on which type of Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) the person is taking, as the hormones cause different parts of the body to grow or change shape.

Here is where they typically appear for each type of transition:

**1. For Feminizing HRT (Estrogen)**
This is the most common context for this specific meme. Estrogen causes breast growth and fat redistribution.
*   **Breasts:** This is the most common place. Just like during puberty for cisgender girls, rapid breast growth often causes stretch marks on the breasts or around the areola.
*   **Hips, Thighs, and Buttocks:** Estrogen causes fat to move to these areas, widening the hips. Rapid expansion here can cause stretch marks.

**2. For Masculinizing HRT (Testosterone)**
Testosterone causes muscle growth and changes in fat distribution.
*   **Shoulders and Back:** As the shoulders broaden and back muscles grow, stretch marks can appear there.
*   **Arms:** Rapid muscle growth in the biceps and triceps can cause the skin to stretch.
*   **Stomach:** Testosterone often increases appetite and leads to muscle/weight gain, which can cause stretch marks on the abdomen.

**Why does this happen?**
HRT essentially triggers a "second puberty." During puberty, the body grows very quickly. When the skin stretches faster than it can produce new collagen, the dermis tears slightly, resulting in stretch marks. In the context of the meme, seeing them is a sign that the body is finally changing to match the person's identity.
