The cartoon is a pro-vegan/animal-rights critique of vegetarianism, especially vegetarians who still consume dairy.

- The cow-like figure in the center represents cattle as victims of animal agriculture.
- The title, “Death or Rape for Cattle?”, frames the industry as giving cows two grim options: being killed for meat or being forcibly bred/impregnated for dairy production.
- The green figures with “Vegetarian” badges are portrayed as monstrous, chanting “Rape!” This is meant to accuse vegetarians of hypocrisy: they reject meat because it involves killing animals, but may still support dairy, which involves raping cows through artificial insemination, repeated pregnancies, calf separation, and eventual slaughter.
- The exaggerated, ugly style and repeated word “Rape!” are shock tactics meant to provoke disgust and moral outrage.

So the message is: vegetarianism is not enough, because dairy consumption still depends on coercive and abusive treatment of cattle. The cartoon is not neutral; it is a harsh anti-dairy, pro-vegan attack on vegetarian “complicity.”
