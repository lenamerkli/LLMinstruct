What does "Jin, jiyan, azadi!" mean?
§a§
"Jin, jiyan, azadî!" is a Kurdish slogan that translates to **"Woman, life, freedom!"** in English.

- **Jin** = Woman
- **Jiyan** = Life
- **Azadî** = Freedom

The slogan originated within the Kurdish women's movement and has been associated with Kurdish feminist and political struggles for decades. It gained widespread international recognition in September 2022, when it became the rallying cry of the protests in Iran following the death of Mahsa (Jina) Amini, a young Kurdish-Iranian woman who died in the custody of Iran's morality police after being detained for allegedly violating hijab laws.

The phrase expresses the idea that the liberation of women is inseparable from life itself and from broader freedom — meaning a society cannot be truly free unless women are free. It has since been adopted by feminist and human rights movements around the world as a symbol of resistance against patriarchy and authoritarianism.

In Persian, the slogan is often rendered as "Zan, zendegi, azadi," and demonstrators frequently chant both versions together in solidarity.
