This post is highlighting a **double standard in media framing and public discourse**—the tendency to treat violence by white conservatives as individual "lone wolf" acts while treating identical violence by minorities, immigrants, or Muslims as representative of their entire culture or religion.

The "usual questions" aren't being asked because the perpetrator occupies a position of **cultural "in-group" status** in mainstream American media. Here is why that asymmetry exists:

### 1. **Individual vs. Collective Blame**
- **When the perpetrator is a minority:** The media and public often demand that entire communities answer for the crime. Violence becomes a referendum on "Black culture," "Islamic values," or immigrant assimilation.
- **When the perpetrator is white/MAGA:** The narrative immediately individualizes the act. It becomes about "mental illness," "economic anxiety," or a "troubled individual"—never about whiteness, conservatism, or MAGA culture as inherently violent.

### 2. **"Radicalization" is Only for "Them"**
Questions like *"Where was he radicalized?"* or *"What are they teaching in their churches?"* are reserved for out-groups. When white supremacists or extreme MAGA supporters commit violence, media outlets rarely scrutinize Fox News, OANN, or certain evangelical churches as "radicalizing" institutions—even when they promote election denial, dehumanization of political opponents, or violent rhetoric.

### 3. **The "Honor Killing" Hypocrisy**
The question *"Is this what they call 'honor killing?'"* exposes how gender-based violence gets racialized. When Muslim men kill female relatives, it's called an "honor killing" and attributed to Islamic patriarchy. When white Christian men kill female relatives over "disrespect" or disobedience (which happens at higher rates in the U.S.), it's domestic violence or a family tragedy—never a "Biblical honor killing" or evidence of "white Christian patriarchy," despite the Bible also containing verses about female submission.

### 4. **Assimilation and Loyalty Tests**
Questions about assimilation (*"Why won't they adopt our values?"*) are only asked of immigrants. The implication is that white Americans don't need to "assimilate" to American values because they are the default. When white conservatives reject democratic norms or commit violence over political disagreement, they aren't asked why they refuse to assimilate to pluralistic, democratic values.

### 5. **Immigration Status Scrutiny**
Asking *"Was he here legally from Europe?"* reverses the xenophobic tendency to question the citizenship of any perpetrator who isn't white. It highlights how whiteness functions as automatic proof of "belonging," while brownness or foreign accent triggers suspicion of illegitimacy.

---

**The Bottom Line:** These questions aren't being asked because doing so would require treating whiteness and Trump support as **identities subject to the same pathology-based scrutiny** applied to minority groups. The post uses satire to reveal that what gets labeled "cultural violence" for minorities is labeled "personal tragedy" or "political extremism" (individualized) for white conservatives—exposing whose violence gets contextualized and whose gets essentialized.
