Berlin (dpa) – Following media reports about the federal government's plans to introduce a sugar tax, resistance is growing in the industries most affected: The Federal Association of Homeopaths (BdH) sharply criticized a possible levy and warned of massive price increases.

"The sugar tax decided by the government is a clear and targeted attack on all manufacturers of homeopathic preparations," the association writes in a statement. "The Ministry of Health knows full well that globules are practically 100 percent sugar."

Experts therefore assume that the planned sugar tax will hit the homeopathy industry hard.

Passing on the costs is considered risky by the association: "If we become even more expensive, there is a danger that our patients will realize that we are ripping them... uh... perhaps they will then switch to real... uh... conventional medical preparations," said spokesperson Cornelius von Bredow.

This is not the first attack on homeopathy. Just a few years ago, the broadcast of advertising for sweets during children's television was banned. Since then, manufacturers have been unable to advertise their homeopathic preparations there.
