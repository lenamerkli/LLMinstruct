# Solving the Equation

I need to simplify the equation:

$$2 \cdot 4 \cdot s + 1 \cdot (-5) \cdot t + (-2) \cdot 6 = 0$$

## Step-by-Step Simplification

Evaluate each term:

$$8s - 5t - 12 = 0$$

## Solving for one variable

$$\boxed{8s - 5t = 12}$$

This is a linear equation in two unknowns. If solving for **s** in terms of **t**:

$$s = \frac{12 + 5t}{8}$$

Or solving for **t** in terms of **s**:

$$t = \frac{8s - 12}{5}$$
