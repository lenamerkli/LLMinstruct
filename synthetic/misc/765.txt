Simplify the equation first:

2·4·s = 8s
1·(-5)·t = -5t
(-2)·6 = -12

So the equation becomes:

8s - 5t - 12 = 0

Add 12 to both sides:

8s - 5t = 12

Now solve for either variable:

For s:
s = (12 + 5t) / 8

For t:
t = (8s - 12) / 5

Either form is correct depending on what you need 👍
