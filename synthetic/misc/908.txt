§a§
During **Sommerzeit** (CEST, UTC+2), you add 2 hours to the UTC peak times. So the peak hours in Germany would be:

- **3:00 AM – 6:00 AM** (CEST)
- **8:00 AM – 12:00 noon** (CEST)

For reference, during **Winterzeit** (CET, UTC+1), it would be 2:00–5:00 AM and 7:00–11:00 AM.
