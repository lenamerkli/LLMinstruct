What happens when not all required ETS2 DLCs are installed when trying to use Promods?
§a§
Here's a detailed breakdown of what happens when you try to use ProMods in ETS2 without all the required DLCs installed:

---

### 🔴 The Mod Shows as Incompatible in Mod Manager

When you load ETS2 and go into the Mod Manager, all ProMods files (Map, Media, Models, DLC Support, Definitions, Assets) will be marked as **incompatible**. Each of them will display the message: *"Dependencies: Some DLCs which are required by this mod are missing. Make sure you have all of them installed and activated."*

---

### 🚫 You Cannot Load the Mod At All

You simply **can't even load the mod**. There is a DLC dependency in the `manifest.sii` file from the mod files that marks the files as incompatible in the Mod Manager when you don't have the required DLCs or game version.

---

### 🗺️ Why Are All DLCs Required?

ProMods cannot run without the required DLCs because they use **models and assets from the DLCs all over the map**. For instance, without the France DLC they could not do Spain, and without the Italy DLC they could not do Greece.

ProMods uses game assets from **all** of the map DLCs, which is why each one is required as new versions are released.

---

### 📋 Required DLCs (for modern ProMods versions)

You must make sure you have all of the following installed and activated: `dlc_east`, `dlc_north`, `dlc_fr`, `dlc_it`, `dlc_balt`, `dlc_balkan_e`, `dlc_balkan_w`, `dlc_iberia`.

---

### 🕹️ Is There Any Workaround?

If you want to play ProMods without all DLCs, you would have to **downgrade ETS2 to version 1.18** and play **ProMods 1.91**, but this is not recommended as you would miss many new ETS2 and ProMods features and would likely encounter many bugs.

Additionally, downgrading to version 1.18 via Steam is no longer easily possible, as the lowest version accessible through Steam is ETS2 **1.26**.

---

In short, missing even one required map DLC will prevent ProMods from loading entirely, and there is no supported way around it for modern versions of the mod.
