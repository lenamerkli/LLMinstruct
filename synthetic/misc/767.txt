2 \cdot 4 \cdot s + 1 \cdot (-5) \cdot s + (-2) \cdot 6 \cdot s = 0
§a§
The given equation is:

\[
2 \cdot 4 \cdot s + 1 \cdot (-5) \cdot s + (-2) \cdot 6 \cdot s = 0
\]

First, compute each product:

- \(2 \cdot 4 = 8\), so the first term is \(8s\).
- \(1 \cdot (-5) = -5\), so the second term is \(-5s\).
- \((-2) \cdot 6 = -12\), so the third term is \(-12s\).

Now combine like terms:

\[
8s - 5s - 12s = (8 - 5 - 12)s = (3 - 12)s = -9s
\]

Thus the equation simplifies to:

\[
-9s = 0
\]

Dividing both sides by \(-9\) gives:

\[
s = 0
\]

Therefore, the solution is \(s = 0\).
