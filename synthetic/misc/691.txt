The standard memory offering for the Tesla V100 began with 16GB, but was doubled to a 32GB configuration, and Tesla V100 maintains the same SXM2 board form factor as Tesla P100, with the main differences being the use of the GV100 GPU instead of GP100.

So to answer your question: **No, this is not always the case.** The V100 is available in:
- **16GB version**: SXM2 form factor
- **32GB version**: Both SXM2 and SXM3 form factors
