Likely a combo of Reddit dynamics and tone:

- People often use downvotes as “I disagree.” In a thread where most eat meat and didn’t mind the carcass, saying you hate seeing any dead animal (including on a plate) is a minority take.
- The phrasing (“Ugh… I’m at least being consistent”) reads a bit moralizing/condescending, which triggers downvotes.
- It doesn’t add much to the discussion beyond a personal dislike, which many see as low‑effort/off‑topic for r/WTF.
- It was a direct reply to a popular comment; children that contradict an upvoted parent often get dog‑piled.

So it’s less about the logic and more about audience, tone, and how voting is used on Reddit.
