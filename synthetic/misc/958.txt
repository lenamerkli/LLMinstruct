What are the recommended sampling parameters for gemma-4-E4B-it-qat?
§a§
Here are the recommended sampling parameters for **gemma-4-E4B-it-qat**:

The recommended sampling defaults for Gemma 4 QAT (applicable to all runtimes) are:

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Temperature** | `1.0` |
| **top_p** | `0.95` |
| **top_k** | `64` |


These are Google's recommended values for the QAT checkpoints. The 1.0 temperature is intentional — Gemma 4 is tuned for it.

### Additional Notes

- Compared to Gemma 3, the models use standard `system`, `assistant`, and `user` roles.
- The E4B variant supports context windows of up to 128K tokens.
- The model is compatible with llama.cpp, LM Studio, Jan, MLC LLM, and mobile GGUF runtimes.
- When using llama.cpp, use the `--jinja` flag, and to disable thinking, pass `--chat-template-kwargs '{"enable_thinking":false}'`.
