You know what absolutely breaks my heart about this species?

The fact that if the consumption of animal products was banned tomorrow, MORE people would rise up and revolt against this ethical, moral change to the law than those who marched for any of the actual justice movements in history combined.

Hundreds of millions of people would actually take to the streets to march FOR animal slavery—more than any who have ever marched AGAINST it, and more than any who have ever marched against the oppression of women, black people, gays, and so on.

The anger, intensity and passion shown by those who "want their bacon back" would outdo any anger, intensity and passion they'd shown for any issue regarding standing up AGAINST oppression in their entire lives.

How can any vegan NOT be a misanthrope when presented with that harsh truth. What a pathetic fucking virus we are.
