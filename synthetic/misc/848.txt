## F1 TV Timing Graphics Icons

This is a Formula 1 meme based on the on-screen timing tower icons:

**🟣 Purple Stopwatch** — Indicates the driver currently holds the **fastest lap** of the race. Purple is universally used in F1 graphics to denote the fastest time (fastest sectors and laps are shown in purple).

**🔴 Red Exclamation Mark (!)** — Indicates the driver has been given a **time penalty** (e.g., 5 or 10 seconds) by the stewards.

**🟡/🟢 Yellow Exclamation Mark (!)** — Indicates the driver is **under investigation** by the stewards or has received a **warning** (such as for track limits violations).

## The Joke

The meme uses the famous Simpsons scene of Homer at Moe's Tavern surrounded by everyone else. Here:

- **Antonelli** (Homer) is the only driver with the **purple fastest lap icon** — clean and quick
- **Everyone else** at the bar has penalty/investigation icons over their faces

It references a chaotic race (the timing screen shows a Safety Car period) where seemingly the entire field was being penalized or investigated, while Antonelli alone stood out positively with the fastest lap — making him the odd one out in the best way possible.
