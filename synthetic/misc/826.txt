Is it gay to love trans men?
§a§
Sexual orientation is generally defined by the **gender** a person is attracted to, not by the sex they were assigned at birth or their anatomy. Since **trans men are men**, attraction to them is attraction to men. Whether that attraction is considered "gay" depends on your own gender and your overall pattern of attraction:

- **If you're a man** and you're attracted to trans men (and other men), that attraction aligns with what's typically called gay or queer.
- **If you're a woman** and you're attracted to trans men, that's attraction to men, which is typically considered straight (heterosexual).
- **If you're non-binary, queer, or fluid**, your label might be bi, pan, queer, or something else that reflects your specific attractions.

It's also worth noting that:
- Labels are personal. Some people use "gay," "straight," "bi," "pan," or "queer" for different reasons, and that's okay.
- What matters most is mutual respect, consent, and recognizing trans men as men.
- Your attraction doesn't need to be "categorized" to be valid. Love and attraction are human experiences that don't always fit neatly into boxes.

If you're exploring your own orientation or navigating a relationship with a trans man, many find it helpful to focus on what feels authentic to you rather than worrying about whether a label "fits" according to external expectations.
