Can I put a SXM3 GPU onto a SXM2 board?
§a§
**No, you cannot put an SXM3 GPU onto an SXM2 board**. Here's why:

## Key Incompatibilities:

1. **Different Connectors**: The SXM3 interface features a somewhat different mezzanine connector (400pin Amphenol MEG-Array used in SXM2 was swapped to a similar one that appears to be more robust).

2. **Power Delivery Differences**: SXM3 has an updated power delivery architecture, based on 48V power input (instead of typical 12V) used in SXM2 systems.

3. **Physical Incompatibility**: While some sources mention attempts to adapt SXM3 GPUs to other boards, an adapter seller claimed it could accept SXM3 boards by removing a GPU guide pin located in a different position compared to SXM4, but the update states "this doesn't work".

## Socket Generation Compatibility:

Current socket generations are SXM for Pascal based GPUs, SXM2 and SXM3 for Volta based GPUs, SXM4 for Ampere based GPUs, and SXM5 for Hopper based GPUs.

While both SXM2 and SXM3 were used for Volta GPUs (like the V100), they have different physical and electrical specifications that prevent cross-compatibility. The proprietary nature of these sockets means that the specs for the various SXM2, SXM3, SXM4, SXM5 connectors seem to be trade secret, or proprietary, making unofficial adaptations extremely difficult.

If you need to use an SXM3 GPU, you'll need an SXM3-compatible board or system, such as those found in specific server platforms like the Supermicro SYS-9029GP-TNVRT or Inspur NF5488M5.
